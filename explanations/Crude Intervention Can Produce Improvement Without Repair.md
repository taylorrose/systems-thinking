# Crude Intervention Can Produce Improvement Without Repair

Drenching the human brain with drugs or jolting it with electricity may cause some temporary improvement in some areas of functioning. Because a global disturbance can shift a system’s output in a welcome direction, an intervention can register as effective without having corrected anything.
