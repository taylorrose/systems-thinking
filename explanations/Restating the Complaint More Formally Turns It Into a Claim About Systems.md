# Restating the Complaint More Formally Turns It Into a Claim About Systems

The everyday complaint that things are not working well, its fuller form that things never did work well, the general statement that systems work poorly or not at all, and the technical statement about the efficiency of complicated systems are one observation given in rising degrees of formality. What each restatement changes is the subject and its precision: the vague "things" becomes systems, and systems become measurable ones.
