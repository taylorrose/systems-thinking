# Capabilities a System Hands Out Offer Clues to Its Possible Goals

Humans can only guess at the apparent goals of a large system. What it puts into people's hands offers one guide to more educated guessing: distributing a weapon is evidence that some kind of violence may lie in the direction of the system, without turning that inference into certain knowledge of a “true goal.”
