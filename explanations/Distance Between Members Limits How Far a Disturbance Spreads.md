# Distance Between Members Limits How Far a Disturbance Spreads

Closeness is what carries a signal from one member of a group to all the others, so a group in which everyone is always reachable is a group in which any one member’s reaction becomes everyone’s. Some ability to get away — to be temporarily out of range of the others’ signals — is what keeps a disturbance local. Separation of this kind is a structural property of the group rather than a lapse in its togetherness.
