# A Failure That Does Not Recur on Demand Is Absent Whenever It Is Examined

A failure that does not recur on demand is absent whenever it is examined; for example, [[examples/A Toaster That Only Sometimes Burns the Toast Defeats Its Repairman|a toaster that only sometimes burns the toast defeats its repairman]]. Consequently, [[effects/An Intermittent Failure Can Be Ruled Not to Exist|an intermittent failure can be ruled not to exist]].
