# The Coefficient of Fiction Measures How Much Reality Fails to Reach the Control Unit

The fading of outside reality can be stated as a fraction. Ro is the amount of reality that fails to reach the control unit, and Rs is the total amount of reality presented to the system; their ratio, Ro/Rs, is named the Coefficient of Fiction.

The coefficient runs from zero, where the system is fully aware of outside reality, to unity, where no reality gets through at all.
