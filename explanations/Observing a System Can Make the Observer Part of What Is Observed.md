# Observing a System Can Make the Observer Part of What Is Observed

An observer who looks into a system may find the system looking back: the peeper peeps and sees the thing it is peeping at peeping at the peeper. The act of observing has then entered the behavior being observed.

Stated that way, the self-referential quality of the interaction becomes apparent: observer and observed are no longer two separate things but one loop in which each is watching the other watch.
