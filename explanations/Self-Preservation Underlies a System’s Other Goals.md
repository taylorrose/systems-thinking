# Self-Preservation Underlies a System’s Other Goals

Self-preservation appears prior to a system’s other goals; consequently, [[effects/Attempts to Dismantle an Operating System Meet Resistance|attempts to dismantle an operating system meet resistance]], and even where the system seems bent on self-destruction, [[effects/The Ghost of the Old System Haunts the New|the ghost of the old system haunts the new]].
