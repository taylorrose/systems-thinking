# Rare Remedies Change Who Can Hold an Office or What the Office Offers

Successful attempts to control parasitic office-holding are rare. The examples change the structure of the role, eligibility for it, or the attractions it offers; for example, [[examples/Egypt, China, and Britain Changed the Structure, Eligibility, or Appeal of High Office|Egypt, China, and Britain changed the structure, eligibility, or appeal of high office]].
