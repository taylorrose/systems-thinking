# Unquestioned Assumptions Turn Default Decisions Into Destiny

When entry into a system is treated as unavoidable, the assumptions underneath that appearance are never presented as choices. The decision then occurs by default, and its accumulated consequences appear as destiny even though they began in premises no one stopped to question.
