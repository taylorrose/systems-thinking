# Most Systems Do Not Advertise Their Uses the Way a Shovel Does

A shovel shows what it is for: its form names its task and its handle. Most of the systems encountered in daily life are more like the Civil Service, whose shape says nothing about which tasks it performs well or where it should be taken hold of.
