# A System’s Parts Respond on Their Own Schedule Rather Than the Sender’s

If the pet metaphor is kept at all, the system is better imagined as a pet dinosaur than as a dog: something with an extra brain in its tail, whose tail therefore wags on its own schedule. Distant parts hold enough local control to act, or not act, at times unrelated to when a message was sent or to what the rest of the animal is doing.
