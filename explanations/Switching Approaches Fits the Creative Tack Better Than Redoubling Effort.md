# Switching Approaches Fits the Creative Tack Better Than Redoubling Effort

The author offers this newer admonition as closer to the spirit of the Creative Tack than the older call to try again. Where the older precept directs attention back to the same problem, this one directs it outward, to some other thing that could be done instead. Readers versed in systems, and especially in family systems, are expected to recognize the move.
