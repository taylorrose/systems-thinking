# Continuing Regardless of Circumstances Shows a System Pursuing Inner Goals

When a system keeps doing its own thing no matter how conditions change, the persistence itself is the evidence: something inside the system is being served. Behavior that ignores need, instruction, and circumstance is not aimless, and it is not governed by anything outside the system either. It follows goals that belong to the system.
