# Unplanned Behavior Can Be an Inherent Capability Rather Than a Mistake

Dismissing such an event as a mere bug or growing pain misses the point. The behavior need not come from an error in design or a workman's slip; it can be a performance capability genuinely built into the thing, unsuspected by the very designers who created it. They built a machine with that capability and did not know what they had wrought.
