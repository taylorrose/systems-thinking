# Encroachment Means Making Someone Else Do the System’s Work

Systems do not only grow; they also encroach. Encroachment is the transfer of work the system exists to perform onto someone outside it, usually the very people it serves. The function is not abandoned and the system is not reduced—the labor is simply relocated, often while being described as a convenience or a saving for the person now doing it.
