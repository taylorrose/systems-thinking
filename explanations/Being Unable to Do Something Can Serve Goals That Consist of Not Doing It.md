# Being Unable to Do Something Can Serve Goals That Consist of Not Doing It

Creative incompetence, as expounded by Dr. L. J. Peter, treats incapacity as an instrument rather than a defect. Where a goal consists of *not doing something*, lacking the means to do it can be a powerful method of reaching that goal, because a request cannot be pressed on someone who plainly could not carry it out. The technique is closely linked with declining involvement in a system at the outset.
