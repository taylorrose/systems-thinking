# Fuzzy Systems Do Not Justify Fuzzy Thinking

Much of the world consists of systems whose boundaries, goals, and behavior are genuinely indistinct. That indistinctness describes the subject matter, not the standard for thinking about it. Vague concepts do not become adequate because their object is vague; fuzzy thinking is not a way of coping with such a system, let alone of prevailing over it.
