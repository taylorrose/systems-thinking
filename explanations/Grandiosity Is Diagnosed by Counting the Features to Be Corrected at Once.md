# Grandiosity Is Diagnosed by Counting the Features to Be Corrected at Once

The diagnosis is made on a purely quantitative basis: how many features of the present system, and at what level, are to be corrected at once. Nothing about the merit, the motive, or the wording of the plan enters into it.
