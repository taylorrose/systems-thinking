# What a System Will Keep Doing Can Be Known Even When What It Is Doing Cannot Be Judged

Whether a large system is functioning, succeeding, or doing anything at all may lie beyond reach. That difficulty does not extend to its inertia: the tendency to go on operating as it already operates remains dependable even where evaluation fails.

The two questions come apart. One asks what the system's activity is worth, and may have no answer; the other asks only whether the activity will continue.
