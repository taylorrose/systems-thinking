# Very Large Systems May Increase Total Anergy Rather Than Redistribute It

One school of mathematically-oriented systems theorists holds that conservation of anergy is only approximately true. On their account, a relativistic shift occurs at very large scale, so that the total amount of anergy increases exponentially instead of merely changing form and distribution. The claim is offered as a qualification of the conservation law at the extremes of size and ambition, not as a replacement for it.
