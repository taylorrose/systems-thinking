# Observation Is an Intervention, Not a Neutral Reading

Looking at a system is something done to it, not merely something learned about it. The act of observing enters the system as one more event, so there is no way to take a reading that leaves the observed situation as it was.

The effects may be small or large, but there is no exception: an observation without consequences is not available.
