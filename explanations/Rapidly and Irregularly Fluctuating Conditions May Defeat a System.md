# Rapidly and Irregularly Fluctuating Conditions May Defeat a System

The jobs a system handles badly seem to have something in common technically: the parameters fluctuate rapidly and irregularly. A system responds on its own rhythm to conditions it can register, and conditions that shift faster and less predictably than that offer it nothing stable to work against.
