# Strange Behavior in Systems Is Not Primarily Incompetence

People within a system do rise to their Level of Incompetence, with results everywhere visible, but competence has little if anything to do with the larger-scale manifestations of systems-function. Explaining bizarre institutional behavior by incompetence is like explaining crime by “criminality”: it names the behavior again instead of accounting for it. Would that the solution were so simple.
