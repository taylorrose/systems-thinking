# Self-Reference Is Often Felt Before It Is Recognized

Self-reference frequently announces itself as a sensation rather than as an analysis. The usual signals are a momentary confusion or double-take, a fleeting urge to laugh, or a feeling of exasperation that somehow fails to come to a specific focus.

Because the feeling arrives before the diagnosis, it can serve as an early indication that a statement, a system, or a situation has folded back on itself.
