# The Information at Hand May Not Be the Information Being Sought

The information already available is determined by what has been collected, retained, and allowed to reach the present inquiry. The information being sought is determined by the question now being asked. Because those are different conditions, possession does not imply relevance to the search.

The source states the mismatch emphatically while conceding that the two sometimes coincide. The claim is a warning about their tendency to diverge, not a denial that available information can occasionally be the information wanted.
