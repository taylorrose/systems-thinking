# The Probing Phase Is Never Finished

Asking when the probing phase is over, when the picture of the system is complete, or when the total response repertoire has been elicited answers itself: never. To assert that a list of a system's possible behaviours is complete would be to claim that every possible probe had been tried under every conceivable circumstance.

Reality does not allow that claim, so the potential behaviours of a system cannot be known in full. The limit belongs to the situation rather than to the diligence of the prober.
