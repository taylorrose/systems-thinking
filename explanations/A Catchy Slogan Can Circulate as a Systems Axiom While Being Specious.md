# A Catchy Slogan Can Circulate as a Systems Axiom While Being Specious

A formulation can be repeated and even asserted as though it were a genuine systems-axiom on the strength of how well it is phrased. Being catchy is a property of the wording, not evidence about how systems behave, so a memorable maxim may still be specious.

This slogan locates the problem in the people standing outside the solution, which is why it can be restated in a correct form that does not.
