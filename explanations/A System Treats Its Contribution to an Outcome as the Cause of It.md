# A System Treats Its Contribution to an Outcome as the Cause of It

A favorable event that passes anywhere near a system is read as the system’s own product. Having taught, funded, licensed, or processed the participants is taken as having produced the result, so the system’s share of the conditions is mistaken for the cause.
