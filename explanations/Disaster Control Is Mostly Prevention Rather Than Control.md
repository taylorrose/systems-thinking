# Disaster Control Is Mostly Prevention Rather Than Control

One is rarely privileged to control a disaster once it is under way, so the work carried on under that name consists of nipping disasters in the bud, limiting their effects, or preventing them outright. Understood this way it is a substantial portion of management and perhaps its most important part, which is why a manager who does it may be worth keeping even when nothing else appears to have gotten done.
