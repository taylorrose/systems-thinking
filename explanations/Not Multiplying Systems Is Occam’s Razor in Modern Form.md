# Not Multiplying Systems Is Occam’s Razor in Modern Form

The rule against unnecessary systems restates a familiar principle of parsimony. Where Occam’s Razor holds that entities should not be multiplied without necessity, its modern counterpart holds the same of systems.

The parallel places the burden where the older principle placed it. An additional system, like an additional entity, is not neutral: it stands in need of justification rather than merely of permission.
