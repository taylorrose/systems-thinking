# The Missing Word for Graceful Degradation Reflects a Taboo on Failure

Through the whole long history of human failures, no one has coined a word for the process of continuing to operate while degrading. The gap is traceable to an all-too-human taboo against thinking about failure at all. The borrowed systems-theory term ‘Graceful Degradation’ has to serve in the meantime, which leaves the most pervasive process around us named only by an awkward loan.
