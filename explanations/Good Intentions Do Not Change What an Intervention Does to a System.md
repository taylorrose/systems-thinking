# Good Intentions Do Not Change What an Intervention Does to a System

The kick meant to restore a stalled system is the same event to the system as any other kick; the helpful motive is not part of what the parts do next. Because the intervention acts on a system whose workings the helper does not control, trying to be helpful is a delicate and dangerous undertaking rather than a safe one.
