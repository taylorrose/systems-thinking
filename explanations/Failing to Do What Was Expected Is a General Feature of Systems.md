# Failing to Do What Was Expected Is a General Feature of Systems

Not doing what we expected them to do is a perfectly general property of systems rather than a defect of particular ones. Failure to function as expected is therefore to be expected, and it follows from systems-laws held to be as rigorous as any in natural science or mathematics.
