# System Designers Build In Ways to Bypass Their Own Systems

Rohe's Theorem holds that designers of systems tend to design ways for themselves to bypass the system. The people best placed to see where a system binds are the people who wrote it, and the exemption is built in at the point where they are the ones bound. The bypass is therefore not an abuse of the system by outsiders but a feature its own authors put there.
