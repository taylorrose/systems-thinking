# Solutions Usually Come from People Who See the Problem as an Interesting Puzzle

Solutions usually come from people for whom the problem is only an interesting puzzle, and whose qualifications would never satisfy a select committee. Great advances may accordingly be achieved by individuals working essentially alone or in small teams rather than by an assembled body of recognized experts.
