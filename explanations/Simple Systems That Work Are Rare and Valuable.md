# Simple Systems That Work Are Rare and Valuable

A simple system is not reliable merely because it is simple; simplicity leaves both outcomes open. The simple systems that do work are uncommon, and each one is a precious addition to the stock of usable human technology rather than a routine result of keeping things small.
