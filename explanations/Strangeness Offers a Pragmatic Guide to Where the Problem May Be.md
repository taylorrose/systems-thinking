# Strangeness Offers a Pragmatic Guide to Where the Problem May Be

“Strangeness” names a suspension of commonsense cause and effect. Its presence is offered as a pragmatic guide, not proof: when things behave very strangely, consider that a feedback situation may mark the locus of the problem.
