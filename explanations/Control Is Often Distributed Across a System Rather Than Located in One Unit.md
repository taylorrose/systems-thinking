# Control Is Often Distributed Across a System Rather Than Located in One Unit

Simplified illustrations such as a thermostat or a script suggest that mastery of a system lies in finding its control unit. That suggestion is misleading, because the simplified cases are unusually tidy. In a family system the control function is spread among the members, each holding some degree of control over the others, and the degree shifts with the activity in question and with other circumstances. The regulator being looked for may therefore be spread thinly across the system rather than seated in any single member.
