# The Law of Conservation of Anergy Treats Effort as a Conserved Quantity

The theorem of constant total anergy is named, naturally, the Law of Conservation of Anergy. Naming it that way treats the effort a situation demands as a quantity rather than as a mood or an opinion, and asserts that the universe's total of that quantity does not fall.
