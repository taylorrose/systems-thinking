# A System Embodies a Dream and Not Only Its Data

A system carries an image of what it is meant to become, held by whoever leads it. That image—a thought, a wish, a hope—is not derived from the reports the system collects; it supplies the direction those reports are read against. On this view a system is the embodiment of such an image made real, and is bounded by what can be imagined for it.
