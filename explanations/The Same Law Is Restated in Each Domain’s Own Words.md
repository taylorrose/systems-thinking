# The Same Law Is Restated in Each Domain’s Own Words

The same law appears in several wordings that carry the same general effect, each borrowing the vocabulary of the field that states it: administration speaks of what is reported, diplomacy of what is official, and television journalism of what happened on camera. Recognizing a familiar local maxim as one of these wordings shows that the maxim is not a quirk of that trade but the law appearing in its idiom.
