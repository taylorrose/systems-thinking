# Poor Performance Has Long Been Blamed on Special Circumstances

Men and women have observed this poor performance repeatedly down through the ages, but in the past it was always attributed to various special circumstances: this period, this office, these particular people. Each instance received its own local account, so the observation accumulated without ever becoming a general claim about systems.
