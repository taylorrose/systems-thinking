# Survival in an Unfavorable Setting Appears to Rest on Paradoxical or Highly Specialized Features

A system is occasionally found that succeeds, or at least survives, in a setting hostile to it. Where that happens, the basis of the success appears to lie in one or more features that are paradoxical or extremely specialized rather than in ordinary sound design. The setting itself remains unfavorable; only the narrow feature makes the difference.
