# Elegant Formulation Does Not Rescue the Promise of Perfection

Restating the expectation more wittily — perfection arriving the day after the final deadline — sharpens its phrasing and leaves its content untouched. No matter how elegantly it is formulated, the idea remains a delusion; what recommends it is its form, not any showing that a system arrives at a final state.
