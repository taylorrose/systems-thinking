# Claims That Systems Are Improving Are Answered by the Record of Events

A claim that systems are improving is met not with polemic but with the record. The reader is invited to survey what has actually happened over a stretch of time and to compare that record with the claim. A short collection of typical items is offered only to show how such a survey proceeds, not to complete it for the reader.
