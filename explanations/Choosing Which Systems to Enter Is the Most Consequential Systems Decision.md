# Choosing Which Systems to Enter Is the Most Consequential Systems Decision

Of all the points at which a person meets a system, the most poignant is that of becoming involved with it in the first place; everything the rest of a book on coping describes comes after that point. Involvement in certain systems may seem almost unavoidable, but the decision remains the one with the widest reach.
