# Refusing to Respond to Feedback Is a Passive Demand That the Environment Adjust

Responding adequately to feedback, especially undesired feedback that makes unexpected demands on the existing system, is difficult and taxing work that demands initiative. Declining that work is a form of passivity: instead of adjusting to its environment, the system is implicitly requiring the environment to adjust to it, which puts the cart before the horse.
