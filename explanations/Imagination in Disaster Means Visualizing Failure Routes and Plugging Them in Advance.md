# Imagination in Disaster Means Visualizing Failure Routes and Plugging Them in Advance

The capacity required is an ability to see the many routes by which a system could fail and to close them off before anything happens, without being paralyzed by the scenarios thus conjured up. Held in this form, the imagined disasters are the means by which timely action in the real world keeps them confined to fantasy.
