# Calling a System Its Own Best Explanation Names the Behavior Without Illuminating It

No axiom, proverb, or saying in common circulation conveys the gist of this behavior. Bertalanffy's Principle of Equifinality is a label unlikely to catch on, and the rendition offered by Watzlawick and colleagues is, in Gall's opinion, even less enlightening.

The phrase supplies a name for the phenomenon and stops there. Knowing that a system explains itself does not tell you which form it is driving toward or what would deflect it, so the behavior is better approached in small steps than through the slogan.
