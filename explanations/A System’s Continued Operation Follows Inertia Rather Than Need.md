# A System’s Continued Operation Follows Inertia Rather Than Need

A function that is already running carries on because it is running, in the manner of a body in motion. What sustains it is the momentum of its own established operation rather than a current requirement for its output.

Need and surrounding conditions are therefore not the terms in which continuation is decided. When either one changes, the operation has been given no reason of its own to change with it.
