# Hireling’s Hypnosis Suspends Normal Mental Activity in Members of a System

Hireling’s hypnosis may afflict anyone from chief officer to night watchman; for example, [[examples/Employees Watched Identical Bills Go Out and Said Nothing|employees watched identical bills go out and said nothing]], [[examples/Forty Years of Better Software Did Not Catch a Dropped Decimal Point|forty years of better software did not catch a dropped decimal point]], [[examples/A Small Bank Persuaded the Largest Banks to Fund Its Unreality|a small bank persuaded the largest banks to fund its unreality]], and [[examples/Amtrak Trains Stood Still Waiting for the Clock to Catch Up|Amtrak trains stood still waiting for the clock to catch up]]. Moreover, [[effects/Hireling’s Hypnosis Spreads to People Outside the System That Produced It|hireling’s hypnosis spreads to people outside the system that produced it]].
