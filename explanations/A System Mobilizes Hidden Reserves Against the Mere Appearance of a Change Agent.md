# A System Mobilizes Hidden Reserves Against the Mere Appearance of a Change Agent

The arrival of a change agent or efficiency expert is enough to provoke an unpredictable, sometimes almost hysterical, response from a system. Vast and previously unsuspected reserves of anergy are mobilized and made available for preservation of the status quo.

Nothing has yet been changed at that point; only the presence of someone who might change something. The reserves are invisible until a probe calls them out, so the state a probe encounters is already a state produced by the probing.
