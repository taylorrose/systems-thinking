# Probing Reveals the Range of Behavior a System Can Produce

Exploratory behavior is not trial and error but a series of probes, each of which elicits one piece of behavior from the system. Accumulating those pieces gives the prober a perspective on the range of responses the system is capable of producing. Variety of behavior on the prober's side is what makes that range visible, and a sufficiently ingenious new probe may elicit something close to the outcome wanted.
