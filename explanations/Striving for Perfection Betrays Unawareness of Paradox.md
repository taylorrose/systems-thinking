# Striving for Perfection Betrays Unawareness of Paradox

In a large system the striving is itself the serious imperfection, so the pursuit works against the goal it announces. Anyone who takes up that pursuit as though it were straightforwardly virtuous shows no awareness of the paradox they are standing inside.
