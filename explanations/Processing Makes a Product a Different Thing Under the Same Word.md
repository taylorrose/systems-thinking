# Processing Makes a Product a Different Thing Under the Same Word

What a product is depends on the systems-operations performed in making it, which is where Korzybski’s contribution to systems thinking becomes apparent. An apple processed through the supermarket system is not the apple picked off the tree, and using one word for two different things is an error.
