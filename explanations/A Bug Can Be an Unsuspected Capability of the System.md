# A Bug Can Be an Unsuspected Capability of the System

Sometimes what presents itself as a bug is a spontaneous offering of capabilities the system was not known to have, a glimpse of alternative functioning that the design specifications never contemplated. Whether the behaviour counts as a defect or as a capability depends on the frame of reference brought to it, not on the behaviour alone.
