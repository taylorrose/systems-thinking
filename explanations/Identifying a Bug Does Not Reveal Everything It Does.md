# Identifying a Bug Does Not Reveal Everything It Does

Finding a bug settles only that a particular fault exists. It does not deliver an inventory of what that fault has already been doing elsewhere in the system, nor of what the behaviour depends on further away. The effects that are known are the ones that happened to become visible; the rest remain outside the account, so a bug can be thoroughly known and its consequences still be only partly known.
