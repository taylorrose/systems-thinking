# A Simple System’s Stability Can Lie in Its Design or in Its Operator

A simple system that works often carries attributes of instability, functioning only when it is handled with special skill. Other simple systems possess stability as an attribute of the arrangement itself and behave the same way for whoever uses them. Both belong to the class of simple systems that work, but the location of their reliability differs.
