# A Receiver Constructs the Message from Their Own Assumptions

What arrives is not the sender’s message but the receiver’s reconstruction of it, assembled out of whatever the receiver already assumes. Everything presented, including the aids chosen to make the point clearer, is available as material for that reconstruction, so the message received need not be the message sent.
