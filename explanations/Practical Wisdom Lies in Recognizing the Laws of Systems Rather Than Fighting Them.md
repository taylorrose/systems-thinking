# Practical Wisdom Lies in Recognizing the Laws of Systems Rather Than Fighting Them

Systems behave according to regularities that hold whether or not anyone approves of them. The competence that matters in dealing with a system is therefore the ability to recognize which of those regularities is operating and to stop expending effort against it. Skill here consists of accurate perception rather than force of will.
