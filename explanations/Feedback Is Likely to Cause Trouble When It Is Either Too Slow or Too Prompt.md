# Feedback Is Likely to Cause Trouble When It Is Either Too Slow or Too Prompt

Promptness is not a quantity to be maximized. Feedback that returns faster than the system can absorb it is likely to cause trouble of its own, just as feedback that lags is. Both extremes are failures of timing rather than failures of information.
