# Trends Can Be Noted Even When the Future Cannot Be Predicted

Unpredictability does not make observation worthless. A trend is a description of a direction already visible in the record, and noting it is available even where forecasting is not. It reports what has been moving which way, and claims nothing about where the movement ends.
