# A Request for Information May Be Misdirected from the Decision

Wanting information identifies what the inquiry is aimed at; needing information identifies what the decision actually depends on. A request can be answered exactly as asked while leaving the decision no better informed because the request was aimed at the wrong thing.

The source deliberately overstates this separation to put emphasis on the gap. It does not claim that wanted and needed information can never coincide.
