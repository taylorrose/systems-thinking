# The Parts of a System Do Not Share the System’s Purpose

A system may be designed to do something, but its parts are unlikely to have, as parts, any tendency to do that thing spontaneously. Their tendencies often run the other way, and many parts are needed mainly as correctives to the tendencies of other parts. What the system does, it does only as a system, which places its behavior at a different level from the behavior of anything inside it.
