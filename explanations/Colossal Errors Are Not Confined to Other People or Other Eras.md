# Colossal Errors Are Not Confined to Other People or Other Eras

It is tempting to believe that colossal errors happened to others, at a remote historical distance, and could never happen again, least of all to us. That temptation has to be resolutely shaken off; the recorded cases are offered not as history but as reason to look at present undertakings of comparable scale.
