# A Metaphor About Armies States a General Systems Pattern

The army preparing for the previous war is offered as a metaphor, chosen to make an abstract principle of systems-operation vivid rather than to make a claim about military affairs alone. The figure applies to any system that has organized itself around the challenge it last faced. The literal case is not thereby rare: the list of armies that really were prepared for the previous war is impressively long.
