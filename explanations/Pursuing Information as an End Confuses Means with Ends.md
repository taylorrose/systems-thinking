# Pursuing Information as an End Confuses Means with Ends

Information is a means: it is worth having when it serves a question someone actually needs answered. Pursuit of information-as-such reverses that order, treating accumulation itself as the goal, so effort goes into gathering rather than into the purpose the gathering was supposed to serve.
