# Too Much Feedback Overwhelms the Channels That Must Respond to It

Total feedback is not a benefit. A system has a limited capacity to act on what it receives, and feedback arriving faster or in greater volume than that capacity can saturate rather than improve its responses. Even a system built to accept massive feedback, such as the human brain, has this limit.
