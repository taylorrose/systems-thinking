# Patching a System That Will Not Fly Is Pushing on It at the Design Level

Discovering that a large new system will not fly comes as a shock, and the natural response is to patch it up and make it go by bolting on new features, accessories, and jury-rigged appendages.

That response is the design-level equivalent of Pushing On the System: effort applied against the arrangement rather than to the arrangement, and it works about as poorly.
