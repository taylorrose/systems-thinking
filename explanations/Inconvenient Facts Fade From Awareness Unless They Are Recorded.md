# Inconvenient Facts Fade From Awareness Unless They Are Recorded

Observations that run counter to a theory are not held in mind the way confirming observations are. Attention slides off them, and a painful or awkward encounter tends to disappear from consciousness once it is over. Unless the fact is given special notice at the moment it appears, it simply fades out of awareness and stops counting as evidence.
