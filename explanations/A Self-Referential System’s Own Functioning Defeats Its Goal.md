# A Self-Referential System’s Own Functioning Defeats Its Goal

Even a small system that becomes significantly self-referential is in for trouble. When the activity a system performs is also the obstacle to the result it wants, running the system harder moves the goal further away.

The functioning and the failure are then the same event: trying to produce the result produces its opposite.
