# Grandiose Goals Make an Obvious Risk Easy to Forget

That large failures follow large systems is close to a truism, yet it is often overlooked in the excited pursuit of grandiose goals by means of overblown systems. The obviousness of the risk does not protect against it; enthusiasm for the goal is what pushes the risk out of view.
