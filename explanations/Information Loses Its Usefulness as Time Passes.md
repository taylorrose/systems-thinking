# Information Loses Its Usefulness as Time Passes

Information is not a durable holding. What was accurate when recorded describes a state of affairs that continues to change, so the same stored item steadily loses its correspondence to the situation it was meant to describe. Decay is the normal condition of information, not an accident of poor storage.
