# The Crash Approach Adds Anxiety and Hysteria to a Misconceived Problem

The “Crash” approach keeps the adverse dynamics of treating a puzzle as a Capital-P Problem and adds two further pressures: Administrator’s Anxiety, which pushes on the system to make it work, and plain hysteria. The result is even more disastrous than the “Problem” approach it starts from, because the artificial urgency is applied to a conceptualization that was already wrong.
