# A Total System Is One That Involves Everything in Itself

A total system is a colossal system carried far enough that everything is caught up in it. Nothing of consequence is left outside its boundary, so the errors and delusions of colossal systems can be expected to appear in it at a still higher pitch of grandiosity.
