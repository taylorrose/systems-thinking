# A Higher-Level Pattern of Organization Becomes Real Once the Structure Supports It

As the necessary richness of underlying structure is attained, a new property emerges, seemingly out of nowhere. Nothing was added at the moment of emergence; a higher-level pattern of organization has simply become a reality, and the system now has a property that belongs to that level rather than to any of its parts.
