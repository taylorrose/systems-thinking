# System Growth Is Independent of the Work to Be Done

Parkinson's tentative study of administrative systems found an average rate of growth of five to six percent per annum, corrected for inflation, regardless of the work to be done. Growth on this account is a property of the system itself rather than a response to demand: the workload may rise, fall, or stay level while the rate of enlargement continues largely unchanged.
