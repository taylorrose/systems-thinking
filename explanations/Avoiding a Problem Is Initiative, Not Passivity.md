# Avoiding a Problem Is Initiative, Not Passivity

Because the strategy is so alien to ingrained habits of thinking and acting, it is sometimes rejected as unworthy and confused with passivity. But the opposite of passivity is initiative, or responsibility — not energetic futility. Choosing not to engage is itself a decision actively and responsibly taken, and it disposes of the whole meta-problem of dealing with the problem.
