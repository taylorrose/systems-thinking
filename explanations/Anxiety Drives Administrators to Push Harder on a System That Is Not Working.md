# Anxiety Drives Administrators to Push Harder on a System That Is Not Working

Pushing harder on a nonfunctioning system to make it work better is the most widespread and futile of all administrative errors. It follows from not knowing that big systems work on their own or not at all: an administrator who believes the system’s functioning is his to supply responds to its failure with pressure, and the anxiety of the position keeps the pressure coming.
