# A System Can Seem Malignant Without Being So

Stated as a law about what *will* go wrong, the observation smuggles in purpose: something appears to be choosing the worst available outcome. That teleological element is gratuitous and unjustified, closer to superstition or belief in magic than to an account of how things behave. The universe is not actually malignant; it only seems so to whoever is standing where the failure lands.
