# Apparent Exceptions to the Operational Fallacy Almost Invariably Dissolve Under Closer Inspection

Cases that seem to show a large system delivering just what was wanted can be found in abundance. Closer inspection of the operations behind the product will almost invariably reveal the true state of affairs instead.
