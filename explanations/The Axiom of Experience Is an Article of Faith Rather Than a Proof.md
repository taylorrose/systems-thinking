# The Axiom of Experience Is an Article of Faith Rather Than a Proof

The claim that the future will resemble the past is supported here only by the observation that it has done so before, which is itself an appeal to past experience. As Weinberg notes, that makes the pseudo-theorem an article of faith: it assumes the very continuity it is offered to establish.
