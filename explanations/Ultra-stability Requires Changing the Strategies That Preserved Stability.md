# Ultra-stability Requires Changing the Strategies That Preserved Stability

What has to change is not the system's substance but its *patterns of changing* — the strategies it previously used to prevent drastic internal change. Being able to change in that way, so as to stay stable when the ground rules move, is a higher-order stability, and it earns the name ultra-stability.
