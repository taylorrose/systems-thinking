# A System’s Ultimate Purpose May Be Unknowable

Beyond the inner goals conditioned by a system’s structure there may or may not be some ultimate purpose; if there is, it may lie past anyone’s reach. It is hard enough to establish a few fragmentary aspects of what a system is actually doing, so the question is better set aside than answered confidently.
