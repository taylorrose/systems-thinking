# A Communication Means Whatever Behavior It Produces

The meaning of a communication lies in the behavior that follows it, not in what the sender intended or believes was said. This runs against ordinary common sense and is uncomfortable to accept, and the question it puts is a plain one: are we willing to subject our communications to the test of actual outcomes?
