# A Fail-Safe Device Is Itself a Part That Can Fail

A device added to a system to guarantee safe failure is one more component of that system, with its own ways of breaking. Its design governs how it behaves while it is performing its protective function; it says nothing about how it behaves during ordinary operation, when it is simply part of the running system. A breakdown at that moment is not covered by the safety it was installed to provide.
