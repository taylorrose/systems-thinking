# No Single Hidden Defect Explains Why Systems Fail

Monistic accounts try to name the one flaw behind every breakdown. Korzybski's General Semantics took that form, treating all failures of human systems as misunderstandings—failures of communication—and the effort was flawed by that single viewpoint.

Systems in general are not prevented from working by one subtle, hidden defect, whether of communication or of anything else. Human systems differ from other systems only in degree, not in kind, so there is no special human flaw to isolate as the cause.
