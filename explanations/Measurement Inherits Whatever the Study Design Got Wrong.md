# Measurement Inherits Whatever the Study Design Got Wrong

Numbers report on whatever the study was arranged to observe. If the arrangement watches the wrong thing, compares the wrong groups, or asks a question the situation cannot answer, quantifying the results carries that flaw forward in a more precise-looking form.

This is the research form of the general point about bad design: measurement is an addition to a study, and an addition cannot repair the arrangement it is added to.
