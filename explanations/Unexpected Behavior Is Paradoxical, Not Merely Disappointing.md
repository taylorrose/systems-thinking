# Unexpected Behavior Is Paradoxical, Not Merely Disappointing

Beneath the common observation that things do not work very well lies an element of paradox. Systems do not merely fall short of what was intended; they work out in strange ways, producing results nobody expected and often the opposite of the one aimed at.
