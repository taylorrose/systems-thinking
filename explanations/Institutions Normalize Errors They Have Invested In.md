# Institutions Normalize Errors They Have Invested In

Institutions can absorb contrary evidence without recognizing the investment itself as error; for example, [[examples/An Unworkable Missile System Was Declared Unnecessary After Completion|an unworkable missile system was declared unnecessary after completion]], [[examples/Russia Built and Abandoned Its Matching Missile System|Russia built and abandoned its matching missile system]], and [[examples/Medical Research Spent Twenty Years Studying the Wrong Fats|medical research spent twenty years studying the wrong fats]].
