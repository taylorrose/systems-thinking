# The Word Bug Makes Its Information Look Worthless

The word “bug” suggests something worthless if not repulsive, something whose only proper fate is elimination. That connotation sets the disposal of the fault as the whole task and makes what the fault reports look like nothing worth keeping, which is a judgement carried by the name rather than by the thing named.
