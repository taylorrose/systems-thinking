# When Competition Never Ends, Sheer Survival Becomes the Trait Selected

Often enough the competitive conditions never come to an end. Where the contest simply continues, the quality finally selected for is not skill, insight, or output but the brute ability to survive, because survival is the only test the contest keeps administering.
