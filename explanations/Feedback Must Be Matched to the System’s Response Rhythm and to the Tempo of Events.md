# Feedback Must Be Matched to the System’s Response Rhythm and to the Tempo of Events

Two independent tempos constrain feedback timing. One is the rhythm at which the system can register information and respond to it; the other is the pace at which the events being reported actually unfold. Feedback has to be adjusted to both at once, which is a double restriction rather than a single target.
