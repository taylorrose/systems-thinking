# A System Delivers You to Its Own Terminus, Not to the Place You Named

A system operates on its own geography and its own states, and those termini carry the names of the destinations people care about without being those destinations. What the system can deliver is its own end point; what the name promises lies outside it. Reaching the point the system calls the destination therefore need not mean reaching the thing the name refers to.
