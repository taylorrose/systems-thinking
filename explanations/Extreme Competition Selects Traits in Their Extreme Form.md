# Extreme Competition Selects Traits in Their Extreme Form

When a system's demand takes the form of competition and that competition is pushed to an extreme, the traits it selects are selected in their extreme degree rather than in a moderate one. The result is a peculiar quality rather than a merely strong one: what wins under extreme conditions looks bizarre when measured against ordinary conditions.
