# The Cost of Ignoring Feedback Is a Cybernetic Law, Not an Opinion

That a system ignoring feedback has entered terminal instability is presented as a general cybernetic law rather than as a preference or a piece of advice. It therefore cannot be repealed by those who dislike its implications, and disagreeing with it does not exempt a system from it.
