# A Stuck Problem May Lie in the Probe, the Solution, or the Question

Three placements are worth considering before assuming a problem is unsolvable. The problem may lie in the probe, where our own efforts fail to elicit the behavior we want from the system. It may lie in the solution, where the trouble is a consequence of the system functioning exactly as designed. Or it may lie in the question, where the problem was inadequately formulated at the outset.
