# The Belief in Imminent Perfection Is Held with Religious Fixity

The expectation that finality is nearly in hand is not held as a tentative estimate. Its original author is unknown and nothing is offered in its support, yet it is repeated with all the fixity of revealed religion — as an article of faith rather than a claim anyone expects to test.
