# Eccentric Behavior Shows Ingenuity Rather Than Defect

The salient feature of most mad and eccentric behavior is not its defectiveness but its incredible ingenuity — ingenuity of a kind that merely rational persons, doctors among them, cannot deal with. Reading the behavior as a malfunction of the organ mistakes the very quality that makes it hard to treat.
