# Problem Avoidance Sidesteps a Problem That Offers No Toe-Hold

Problem avoidance is the strategy of declining a head-on encounter with a stubborn problem that offers no good point d’appui, or toe-hold. Its practitioners are not struggling valiantly against staggering odds; they are simply somewhere else, successfully doing something else. It is, the author suggests, the most under-rated of all methods of dealing with problems.
