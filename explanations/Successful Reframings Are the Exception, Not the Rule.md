# Successful Reframings Are the Exception, Not the Rule

A reframing may occasionally be encountered, and more happily still, initiated. The Reality Principle holds that such occurrences remain exceptional rather than typical, so tempered and moderate pessimism, rather than expectation of success, is the mark of a seasoned systems-student.
