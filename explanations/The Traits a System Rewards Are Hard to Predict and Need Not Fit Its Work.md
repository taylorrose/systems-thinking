# The Traits a System Rewards Are Hard to Predict and Need Not Fit Its Work

The traits a system fosters are difficult to infer in advance and need not be the traits required to operate it successfully; for example, [[examples/Getting Elected Requires Different Qualities Than Running the Country|getting elected requires different qualities than running the country]].
