# Specialization Makes the Simplest Tasks Too Difficult

As a system becomes ever more highly specialized, the tasks that were once trivial for it mysteriously become too difficult. Growth in size and complexity tends to run alongside a reverse process in which established functions are lost rather than added, so the elaborate later system may be unable to do what its simple predecessor did easily.
