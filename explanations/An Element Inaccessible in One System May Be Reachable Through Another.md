# An Element Inaccessible in One System May Be Reachable Through Another

Because the same element belongs to more than one system at once, its accessibility depends on which system is being worked through. Viewed as a component of system A it may be beyond reach; viewed as a component of system B, C, or D it may perhaps be moved in the desired direction by intervening there instead. Linkage is what makes this route available.
