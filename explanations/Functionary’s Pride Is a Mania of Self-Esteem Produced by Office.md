# Functionary’s Pride Is a Mania of Self-Esteem Produced by Office

Shakespeare characterized this ancient disorder as “the insolence of office”: a mania of self-esteem induced by titles and the illusion of power, familiar enough to need no elaboration. What is new is the attribution — not to inherent vice in the holder, nor to maladministration, but to the operation of the F.L.A.W. in the system itself upon whoever holds the office.
