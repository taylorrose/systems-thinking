# Counting Information as a Bulk Commodity Ignores Whether a Message Matters

Information theory treats what remains of a communication after its meanings have been removed, defining information as a bulk commodity to be weighed out without regard to the quality of individual units. Under that accounting, “Let there be light!” and a cigarette jingle carry exactly equal weight, because the measure has no place to record which message matters.
