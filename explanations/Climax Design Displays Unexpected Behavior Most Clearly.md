# Climax Design Displays Unexpected Behavior Most Clearly

Climax Design is the construction of the largest and most complex examples of manmade systems: buildings, ships, planes, or organizations built at the limit of what can be built. The ultimate model—the largest, fastest, or tallest—often, if not invariably, exhibits behavior so unexpected as to verge on the uncanny, which makes it the clearest place to watch unpredictability at work.
