# Success Comes from Commitment to How Systems Behave Rather Than to One System

The loyalty that produces success is not loyalty to a particular system. It is loyalty to an understanding of how systems behave, which treats any given system as one available means among others rather than as the thing to be served.

Held that way, a system and even the goal it was adopted to reach remain revisable. What stays fixed is the attention to systems behavior that decides which system and which goal are worth holding now.
