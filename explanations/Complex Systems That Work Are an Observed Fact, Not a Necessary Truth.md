# Complex Systems That Work Are an Observed Fact, Not a Necessary Truth

That some complex systems function is a report of what has been observed, not an axiom and not a truism. Nothing in the reasoning about systems requires it to hold, and it stands as the obverse of the Primal Scenario rather than as a consequence of it. It is accepted as a given because it is found, not because it is guaranteed.
