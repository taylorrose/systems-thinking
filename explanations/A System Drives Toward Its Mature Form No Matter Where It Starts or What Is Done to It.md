# A System Drives Toward Its Mature Form No Matter Where It Starts or What Is Done to It

A large system often behaves as though it knew where it wanted to go and were determined to get there, whatever its starting point and whatever anyone else wants. Hardship, interference, and repeated correction change the route rather than the destination.

Unless it is stopped completely, what emerges is recognizably the form the system was heading toward.
