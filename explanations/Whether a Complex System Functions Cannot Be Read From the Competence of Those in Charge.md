# Whether a Complex System Functions Cannot Be Read From the Competence of Those in Charge

Whether a complex system functions cannot be inferred from the competence of those in charge; for example, [[examples/Insane Monarchs Ruled With Results Indistinguishable From the Wisest Kings|insane monarchs ruled with results indistinguishable from the wisest kings]], whereas [[examples/Charlemagne’s Fair Division of His Empire Produced a Thousand Years of Strife|Charlemagne’s fair division of his empire produced a thousand years of strife]].
