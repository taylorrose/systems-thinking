# The Difficulty Lies in the Manner of Coping, Not in the Occurrence of Problems

That a problem has arisen is not itself the source of the trouble; problems arrive as a condition of dealing with systems at all. What differs from case to case is the manner of coping — how the situation is approached and how it is described — and it is there that things go wrong. Locating the difficulty in the problem's mere occurrence points attention away from the part of the situation actually in question.
