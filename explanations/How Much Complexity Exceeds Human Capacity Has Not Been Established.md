# How Much Complexity Exceeds Human Capacity Has Not Been Established

Asking how much complexity is too much for humans to handle presumes a threshold, and little relevant research has been done on it. What is available instead is illustrative anecdote, which can show that the limit is real without fixing where it lies. The theorem it supports is therefore offered without commitment as to its ultimate correctness.
