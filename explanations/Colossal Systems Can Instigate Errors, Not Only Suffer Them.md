# Colossal Systems Can Instigate Errors, Not Only Suffer Them

More unsettling than the size of a big system’s failures is its propensity for actually instigating or promoting errors of matching scale. The system does not merely magnify an error made elsewhere; it can be the setting in which the error is produced. Warfare, perhaps the most grandiose of human enterprises, offers many prime examples, and the same tendency appears in large peacetime undertakings.
