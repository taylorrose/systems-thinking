# A Limitation Is Recognized by Bumping Into It Repeatedly

The distinguishing feature of a genuine limitation is that one keeps bumping up against it. The same boundary has already appeared in other guises — as the generalized uncertainty principle, as the Kantian hypothesis, and elsewhere — which is why encountering it again should not be a surprise.

Recurrence under different descriptions is the evidence that a wall has been found rather than a particular method exhausted.
