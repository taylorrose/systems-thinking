# Striving for Perfection Narrows Attention to a Hypnotic Focus

Striving for perfection produces a kind of tunnel-vision, resembling a hypnotic state. Absorbed in perfecting the system at hand, the striver attends to the details of the current design and to little else; the narrowing is a property of the striving itself rather than of the system being worked on.
