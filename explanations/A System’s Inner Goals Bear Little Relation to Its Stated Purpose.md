# A System’s Inner Goals Bear Little Relation to Its Stated Purpose

A system’s inner goals bear little relation to its stated purpose; for example, [[examples/Slogans Like “Making the World Safe for Democracy” Reveal a Delusion System|slogans like “making the world safe for Democracy” reveal a delusion system]]. Therefore, [[practices/Read the Stated Purpose as a Gauge of the Designers’ Delusion|read the stated purpose as a gauge of the designers’ delusion]].
