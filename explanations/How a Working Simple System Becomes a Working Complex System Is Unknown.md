# How a Working Simple System Becomes a Working Complex System Is Unknown

That working complex systems grow out of working simple ones says nothing about the mechanism of the growth. The transition itself has not been described, so the rule can be followed without any account of why it holds. Few questions about systems offer greater potential reward for first-rate research.
