# The Creative Tack Is Active Rather Than a Response to Impasse

Formulations that tell you to stop doing what is not working still wait for an impasse before they apply, and so miss the agile, flexible spirit of the maneuver. The Creative Tack is not passive and does not wait: it is the active fitting together of the right problem with the right resources and the right timing.
