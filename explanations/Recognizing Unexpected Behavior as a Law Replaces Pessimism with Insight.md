# Recognizing Unexpected Behavior as a Law Replaces Pessimism with Insight

Observing that one system is not working as expected remains a pessimistic feeling about that case. Recognizing unexpected behavior as a general law instead turns the same experience into an insight about systems as a class. The source calls this law the Generalized Uncertainty Principle.
