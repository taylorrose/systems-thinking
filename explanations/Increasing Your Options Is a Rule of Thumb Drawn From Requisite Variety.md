# Increasing Your Options Is a Rule of Thumb Drawn From Requisite Variety

The imperative comes from cybernetics rather than from ethics on its own terms. Taking the law that control follows the greatest variety of behavioral responses, some cyberneticists proposed acting so as to increase one's options as a rule of thumb, and von Foerster's name is attached to it as an ethical imperative. Its standing is that of a rule of thumb derived from requisite variety, not a demonstrated method.
