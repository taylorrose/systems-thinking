# A System Can Be Entered by Satisfying Its Signals Rather Than Its Purpose

A system can be entered by producing the appearance it reads as qualifying rather than the condition it was meant to serve; for example, [[examples/A Healthy Man Won Two Hundred Hospital Admissions by Appearing Ill|a healthy man won two hundred hospital admissions by appearing ill]].
