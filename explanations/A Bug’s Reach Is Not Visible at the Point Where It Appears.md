# A Bug’s Reach Is Not Visible at the Point Where It Appears

A bug shows itself at one place in a system, and that appearance suggests a bounded defect awaiting a quick hard-wire fix. But the place where a fault becomes visible does not establish the extent of what it touches, so locality remains an assumption rather than an observation, however plausible it looks.
