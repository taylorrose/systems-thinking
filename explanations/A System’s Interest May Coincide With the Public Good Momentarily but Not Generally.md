# A System’s Interest May Coincide With the Public Good Momentarily but Not Generally

Propositions of this form can be accurate at a particular moment, when what helps the system happens to help everyone else as well. They cannot be generally correct in any logical or scientific sense, because nothing links the two goods beyond that coincidence.
