# Reframing Is Itself a System Subject to the Same Laws

However powerful it is, the technique of reframing is not a panacea. It is a system, and therefore falls under every law that governs systems, along with others not yet discovered. In actual experience it will be found to act up, kick back, and fail unexpectedly as insidiously and enthusiastically as any other system.
