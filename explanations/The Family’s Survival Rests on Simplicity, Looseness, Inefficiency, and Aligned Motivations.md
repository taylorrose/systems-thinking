# The Family’s Survival Rests on Simplicity, Looseness, Inefficiency, and Aligned Motivations

The family has survived stresses greater than those of the present day. The source attributes that strength to an extremely simple structure, looseness in everyday functioning, “inefficiency” in an efficiency expert’s sense, and strong alignment with basic primate motivations.
