# Past Behavior Is the Most Reliable Prediction of a System’s Future Behavior

Stated without technical language, inertia is a rule of thumb about repetition: the record of what a system has already done is the surest available guide to what it will do next.

The prediction rests on nothing more than that record. It does not require knowing why the system behaves as it does, nor any account of its purpose or design.
