# Urgency Shortens the Useful Half-Life of Information

Information has a useful half-life, and that span contracts as the situation it describes moves faster. Where a decision turns on conditions changing second by second, the item describing those conditions is spent almost as soon as it arrives, and larger systems tend to operate at still higher levels of urgency.
