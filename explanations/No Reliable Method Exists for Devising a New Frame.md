# No Reliable Method Exists for Devising a New Frame

In practice a genuinely suitable new frame stays elusive, and no surefire program produces one. Even mathematicians cannot say how they suddenly “see” the elegant route to a solution; the only available formula for creativity is the instruction to be creative. Machines cannot be expected to outdo us here either, since teaching them would require articulating what we ourselves do not understand.
