# Under-Designing for Feedback Breeds the Delusion That Feedback Is Hostile

A system is commonly built without any estimate of how much feedback is appropriate, or even of how much will arrive. That under-design leaves it unable to absorb what comes, which produces fear of feedback and then, by familiar psychological mechanisms, a systems-delusion in which the sources of feedback are treated as hostile and even dangerous to the system.
