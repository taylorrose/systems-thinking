# Grandiosity Belongs to No Particular Party or Ideology

Politics offers many striking examples of Grandiosity, but it is not the property of one party, nor the prerogative of Conservatives or of Liberals. The scale of the intended correction, not its political direction, is what makes a plan grandiose.
