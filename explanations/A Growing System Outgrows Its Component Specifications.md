# A Growing System Outgrows Its Component Specifications

Components are specified for a system of a given size and complexity. As the system grows in both, it gradually but inevitably passes beyond what those specifications were chosen to cover, and parts—whether human or electronic—begin to fail. The failures are a consequence of the growth rather than of defective parts.
