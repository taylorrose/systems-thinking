# Delayed Feedback Prevents a System From Acting Wisely

Wisdom in Nature depends on how quickly consequences return to their source, and Systems generally share that dependence. When feedback is unduly delayed, the system continues to act on a state of affairs that has already changed, so its responses stop tracking the situation they were meant to address.
