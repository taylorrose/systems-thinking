# Grand Purposes Excuse Self-Defeating Means

The more exalted a system’s stated purpose becomes, the more easily actions that defeat it can be defended as necessary; for example, [[examples/Destroying a Village Was Defended as Saving It|destroying a village was defended as saving it]], [[examples/Civilization Was Proposed to Be Preserved Under a Mountain|civilization was proposed to be preserved under a mountain]], [[examples/Tenfold Nuclear Overkill Was Regarded as Insufficient|tenfold nuclear overkill was regarded as insufficient]], and [[examples/An Invulnerable Retaliatory Missile Was Vulnerable and Useless|an invulnerable retaliatory missile was vulnerable and useless]].
