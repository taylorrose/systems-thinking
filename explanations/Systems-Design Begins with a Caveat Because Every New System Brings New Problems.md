# Systems-Design Begins with a Caveat Because Every New System Brings New Problems

The art of systems-design is best approached through a series of caveats rather than through recommendations. Because new systems mean new problems, the first principle available to a designer is a negative one: a warning about what not to build.

Design therefore starts before any structure is chosen. Its opening question is whether a new system should exist at all, since bringing one into being is itself the act that introduces the new problems.
