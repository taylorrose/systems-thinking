# Commonsense Cause and Effect Can Be Suspended Without the Observer Being Mistaken

Inside a controlled loop, an action can reliably produce the opposite of its ordinary result. The observer who reports that reversal is describing what happens, not misperceiving it; empirically the fire does make him cold, and the bigger fire makes him colder. What has failed is the commonsense link between cause and effect, not the observation.
