# Discarded Information Can Be Ripening for Another System

Usefulness is a relation between information and a particular system, not a property the information carries by itself. A quotation, a memo, or a coordinate loses its value only with reference to the system whose questions it was serving.

Information that has outlived its usefulness in one system may be just maturing with reference to another, whose questions arrive later or differ in kind. The same item can therefore be waste at one address and stock in trade at the next.
