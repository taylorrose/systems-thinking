# Reachability Depends on the Starting State, Not on Effort

Which end states a system can reach is settled largely by the state it is in now. Ingenuity and persistence operate only inside the set of configurations the present state makes accessible, and for most starting points the majority of other configurations lie outside that set.

Further manipulation does not enlarge the set. When the wanted state is not among the accessible ones, more effort of the same kind cannot bring it within reach.
