# Unchallenged Credit-Taking Escalates Into Equating the System’s Good With the Common Good

A system that is allowed to keep claiming credit for favorable events does not stop at particular outcomes. The claim grows into the more grandiose conviction expressed by the dictum “What’s Good For General Motors Is Good For The Country” — the system’s own advantage restated as everyone’s advantage.
