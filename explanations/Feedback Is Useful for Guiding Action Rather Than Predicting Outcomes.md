# Feedback Is Useful for Guiding Action Rather Than Predicting Outcomes

If feedback cannot forecast what is coming, the question becomes what it is good for at all. Its use lies in guiding the present response: the most recent available picture can make that response more appropriate than one chosen from older information. Feedback informs the next move rather than revealing the result of it.
