# Stepwise Appearance Restates the Generalized Uncertainty Principle in Optimistic Terms

The sudden arrival of new functions is not a separate law. It is the Generalized Uncertainty Principle stated optimistically: where that principle says systems do unexpected things, this one says some of the unexpected things are new capabilities. The unpredictability is the same in both readings.
