# Attention Fixed on the Goal Keeps Our Own Errors Out of View

Although the mind works by error-correction, it has no appetite for knowing about its errors and keeps its eyes on the goal instead. As Iacocca observed, we like to read about success while what we must deal with in everyday life is failure. The activity that actually occupies us is thus the one we attend to least.
