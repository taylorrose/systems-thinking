# Designers Resist Unpredictability in Systems They Built Themselves

Biologists meet the unpredictability of living things daily, so the principle reads to them as ordinary experience. Mathematicians and engineers, accustomed to creating systems out of their own heads, are affronted when their own creatures behave in ways they never planned or even imagined; some go so far as to call such unpredictability illogical.

Underneath the objection lies a wish rather than a fact. Most people would like to think of themselves as anticipating every contingency, but that is a yearning, not an accomplished state.
