# The Creative Tack Finds Problems That the Resources at Hand Can Solve Elegantly

The Creative Tack is the maneuver of finding problems that can be neatly and elegantly solved with the resources, or systems, already at hand. It is allied to problem avoidance but distinct from it: avoidance declines a problem that offers no toe-hold, while the Creative Tack goes looking for the problem that does. Popular lore, the author notes, offers no proverb that designates the maneuver precisely.
