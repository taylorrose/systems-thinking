# Bad Design Is Sometimes Apparent on Simple Inspection

A design fault does not always wait for operation to reveal itself. Comparing what a system is built to do against what its parts are actually rated to withstand can expose the mismatch before any failure occurs, in familiar everyday systems as readily as in unfamiliar ones.
