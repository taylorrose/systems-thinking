# Stability Won Under One Set of Rules Does Not Survive a Rule Change

Designing a system to settle into equilibrium somewhere inside the range of achievable environmental conditions is already an art. But the history of large systems shows a subtler challenge waiting past that one: remaining stable when the rules themselves change. Machines, organizations, and organisms that fail this second test find their earlier stability is no longer of any use to them.
