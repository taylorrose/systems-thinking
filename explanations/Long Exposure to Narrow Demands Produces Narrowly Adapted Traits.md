# Long Exposure to Narrow Demands Produces Narrowly Adapted Traits

Specialized Selection is the principle that a narrow, sustained demand shapes whatever is subjected to it. A relatively undifferentiated organism, exposed long enough to one set of pressures, can develop capacities that answer those pressures and little else. The same logic is what a specialized system applies to the people it recruits and keeps: the more specialized the demand, the more specialized the traits that pass through it.
