# Finding a Helpful System Is a Chancy Art With an Outsized Payoff

Locating the system that offers the leverage resembles reframing closely enough that at some deeper level the two may be identical, and it shares reframing's unreliability: both are chancy arts rather than procedures. What makes the attempt worth making is the size of the occasional success, which stands well above the small returns a systems-student ordinarily expects.
