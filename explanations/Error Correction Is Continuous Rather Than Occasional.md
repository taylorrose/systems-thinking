# Error Correction Is Continuous Rather Than Occasional

Life is not a matter of correcting the occasional error, bug, or glitch against a background of things going right. Error-correction is what we are doing every instant. The most often used word in any language is not a, an, or the, nor me, my, or mine; it is “oops.”
