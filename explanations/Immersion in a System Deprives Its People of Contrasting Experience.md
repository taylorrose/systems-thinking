# Immersion in a System Deprives Its People of Contrasting Experience

The F.L.A.W. isolates the systems-person from the everyday world, so the range of experience available to him narrows to what the system admits. Sensory deprivation tends to produce hallucinations; similarly, immersion tends to produce an altered mental state whose malfunctions are recognizable to outsiders but not to the person immersed. Functionary’s Fault is the resulting complex of malfunctions, attributable primarily to that lack of alternative or contrasting experience.
