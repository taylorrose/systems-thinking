# Denial Rather Than Ignorance Keeps the Futility of Pushing Unappreciated

Cautionary examples abound, so a lack of available evidence cannot explain why the futility of pushing on the system remains so widely unappreciated. The likelier cause is denial — the process by which we simply negate whatever part of reality stands in the way of our plans. Where lapses from reality are punished quickly, as in computer technology, the awareness recurs anyway.
