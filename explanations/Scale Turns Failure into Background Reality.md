# Scale Turns Failure into Background Reality

When harm is pervasive and familiar, people may experience it as background reality rather than system failure; for example, [[examples/Fifty Thousand Road Deaths Were Treated as a Fact of Life|fifty thousand road deaths were treated as a fact of life]], while [[examples/Replacing Street Railways With Cars Was Called Progress|replacing street railways with cars was called progress]].
