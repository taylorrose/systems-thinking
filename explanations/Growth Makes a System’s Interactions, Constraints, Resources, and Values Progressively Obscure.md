# Growth Makes a System’s Interactions, Constraints, Resources, and Values Progressively Obscure

As a system becomes larger its parts interact more, so its environmental constraints become harder to understand and the question of what resources should be made available becomes more obscure. Deepest of all, the problem of the system's legitimate values becomes more difficult. Difficulty of evaluation therefore rises with size along several dimensions at once, the hardest of which is not technical.
