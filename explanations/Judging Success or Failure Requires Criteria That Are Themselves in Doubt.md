# Judging Success or Failure Requires Criteria That Are Themselves in Doubt

Judging success or failure first requires a criterion for what the system is supposed to do, and that criterion may itself remain unsettled; for example, [[examples/The Feudal System Can Be Called a Success or a Failure Depending on the Criterion|the feudal system can be called a success or a failure depending on the criterion]].
