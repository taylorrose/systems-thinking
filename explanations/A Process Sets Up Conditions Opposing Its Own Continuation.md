# A Process Sets Up Conditions Opposing Its Own Continuation

Le Chatelier’s Principle states that a natural process, whether physical or chemical, tends to set up conditions that oppose the further operation of that process. The opposition is not imposed from outside; it is produced by the process running.

Usually treated as a laboratory curiosity, the principle generalizes to systems, where it is better read as a cornerstone than as an oddity.
