# A Previous Success Becomes the Strategy Applied to the Next Challenge

Toynbee traced conspicuous malfunction after great success to a strong tendency to meet a new challenge with the strategy that answered the previous one. A response shaped by its success against one situation is carried forward even though the next situation is new.
