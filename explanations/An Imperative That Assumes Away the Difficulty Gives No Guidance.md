# An Imperative That Assumes Away the Difficulty Gives No Guidance

Instructing someone to make correct decisions restates the goal as the method. The entire difficulty lies in knowing which decision is correct, and an imperative that skips over that difficulty leaves the decider exactly where they were. Advice of this shape can be cited as a principle while offering nothing that can be carried out.
