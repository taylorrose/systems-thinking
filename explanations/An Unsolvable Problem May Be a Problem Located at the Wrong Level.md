# An Unsolvable Problem May Be a Problem Located at the Wrong Level

When a problem seems unsolvable, the difficulty may not lie in the solutions tried but in where the problem has been placed. Effort directed at the wrong logical level cannot reach what governs the problem, so locating it correctly becomes a prior problem about the problem.
