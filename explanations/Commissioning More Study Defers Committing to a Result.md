# Commissioning More Study Defers Committing to a Result

A study is a form of activity that produces no commitment. While a question remains under study, the system has not yet declared an answer, so nothing it has said can be held against it. Ordering another study is therefore a way of staying busy at the point where a conclusion would otherwise have to be adopted and defended.
