# Perfected Plans Can Arrive After Their Occasion Has Passed

Parkinson applies the principle to architecture: the completion of an elaborate building or plan can coincide with defeat, disgrace, or the loss of the purpose it was meant to serve. In these cases perfection is evidence about the system’s condition and timing, not proof of its present capability.
