# A Big System’s Functioning Does Not Follow From the Authority Over It

Whether a big system works is a property of the system itself, not a grant from whoever commands it. A system can be designed expressly as an instrument of one person’s will and still refuse to carry that will into effect, and the holder of complete authority over it has no further lever to pull. Systems have their own functional vagaries: they fail when most needed and work when one could do without them.
