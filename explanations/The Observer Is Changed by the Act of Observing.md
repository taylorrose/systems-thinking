# The Observer Is Changed by the Act of Observing

The probe is not a neutral instrument that returns unaffected from the system it examines. The observer leaves the encounter in a different condition than the one in which the observation began.

Alteration therefore runs in both directions. Whatever the probe was before, afterwards it is something that has been entered into the system and marked by it.
