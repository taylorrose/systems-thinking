# Controls Can Register What the Operator Wishes Instead of What Is

A control panel shows whatever its wiring makes it show. If an indicator is driven by the command that was issued rather than by the condition of the thing commanded, it reports the operator's intention back to the operator. The display is then a record of what the system was asked to do, presented in the place reserved for what the system is actually doing.
