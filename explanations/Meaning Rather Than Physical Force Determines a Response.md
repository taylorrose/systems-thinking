# Meaning Rather Than Physical Force Determines a Response

When the receiver of an act can interpret it, masses, energies, velocities, and vectors are not enough to account for what happens next. The response follows from the message the receiver takes the act to be, and it is the receiver, not the sender, who settles that meaning. Communication theory was invented to address the situations a physical account leaves unexplained.
