# Membership in an Infinity of Systems Leaves Nothing Uncorrelated

If any element already belongs to an infinity of other systems, then no two things can be assumed to stand apart from each other; some shared system links them. The claim is presented as the realization that follows once membership in many systems is granted, and it is unsettling enough to leave a newcomer reeling.
