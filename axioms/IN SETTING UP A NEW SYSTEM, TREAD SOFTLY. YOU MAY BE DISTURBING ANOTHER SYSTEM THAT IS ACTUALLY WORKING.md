[[explanations/Systems That Answer Each Other in Kind Escalate|Systems that answer each other in kind escalate]]; however, [[effects/A System’s Impact Returns Even When the Other System Does Not Retaliate|a system’s impact returns even when the other system does not retaliate]], for example, [[examples/Cutting Hospital Beds Shifted Greater Costs onto Other Agencies|cutting hospital beds shifted greater costs onto other agencies]].
