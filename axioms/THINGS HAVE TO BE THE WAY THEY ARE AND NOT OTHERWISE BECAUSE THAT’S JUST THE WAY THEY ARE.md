# THINGS HAVE TO BE THE WAY THEY ARE AND NOT OTHERWISE BECAUSE THAT’S JUST THE WAY THEY ARE.

[[explanations/Calling a Condition Inevitable Removes It From Question|Calling a condition inevitable removes it from question]]; for example, [[examples/Little Could Be Done About the Cold of the Ice Ages Until Someone Noticed It|little could be done about the cold of the Ice Ages until someone noticed it]], and [[examples/A Nation Called Its Own Deprivation Normalcy Until Roosevelt Named It|a nation called its own deprivation normalcy until Roosevelt named it]]. Consequently, [[effects/A Condition Held to Be Inevitable Attracts No Attempt to Change It|a condition held to be inevitable attracts no attempt to change it]]; therefore, [[practices/Test Whether a Condition Is Actually Unalterable Before Accepting It|test whether a condition is actually unalterable before accepting it]].
