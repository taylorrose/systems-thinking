# THINGS HAVE NEVER BEEN BETTER—BUT THEY’RE IMPROVING

[[explanations/Claims That Systems Are Improving Are Answered by the Record of Events|Claims that systems are improving are answered by the record of events]]; for example, [[examples/An Austerity Budget Produced the Largest Deficits in U.S. History|an austerity budget produced the largest deficits in U.S. history]], and [[examples/A Warship’s Computer Ignored the Missile That Sank It|a warship’s computer ignored the missile that sank it]]. Therefore, [[effects/Systemantics Probably Has Nothing to Offer Readers Who Still Read the Record as Improvement|Systemantics probably has nothing to offer readers who still read the record as improvement]]. However, [[examples/Protesting by Refusing to Break the Rules Shows Systemantics Taking Hold|protesting by refusing to break the rules shows Systemantics taking hold]]; in either case, [[practices/Survey Actual Events Before Accepting a Claim of Improvement|survey actual events before accepting a claim of improvement]].
