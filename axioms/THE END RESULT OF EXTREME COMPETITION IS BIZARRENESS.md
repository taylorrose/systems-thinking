# THE END RESULT OF EXTREME COMPETITION IS BIZARRENESS

[[explanations/Extreme Competition Selects Traits in Their Extreme Form|Extreme competition selects traits in their extreme form]]. Moreover, [[effects/Competition Selects a Capacity to Endure the Competition Itself|competition selects a capacity to endure the competition itself]]. However, [[effects/Traits Selected Under Competition Can Fail When the Competition Ends|traits selected under competition can fail when the competition ends]]; for example, [[examples/Elected Presidents May Keep Campaigning After Winning|elected presidents may keep campaigning after winning]]. Therefore, [[practices/Ask Which Conditions a Person’s Winning Traits Were Selected For|ask which conditions a person’s winning traits were selected for]].
