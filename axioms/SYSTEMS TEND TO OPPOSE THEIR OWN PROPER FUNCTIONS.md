# SYSTEMS TEND TO OPPOSE THEIR OWN PROPER FUNCTIONS

[[explanations/A Written Statement of Goals Turns Open Work into a Scored Program|A written statement of goals turns open work into a scored program]], and [[explanations/Once Goals Are Fixed, Others Can Deduce and Govern How the Work Is Done|once goals are fixed, others can deduce and govern how the work is done]]. For example, [[examples/Trillium Abandons His Research to Write His Goals and Objectives|Trillium abandons his research to write his goals and objectives]], and later [[examples/A Legislature Fixes a Professor’s Hours from His Own Stated Goals|a legislature fixes a professor’s hours from his own stated goals]]. Consequently, [[effects/A System Built to Improve Performance Can Reduce the Work It Measures|a system built to improve performance can reduce the work it measures]], while [[effects/The Measuring Role Can Outgrow the Work It Was Created to Serve|the measuring role can outgrow the work it was created to serve]]; therefore, [[practices/Ask Who Will Measure You Against Goals Before You State Them|ask who will measure you against goals before you state them]].
