# TOGETHERNESS IS GREAT, BUT DON’T KNOCK GET-AWAY-NESS

[[explanations/Too Much Feedback Overwhelms the Channels That Must Respond to It|Too much feedback overwhelms the channels that must respond to it]]; for example, [[examples/Governments Falter When the Privy Council Grows Past Eight Members|governments falter when the privy council grows past eight members]]. Consequently, [[effects/A System Required to Accommodate All Incoming Data Never Reaches Equilibrium|a system required to accommodate all incoming data never reaches equilibrium]], whereas [[explanations/Distance Between Members Limits How Far a Disturbance Spreads|distance between members limits how far a disturbance spreads]]; therefore, [[practices/Preserve Room for Members to Withdraw From the Group’s Feedback|preserve room for members to withdraw from the group’s feedback]].
