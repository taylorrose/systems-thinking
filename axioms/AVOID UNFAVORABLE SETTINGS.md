# AVOID UNFAVORABLE SETTINGS

[[explanations/Rapidly and Irregularly Fluctuating Conditions May Defeat a System|Rapidly and irregularly fluctuating conditions may defeat a system]], although occasionally [[explanations/Survival in an Unfavorable Setting Appears to Rest on Paradoxical or Highly Specialized Features|survival in an unfavorable setting appears to rest on paradoxical or highly specialized features]]; for example, [[examples/Running the Colorado Rapids Required Rowing Backwards or Damping the Waves|running the Colorado rapids required rowing backwards or damping the waves]]. Therefore, [[practices/Steer Clear of Settings a System Cannot Handle|steer clear of settings a system cannot handle]], and [[practices/Identify the Special Feature Before Copying a System That Survives a Hostile Setting|identify the special feature before copying a system that survives a hostile setting]].
