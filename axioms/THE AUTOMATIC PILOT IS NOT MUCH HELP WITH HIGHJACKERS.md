# THE AUTOMATIC PILOT IS NOT MUCH HELP WITH HIGHJACKERS

[[explanations/No Reliable Method Exists for Devising a New Frame|No reliable method exists for devising a new frame]], and [[explanations/The Old System May Resist a New Frame Even When It Is Superior|the old system may resist a new frame even when it is superior]]; for example, [[examples/Talleyrand Could Reframe the Congress of Vienna but Not Napoleon|Talleyrand could reframe the Congress of Vienna but not Napoleon]]. Moreover, [[explanations/Reframing Is Difficult to Aim and Probably Impossible to Stop|reframing is difficult to aim and probably impossible to stop]], because [[explanations/Reframing Is Itself a System Subject to the Same Laws|reframing is itself a system subject to the same laws]]; for instance, [[examples/The Sorcerer’s Apprentice Cannot Stop the Powers He Sets in Motion|the sorcerer’s apprentice cannot stop the powers he sets in motion]]. Consequently, [[effects/A Reframing That Exploits Provokes a Destructive Kickback|a reframing that exploits provokes a destructive kickback]]; therefore, [[practices/Propose Only Reframings That Benefit Every Party|propose only reframings that benefit every party]], within which limits [[effects/Wisely Used Reframing Opens Areas Once Considered Unapproachable|wisely used reframing opens areas once considered unapproachable]].
