# IF DETROIT MAKES IT, IT MUST BE AN AUTOMOBILE

[[explanations/A System Looks Successful When Our Wants Have Adjusted to Its Output|A system looks successful when our wants have adjusted to its output]], because [[explanations/A Familiar Product Name Can Conceal What the System Actually Delivers|a familiar product name can conceal what the system actually delivers]]. For example, [[examples/American White Bread Would Not Be Recognized as Bread in Most of the World|American white bread would not be recognized as bread in most of the world]]. Likewise, [[examples/Leadership Training Produces Following Rather Than Leadership|leadership training produces following rather than leadership]], [[examples/Scouting Popularizes Scouting Rather Than Wilderness Camping|scouting popularizes scouting rather than wilderness camping]], and [[examples/Peace-Keeping Schemes Include Armies|peace-keeping schemes include armies]]. Therefore, [[practices/State What You Originally Wanted Before Judging What a System Delivers|state what you originally wanted before judging what a system delivers]].
