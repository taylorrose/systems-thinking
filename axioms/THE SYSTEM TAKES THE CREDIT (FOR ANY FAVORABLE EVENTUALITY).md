# THE SYSTEM TAKES THE CREDIT (FOR ANY FAVORABLE EVENTUALITY)

Consequently, [[effects/Crediting the System Obscures What Actually Produced the Outcome|crediting the system obscures what actually produced the outcome]]; therefore, [[practices/Trace an Outcome to Its Causes Before Crediting the System|trace an outcome to its causes before crediting the system]].
