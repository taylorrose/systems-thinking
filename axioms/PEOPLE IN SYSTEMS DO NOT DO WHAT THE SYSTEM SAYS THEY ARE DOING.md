# PEOPLE IN SYSTEMS DO NOT DO WHAT THE SYSTEM SAYS THEY ARE DOING

[[explanations/The Larger the System, the Less a Function Resembles Its Name|The larger the system, the less a function resembles its name]]; for example, [[examples/At a Great Shipyard the System Builds the Ship and No One Person Does|at a great shipyard the system builds the ship and no one person does]]. The same gap appears when [[examples/An Admiral’s Ship Turns Out to Be a Legal Apprenticeship|an admiral’s ship turns out to be a legal apprenticeship]], when [[examples/Kings Spend Much of Their Rule Defending Their Position|kings spend much of their rule defending their position]], and when [[examples/University Scholars Work on Assigned and Fundable Problems|university scholars work on assigned and fundable problems]].
