# WHEN BIG SYSTEMS FAIL, THE FAILURE IS OFTEN BIG

[[explanations/Big Systems Concentrate Catastrophic Potential|Big systems concentrate catastrophic potential]], although [[explanations/Grandiose Goals Make an Obvious Risk Easy to Forget|grandiose goals make an obvious risk easy to forget]]. For example, [[examples/A Nuclear Plant Puts a Region’s Wealth in One Place|a nuclear plant puts a region’s wealth in one place]], and [[examples/One Hospital Can Consume an Entire Health Budget|one hospital can consume an entire health budget]]. Consequently, [[effects/Concentrating Resources Leaves Little to Meet Ongoing Needs|concentrating resources leaves little to meet ongoing needs]]; therefore, [[practices/Ask What Else Fails Before Concentrating Resources in One System|ask what else fails before concentrating resources in one system]].
