# BIG SYSTEMS EITHER WORK ON THEIR OWN OR THEY DON’T. IF THEY DON’T, YOU CAN’T MAKE THEM

[[explanations/A Big System’s Functioning Does Not Follow From the Authority Over It|A big system’s functioning does not follow from the authority over it]]; for example, [[examples/A Czar With Absolute Power Could Not Make His Own Bureaucracy Work|a czar with absolute power could not make his own bureaucracy work]], and [[examples/Telephone Systems Either Work or Resist Every Effort to Make Them Work|telephone systems either work or resist every effort to make them work]]. Therefore, [[practices/Determine Whether a Big System Already Works Before Committing to Make It Work|determine whether a big system already works before committing to make it work]].
