# IF YOUR PROBLEM SEEMS UNSOLVABLE, CONSIDER THAT YOU MAY HAVE A META-PROBLEM

[[explanations/An Unsolvable Problem May Be a Problem Located at the Wrong Level|An unsolvable problem may be a problem located at the wrong level]], and [[explanations/A Stuck Problem May Lie in the Probe, the Solution, or the Question|a stuck problem may lie in the probe, the solution, or the question]]. Consequently, [[effects/A Problem Remains Intractable Until Its Meta-Problem Is Solved|a problem remains intractable until its meta-problem is solved]]; therefore, [[practices/Locate the Problem Before Trying to Solve It|locate the problem before trying to solve it]].
