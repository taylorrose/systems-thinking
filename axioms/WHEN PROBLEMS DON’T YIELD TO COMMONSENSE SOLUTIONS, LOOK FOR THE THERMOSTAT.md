# WHEN PROBLEMS DON’T YIELD TO COMMONSENSE SOLUTIONS, LOOK FOR THE THERMOSTAT

[[explanations/A Thermostat Is Where Self-Reference Is Designed Into a System|A thermostat is where self-reference is designed into a system]]; consequently, [[effects/Commonsense Remedies Can Generate Escalating Counterproblems|commonsense remedies can generate escalating counterproblems]]. For example, [[examples/More Food Lets the Population Grow Until People Starve Again|more food lets the population grow until people starve again]], and [[examples/Expanding Transportation Pushes the Suburbs Farther Away|expanding transportation pushes the suburbs farther away]]. Therefore, [[practices/Disengage the Linkage That Holds the Unwanted Condition Steady|disengage the linkage that holds the unwanted condition steady]]; however, [[explanations/Control Is Often Distributed Across a System Rather Than Located in One Unit|control is often distributed across a system rather than located in one unit]].
