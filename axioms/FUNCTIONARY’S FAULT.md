# FUNCTIONARY’S FAULT

[[explanations/Strange Behavior in Systems Is Not Primarily Incompetence|Strange behavior in systems is not primarily incompetence]], because [[explanations/Immersion in a System Deprives Its People of Contrasting Experience|immersion in a system deprives its people of contrasting experience]]. Among its subtypes, [[explanations/Functionary’s Pride Is a Mania of Self-Esteem Produced by Office|functionary’s pride is a mania of self-esteem produced by office]], whereas [[explanations/Hireling’s Hypnosis Suspends Normal Mental Activity in Members of a System|hireling’s hypnosis suspends normal mental activity in members of a system]]. Therefore, [[practices/Treat Bizarre System Behavior as a Symptom of Immersion Rather Than Incompetence|treat bizarre system behavior as a symptom of immersion rather than incompetence]].
