# A SIMPLE SYSTEM MAY OR MAY NOT WORK

[[explanations/Simple Systems That Work Are Rare and Valuable|Simple systems that work are rare and valuable]]; however, [[explanations/A Simple System’s Stability Can Lie in Its Design or in Its Operator|a simple system’s stability can lie in its design or in its operator]]. For example, [[examples/A Fishing Pole Works Only in Practiced Hands|a fishing pole works only in practiced hands]], whereas [[examples/A Foot-Rule Works the Same in Any Hands|a foot-rule works the same in any hands]]. Consequently, [[effects/The Same Simple System Can Work for One Operator and Fail for Another|the same simple system can work for one operator and fail for another]]; therefore, [[practices/Ask Whether a Simple System Works on Its Own or Only in Skilled Hands|ask whether a simple system works on its own or only in skilled hands]].
