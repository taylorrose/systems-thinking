# IN ORDER TO REMAIN UNCHANGED, THE SYSTEM MUST CHANGE

[[explanations/Stability Won Under One Set of Rules Does Not Survive a Rule Change|Stability won under one set of rules does not survive a rule change]], so [[effects/Responses That Once Saved the System Make Things Worse After the Rules Change|responses that once saved the system make things worse after the rules change]]. Consequently, [[explanations/Ultra-stability Requires Changing the Strategies That Preserved Stability|ultra-stability requires changing the strategies that preserved stability]]; for example, [[examples/A Pilot Survived by Pushing the Stick Forward When His Controls Reversed|a pilot survived by pushing the stick forward when his controls reversed]], and [[examples/Changing Its Goal Kept the Polio Foundation in Operation|changing its goal kept the polio foundation in operation]]. Therefore, [[practices/Treat a Failing Familiar Response as a Sign the Rules Have Changed|treat a failing familiar response as a sign the rules have changed]], and [[practices/Choose a Goal Unlikely to Go Out of Date|choose a goal unlikely to go out of date]].
