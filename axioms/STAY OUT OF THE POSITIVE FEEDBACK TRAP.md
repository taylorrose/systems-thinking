# STAY OUT OF THE POSITIVE FEEDBACK TRAP

[[explanations/Relief That Rebounds Worse Than the Original Trouble Is a Positive Feedback Trap|Relief that rebounds worse than the original trouble is a positive feedback trap]]; for example, [[examples/Nasal Spray Relieves Stuffiness and Returns It Worse|nasal spray relieves stuffiness and returns it worse]]. Therefore, [[practices/Stop Applying a Remedy Whose Relief Rebounds Worse|stop applying a remedy whose relief rebounds worse]].
