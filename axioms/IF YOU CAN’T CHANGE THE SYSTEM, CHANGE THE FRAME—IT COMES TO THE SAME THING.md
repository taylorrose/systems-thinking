# IF YOU CAN’T CHANGE THE SYSTEM, CHANGE THE FRAME—IT COMES TO THE SAME THING

[[explanations/Creative Reframing Substitutes Useful Metaphors for Limiting Ones|Creative reframing substitutes useful metaphors for limiting ones]]; for example, [[examples/Talleyrand Reframed France from Aggressor to Victim of Napoleon|Talleyrand reframed France from aggressor to victim of Napoleon]], and [[examples/Bismarck Accepted Social Benefits After Civil Servants Were Reframed as a Standing Army|Bismarck accepted social benefits after civil servants were reframed as a standing army]]. Consequently, [[effects/A Successful Reframing Can Make the Problem Cease to Exist|a successful reframing can make the problem cease to exist]]; therefore, [[practices/Treat Limiting Labels as Terminology and Seek a More Appropriate Model|treat limiting labels as terminology and seek a more appropriate model]]. However, [[explanations/Successful Reframings Are the Exception, Not the Rule|successful reframings are the exception, not the rule]], because [[explanations/No Reliable Method Exists for Devising a New Frame|no reliable method exists for devising a new frame]]. Therefore, [[practices/Resist Treating Every Problem as One That Needs Reframing|resist treating every problem as one that needs reframing]].
