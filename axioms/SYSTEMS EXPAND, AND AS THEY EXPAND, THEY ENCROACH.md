# SYSTEMS EXPAND, AND AS THEY EXPAND, THEY ENCROACH.

[[explanations/Encroachment Means Making Someone Else Do the System’s Work|Encroachment means making someone else do the system’s work]]; for example, [[examples/The Do-It-Yourself Movement Returns Factory Work to the Buyer|the do-it-yourself movement returns factory work to the buyer]], and [[examples/Taxpayers Compute the Taxes the Revenue Service Collects|taxpayers compute the taxes the Revenue Service collects]]. Consequently, [[effects/A System Set Up to Protect People Can Encroach Until It Burdens Them|a system set up to protect people can encroach until it burdens them]], while [[effects/Encroachment Spreads Into Areas It Has Not Yet Reached|encroachment spreads into areas it has not yet reached]], as [[examples/Self-Service Stations and Supermarkets Show Encroachment Spreading|self-service stations and supermarkets show encroachment spreading]]. Moreover, [[effects/Systems Can Shift the Consequences of Failure onto the People They Serve|systems can shift the consequences of failure onto the people they serve]], and [[examples/The Telephone Company Charges for What It Once Provided|the Telephone Company charges for what it once provided]]. Therefore, [[practices/Ask Who Is Doing the Work the System Was Built to Do|ask who is doing the work the system was built to do]].
