# SYSTEMS OPERATE BY REDISTRIBUTING ANERGY INTO DIFFERENT FORMS AND INTO ACCUMULATIONS OF DIFFERENT SIZES

[[effects/Systems Can Redistribute Problems Without Reducing Them|Systems can redistribute problems without reducing them]]; however, [[explanations/Very Large Systems May Increase Total Anergy Rather Than Redistribute It|very large systems may increase total anergy rather than redistribute it]], for example, [[examples/Metropolitan Garbage Collection Leaves the Garbage and Adds New Emergencies|metropolitan garbage collection leaves the garbage and adds new emergencies]]. Consequently, [[effects/The Original Problem Can Persist While New Problems Accumulate|the original problem can persist while new problems accumulate]], and [[effects/Problems That Would Have Occurred Anyway Become Much Worse With the System|problems that would have occurred anyway become much worse with the system]]; therefore, [[practices/Check Whether a Large System Redistributed the Effort or Added to It|check whether a large system redistributed the effort or added to it]].
