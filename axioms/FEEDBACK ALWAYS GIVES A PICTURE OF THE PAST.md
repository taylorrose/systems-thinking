# FEEDBACK ALWAYS GIVES A PICTURE OF THE PAST

[[explanations/Feedback Reports Only What Has Already Happened|Feedback reports only what has already happened]]; consequently, [[effects/Using Feedback to Predict the Future Requires Faith Rather Than Evidence|using feedback to predict the future requires faith rather than evidence]]. Therefore, [[practices/Treat Feedback as a Report on the Past Rather Than a Forecast|treat feedback as a report on the past rather than a forecast]].
