# THE MOST URGENTLY NEEDED INFORMATION DECAYS FASTEST

[[explanations/Urgency Shortens the Useful Half-Life of Information|Urgency shortens the useful half-life of information]]; for example, [[examples/A Gold Quotation Can Be Useless Within Thirty Seconds|a gold quotation can be useless within thirty seconds]], and [[examples/Satellite Coordinates May Need Tracking at Millisecond Intervals|satellite coordinates may need tracking at millisecond intervals]]. Consequently, [[effects/Critical Information Can Expire Before Anyone Acts on It|critical information can expire before anyone acts on it]]; therefore, [[practices/Match How Often You Refresh Information to How Fast It Decays|match how often you refresh information to how fast it decays]].
