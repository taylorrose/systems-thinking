# A SYSTEM THAT IGNORES FEEDBACK HAS ALREADY BEGUN THE PROCESS OF TERMINAL INSTABILITY

[[explanations/Under-Designing for Feedback Breeds the Delusion That Feedback Is Hostile|Under-designing for feedback breeds the delusion that feedback is hostile]]; for example, [[examples/Killing the Bearer of Bad News Is a Poor Way to Handle Unwelcome Information|killing the bearer of bad news is a poor way to handle unwelcome information]]. However, [[explanations/Refusing to Respond to Feedback Is a Passive Demand That the Environment Adjust|refusing to respond to feedback is a passive demand that the environment adjust]], and [[explanations/The Cost of Ignoring Feedback Is a Cybernetic Law, Not an Opinion|the cost of ignoring feedback is a cybernetic law, not an opinion]]. Consequently, [[effects/A System That Ignores Its Environment Is Eventually Shaken to Pieces by It|a system that ignores its environment is eventually shaken to pieces by it]]; therefore, [[practices/Respond to Unwelcome Feedback Even When It Demands Initiative|respond to unwelcome feedback even when it demands initiative]].
