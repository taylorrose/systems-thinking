# PUSHING ON THE SYSTEM DOESN’T HELP

[[explanations/Anxiety Drives Administrators to Push Harder on a System That Is Not Working|Anxiety drives administrators to push harder on a system that is not working]], but [[examples/Pounding the Call Button Does Not Bring the Elevator Sooner|pounding the call button does not bring the elevator sooner]]. Consequently, [[effects/A Pushed System Usually Gives One Last Spasm and Then Stops Entirely|a pushed system usually gives one last spasm and then stops entirely]], and yet [[explanations/Denial Rather Than Ignorance Keeps the Futility of Pushing Unappreciated|denial rather than ignorance keeps the futility of pushing unappreciated]]. Therefore, [[practices/Stop Pushing and Diagnose Whether the System Can Work at All|stop pushing and diagnose whether the system can work at all]].
