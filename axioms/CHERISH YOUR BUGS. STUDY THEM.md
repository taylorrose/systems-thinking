# CHERISH YOUR BUGS. STUDY THEM

Although [[explanations/The Word Bug Makes Its Information Look Worthless|the word “bug” makes its information look worthless]], [[explanations/Every Bug Reports One More Way the System Can Fail|every bug reports one more way the system can fail]]. Consequently, [[effects/Each Bug Found Advances the Work of Avoiding the Likeliest Failures|each bug found advances the work of avoiding the likeliest failures]]; therefore, [[practices/Record What Each Bug Tells You About How the System Can Fail|record what each bug tells you about how the system can fail]].
