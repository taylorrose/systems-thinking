# Look for the Working Simple System a Complex System Grew From

When a complex system is working, trace its history back and identify the simple system it developed out of. Ask what that earlier system did, whether it worked on its own, and which of its functions the present arrangement still rests on.

Where no such predecessor can be found, treat the account of the system’s origin as incomplete rather than as an exception.
