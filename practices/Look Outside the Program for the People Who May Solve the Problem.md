# Look Outside the Program for the People Who May Solve the Problem

When a problem has a large program attached to it, do not treat that program's roster as the population of possible solvers. Ask who is working on the problem for interest rather than assignment, in a neighboring field, on a small team, or with credentials no selection committee would accept.

Keep such work reachable and unencumbered: leave routes by which an unqualified solution can be heard, and avoid committing every competent worker in the field to making the existing program succeed.
