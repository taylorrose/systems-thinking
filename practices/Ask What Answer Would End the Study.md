# Ask What Answer Would End the Study

Before joining or accepting another round of study, ask which findings would be treated as settling the question and which would trigger a further round. If only one range of answers can end the inquiry, the inquiry is selecting a conclusion rather than testing one.

Then count what has already been produced: how many times the question has been examined, what each round found, and which of those findings were carried forward. A record of repeated examination with no retained conclusion is worth reading as deferral, and the earlier findings are worth recovering before the next round begins.
