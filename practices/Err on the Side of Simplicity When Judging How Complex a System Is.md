# Err on the Side of Simplicity When Judging How Complex a System Is

When estimating how complex a system already is, take the higher estimate rather than the lower one. As a working rule of thumb, treat any system with more than two elements as complex, at least for purposes of human interaction.

Do not assume that a system only slightly larger than trivial is still fully within view; plan for its evaluation being harder than it looks rather than easier.
