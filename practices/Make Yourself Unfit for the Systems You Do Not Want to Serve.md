# Make Yourself Unfit for the Systems You Do Not Want to Serve

Name the involvements you do not want, then ask what makes you eligible for them: a skill, a location, a credential, a reputation for being available. Consider declining to acquire or maintain that eligibility, rather than acquiring it and then arguing your way out each time it is called upon.

Apply this to what you are able to be asked for, not to work you have already undertaken. Whether the same technique could be practiced on a wider scale, against the grandiose enterprises of leaders generally, the author leaves open.
