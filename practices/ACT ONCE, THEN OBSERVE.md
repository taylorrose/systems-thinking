# ACT ONCE, THEN OBSERVE

Make one bounded change, timestamp it, and record the state immediately before and after. Expect [[axioms/THE SYSTEM IS ALTERED BY THE PROBE USED TO TEST IT|the intervention to alter the system]], [[axioms/THE PROBE IS ALTERED ALSO|the system to alter the probe]], and [[axioms/NO OBSERVATION WITHOUT ITS EFFECTS|observation to have effects]]. Preserve anomalies by choosing to [[axioms/CHERISH YOUR EXCEPTIONS|keep exceptions]] and [[axioms/CHERISH YOUR BUGS. STUDY THEM|study unexpected behavior]].

Observe behavior rather than the return of a signal. [[axioms/JUST CALLING IT FEEDBACK DOESN’T MEAN THAT IT HAS ACTUALLY FED BACK|A returned signal is not enough]]; [[axioms/IT HASN’T FED BACK UNTIL THE SYSTEM CHANGES COURSE|feedback changes course]]. Shorten delay because [[axioms/NATURE IS ONLY WISE WHEN FEEDBACKS ARE RAPID|rapid feedback supports correction]], while remembering [[axioms/FEEDBACK ALWAYS GIVES A PICTURE OF THE PAST|every signal is historical]]. Prevent [[axioms/OUTPUT IS DANGEROUS|output suppression]] and the impulse to [[axioms/KEEP IT IN THE STUDY PHASE|substitute study for action]].

Read the response as system behavior. Expect [[axioms/THE SYSTEM ALWAYS KICKS BACK|kickback]], [[axioms/SYSTEMS GET IN THE WAY|structural obstruction]], and [[axioms/SYSTEMS TEND TO OPPOSE THEIR OWN PROPER FUNCTIONS|opposition to stated function]]. If [[axioms/WHEN EVERYTHING CORRELATES WITH EVERYTHING ELSE, THINGS WILL NEVER SETTLE DOWN|coupling prevents settling]], create [[axioms/TOGETHERNESS IS GREAT, BUT DON’T KNOCK GET-AWAY-NESS|useful separation]].

Interpret direction without pretending to predict. Use [[axioms/IN DEALING WITH THE SHAPE OF THINGS TO COME, IT PAYS TO BE GOOD AT RECOGNIZING SHAPES|emerging shapes]] and [[axioms/THE FUTURE IS NO MORE PREDICTABLE NOW THAN IT WAS IN THE PAST, BUT YOU CAN AT LEAST TAKE NOTE OF TRENDS|trends without certainty]], not the assumption that [[axioms/THE FUTURE WILL BE LIKE THE PAST, BECAUSE, IN THE PAST, THE FUTURE WAS LIKE THE PAST|the future must repeat]]. Include agency because [[axioms/WHEN THE SYSTEM ACTS, IT PARTICIPATES IN THE CREATION OF THE FUTURE|system action creates the future]] and [[axioms/THE FUTURE IS PARTLY DETERMINED BY WHAT WE DO NOW|the intervention shapes what follows]].

Branch on evidence. If the desired effect improves without intolerable displacement, repeat the test before scaling. If evidence is mixed, wait only for a named delay or improve sensing. If [[axioms/IF THINGS SEEM TO BE GETTING WORSE EVEN FASTER THAN USUAL, CONSIDER THAT THE REMEDY MAY BE AT FAULT|the remedy worsens the result]], reverse it; [[axioms/ESCALATING THE WRONG SOLUTION DOES NOT IMPROVE THE OUTCOME|do not add force]]. If [[axioms/IF SOMETHING ISN’T WORKING, DON’T KEEP DOING IT. DO SOMETHING ELSE INSTEAD|the action keeps failing]], stop and reframe, treating [[axioms/ERROR CORRECTION IS WHAT WE DO|correction as the work]]. Recheck whether [[axioms/THE OLD SYSTEM IS NOW THE NEW PROBLEM|the old control is now the problem]].

Act before instability compounds. [[axioms/A SYSTEM THAT IGNORES FEEDBACK HAS ALREADY BEGUN THE PROCESS OF TERMINAL INSTABILITY|Ignored feedback begins terminal instability]]; [[axioms/TOTAL SYSTEMS TEND TO RUN AWAY (GO OUT OF CONTROL)|total systems run away]], and [[axioms/A TOTAL SYSTEM THAT GOES INTO A RUNAWAY SEQUENCE MAY BE FORCED TO GROW OR TO DISINTEGRATE IN CHAOS|runaway forces growth or chaos]]. Stop, contain, or reverse at the first defined trigger, then return to discovery with the system that now exists.
