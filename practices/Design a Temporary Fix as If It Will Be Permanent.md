# Design a Temporary Fix as If It Will Be Permanent

Before installing a stopgap, assume it will still be in place long after the occasion for it has gone. Ask what it will cost to live with for years: what it occupies, what it obstructs, what will come to depend on it, and who would have to act to remove it.

Where nothing and no one is assigned to end it, treat the fix as an addition to the permanent landscape and choose it on those terms, or name in advance the condition and the party that will retire it.
