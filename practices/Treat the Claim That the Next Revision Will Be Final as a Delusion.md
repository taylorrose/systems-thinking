# Treat the Claim That the Next Revision Will Be Final as a Delusion

When a plan promises that one more revision, or one more day past the deadline, will leave the system perfect, treat that promise as a delusion rather than as a schedule. Ask what became of the previous promise of finality, what evidence distinguishes this one from it, and whether the appeal of the claim rests on how neatly it is phrased.

Then plan for a system that will still need work afterward: leave capacity for the revision after this one, and judge the current effort by the improvement it delivers rather than by the finality it announces.
