# DISCOVER THE ACTUAL SYSTEM

Draw a provisional boundary and expect [[axioms/REALITY IS MORE COMPLEX THAN IT SEEMS|more relations than the model]]. A machine may still [[axioms/A MACHINE ACTS LIKE A SYSTEM|behave systemically]]; [[axioms/THE UNIVERSE IS NOT LIKE A MACHINE|the environment is not mechanical]], yet [[axioms/THE UNIVERSE IS LIKE A VERY LARGE SYSTEM|the environment belongs in the boundary]]. Do not scale conclusions: [[axioms/A LARGE SYSTEM, PRODUCED BY EXPANDING THE DIMENSIONS OF A SMALLER SYSTEM, DOES NOT BEHAVE LIKE THE SMALLER SYSTEM|size changes behavior]]. Accept [[axioms/LARGE COMPLEX SYSTEMS ARE BEYOND HUMAN CAPACITY TO EVALUATE|limits to complete evaluation]], [[axioms/COMPLEX SYSTEMS HAVE COMPLEX BEHAVIORS|complex response]], and [[axioms/COMPLEX SYSTEMS EXHIBIT UNEXPECTED BEHAVIOR|surprise]]. Allow for [[axioms/A SYSTEM CAN FAIL IN AN INFINITE NUMBER OF WAYS|unbounded failure modes]], [[axioms/IN COMPLEX SYSTEMS, MALFUNCTION AND EVEN TOTAL NON-FUNCTION MAY NOT BE DETECTABLE FOR LONG PERIODS, IF EVER|hidden nonfunction]], [[axioms/NO OBSERVATION WITHOUT ITS EFFECTS|observer effects]], and [[axioms/EVERYTHING CORRELATES|correlation without simple causation]].

Observe operations. Compare [[axioms/PEOPLE IN SYSTEMS DO NOT DO WHAT THE SYSTEM SAYS THEY ARE DOING|assigned work with actual work]] and [[axioms/THE SYSTEM ITSELF DOES NOT DO WHAT IT SAYS IT IS DOING|declared function with actual function]]. Infer purpose through [[axioms/THE FUNCTION (OR PRODUCT) IS DEFINED BY THE SYSTEMS-OPERATIONS THAT OCCUR IN ITS PERFORMANCE OR MANUFACTURE|the operations producing it]], not origin claims such as [[axioms/IF DETROIT MAKES IT, IT MUST BE AN AUTOMOBILE|maker defines product]]. Since [[axioms/THE NAME IS MOST EMPHATICALLY NOT THE THING|names are not things]], let [[axioms/THE SYSTEM IS ITS OWN BEST EXPLANATION|repeated behavior explain the system]].

Audit representations. Detect when [[axioms/THINGS ARE WHAT THEY ARE REPORTED TO BE|reports replace events]], [[axioms/THE REAL WORLD IS WHAT IS REPORTED TO THE SYSTEM|reports become internal reality]], [[axioms/IF IT ISN’T OFFICIAL, IT HASN’T HAPPENED|unofficial events disappear]], [[axioms/IF IT DIDN’T HAPPEN ON CAMERA, IT DIDN’T HAPPEN|unrecorded events disappear]], or [[axioms/IF THE SYSTEM SAYS IT HAPPENED, IT HAPPENED|declarations create facts]]. Keep [[axioms/THE CHART IS NOT THE PATIENT|records below subjects]], [[axioms/THE DOSSIER IS NOT THE PERSON|files below people]], and question [[axioms/A SANE MIND, A SOUND BODY, AND A HEALTHY CHART|healthy-looking records]].

Inspect sensing. [[axioms/A SYSTEM IS NO BETTER THAN ITS SENSORY ORGANS|Sensing limits knowledge]]; [[axioms/TO THOSE WITHIN A SYSTEM, THE OUTSIDE REALITY TENDS TO PALE AND DISAPPEAR|insiders lose outside reality]], [[axioms/THE BIGGER THE SYSTEM, THE NARROWER AND MORE SPECIALIZED THE INTERFACE WITH INDIVIDUALS|scale narrows interfaces]], and [[axioms/FUNCTIONARY’S FAULT|immersion narrows perception]]. Separate [[axioms/THE INFORMATION YOU HAVE IS NOT THE INFORMATION YOU WANT|what you have from what you want]], [[axioms/THE INFORMATION YOU WANT IS NOT THE INFORMATION YOU NEED|what you want from what you need]], and [[axioms/THE INFORMATION YOU NEED IS NOT THE INFORMATION YOU CAN OBTAIN|what you need from what is obtainable]]; sometimes [[axioms/DON’T BOTHER TO LOOK FOR IT. YOU WON’T FIND IT|it is unavailable]]. Date evidence because [[axioms/INFORMATION DECAYS|information decays]], [[axioms/KNOWLEDGE DOES NOT KEEP ANY BETTER THAN FISH|knowledge spoils]], and [[axioms/THE MOST URGENTLY NEEDED INFORMATION DECAYS FASTEST|urgent evidence decays fastest]]. Reopen channels when [[axioms/IN A CLOSED SYSTEM, INFORMATION TENDS TO DECREASE AND HALLUCINATION TENDS TO INCREASE|closure produces hallucination]].

Test communication through effects. [[axioms/THE MESSAGE SENT IS NOT NECESSARILY THE MESSAGE RECEIVED|Reception differs from sending]], [[axioms/THE MEANING OF A COMMUNICATION IS THE BEHAVIOR THAT RESULTS|meaning is resulting behavior]], and [[axioms/EVERY PICTURE TELLS A STORY—BUT NOT THE SAME STORY|representations yield different stories]]. [[axioms/EXPERIENCE ISN’T HEREDITARY—IT AIN’T EVEN CONTAGIOUS|Experience does not transfer automatically]]; [[axioms/ONE SYSTEM’S GARBAGE IS ANOTHER SYSTEM’S PRECIOUS RAW MATERIAL|value depends on the receiver]], and [[axioms/EXTRA BRAIN IN TAIL, TAIL WAGS ON OWN SCHEDULE|remote parts keep their own schedule]]. Ask [[axioms/KNOWLEDGE FOR WHAT|what behavior knowledge should change]]. Remember [[axioms/FEEDBACK ALWAYS GIVES A PICTURE OF THE PAST|feedback is historical]] and [[axioms/IT HASN’T FED BACK UNTIL THE SYSTEM CHANGES COURSE|requires changed course]].

Infer operative goals. Expect [[axioms/SYSTEMS DEVELOP GOALS OF THEIR OWN THE INSTANT THEY COME INTO BEING|emergent goals]], [[axioms/INTRASYSTEM GOALS COME FIRST|internal priority]], [[axioms/SYSTEMS DON’T WORK FOR YOU OR FOR ME. THEY WORK FOR THEIR OWN GOALS|goal autonomy]], [[axioms/THE SYSTEM BEHAVES AS IF IT HAS A WILL TO LIVE|self-preservation]], and [[axioms/THE SYSTEM IS A LAW UNTO ITSELF|internal law]]. Selection matters: [[axioms/SYSTEMS ATTRACT SYSTEMS-PEOPLE|systems attract compatible people]], [[axioms/SPECIALIZED SYSTEMS SELECT FOR SPECIALIZATION|specialization selects specialists]], [[axioms/THE END RESULT OF EXTREME COMPETITION IS BIZARRENESS|extreme competition distorts]], and [[axioms/PROLONGED SELECTION SELECTS SURVIVORS|selection proves survival]]. Locate control where [[axioms/CONTROL IS EXERCISED BY THE ELEMENT WITH THE GREATEST VARIETY OF BEHAVIORAL RESPONSES|response variety is greatest]]. Assume [[axioms/IF A SYSTEM CAN BE EXPLOITED, IT WILL BE|exploitable paths will be used]] because [[axioms/ANY SYSTEM CAN BE EXPLOITED|every system is exploitable]], then [[axioms/UTILIZE THE PRINCIPLE OF UTILIZATION|work from demonstrated capabilities]].

Finally, separate success from attribution. Challenge [[axioms/THE SYSTEM TAKES THE CREDIT (FOR ANY FAVORABLE EVENTUALITY)|claimed credit]], [[axioms/WHAT’S GOOD FOR GENERAL SYSTEMANTICS IS GOOD FOR THE COUNTRY|institutional self-interest presented as common good]], and [[axioms/WHEN YOU GET THERE, YOU’RE STILL NOT THERE|premature arrival]]. Record the behavioral model that best predicts the next action, not the account that sounds most complete.
