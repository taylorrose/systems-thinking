# Ask Who Is Doing the Work the System Was Built to Do

For a system you depend on, list the tasks it exists to perform and then trace who actually performs each one now. Count the forms filled in, the computations made, the assembly done, the waiting absorbed, and the charges paid for what was once included.

When a step has moved outward, note how the move was justified—as convenience, saving, or choice—and price the labor it added. Treat the total as part of what the system costs, and expect further steps to move the same way unless the transfer is refused or made visible.
