# Choose the Smallest System That Can Do the Job

When a system cannot be avoided, size it to the job rather than to the ambition behind it. Ask what the smallest arrangement is that could produce the required result, and what each additional part, rule, or role would add beyond that.

Adopt the smallest version that works, and treat every proposed enlargement as a further claim on the effort available for the work itself.
