# Leave Slack in the Parts of a System People Must Live In

Find the places where people actually spend their time inside the system, then ask how much unspecified room those places leave: how much of the day is unscheduled, how much variation passes without correction, how much can be improvised without breaking a rule.

Where that room is narrow, widen it before adding structure. Prefer the simpler arrangement that leaves gaps to the tighter one that closes them, and treat slack an efficiency review would call waste as a cost worth paying where human beings have to live.
