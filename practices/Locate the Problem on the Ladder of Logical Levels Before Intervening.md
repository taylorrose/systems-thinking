# Locate the Problem on the Ladder of Logical Levels Before Intervening

Take "Where's the problem?" as a question to be settled first. Ask which level the behavior you want to change is controlled at, and whether the thing you are about to act on sits at that level or one below it.

Then direct the intervention at the level that governs the outcome, and read continued intractability as a sign that the problem has been placed on the wrong rung rather than as a call for more force at the same one.
