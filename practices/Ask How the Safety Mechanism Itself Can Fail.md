# Ask How the Safety Mechanism Itself Can Fail

When a system is presented as fail-safe, turn the question around from the hazard to the safeguard. Ask what the safeguard is made of, how it behaves during normal operation rather than during the emergency it exists for, and what it does to the rest of the system if it stops working or acts at the wrong moment.

Count the answers as part of the system's hazards rather than as reassurance about them, and treat the absence of an answer as an unexamined failure mode rather than as evidence of safety.
