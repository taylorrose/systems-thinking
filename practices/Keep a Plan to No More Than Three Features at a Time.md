# Keep a Plan to No More Than Three Features at a Time

Count the features of the present system a plan proposes to correct at once, and note the level each sits at. Keep the count to three or fewer.

When the list runs longer, do not rebalance the wording or the justification; take features off the list and schedule them as later, separate changes.
