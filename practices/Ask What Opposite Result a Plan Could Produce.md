# Ask What Opposite Result a Plan Could Produce

Before committing to a plan in a complex system, state its intended result and then ask what the opposite of that result would look like. Trace the paths by which the plan itself could bring the opposite about: needs it creates, responses it provokes, and effects that return to the system through some route outside the plan.

Name the indicators that would show such a reversal beginning, and watch them alongside the measures of intended success. Treat an unexpected result as information about how the system works rather than as a failure of effort.
