# Ask What the System Was Told Before Explaining What It Did

When a system's behavior looks unaccountable, reconstruct its input before reconstructing its motives. Find the reports it actually received, note who wrote them and what each one asserted, and read the behavior against that account rather than against the situation you can see.

Where the two diverge, treat the divergence itself as the finding, and expect the system to keep acting on the reported version until the reports change.
