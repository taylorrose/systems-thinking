# Distinguish Survival Skill from Competence in Long-Serving Insiders

When a system has run one continuous contest for a long time, ask what its longest-serving members have actually demonstrated. Endurance inside the system is evidence of skill at remaining inside it, and is not by itself evidence of ability at the system's stated work.

Look for the two separately: which decisions show competence at the task, and which show competence at persisting. Rely on a long tenure as a qualification only where the tenure required the task to be done well.
