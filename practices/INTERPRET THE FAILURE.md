# INTERPRET THE FAILURE

Establish the condition without reassurance. Test [[axioms/THINGS HAVE NEVER BEEN BETTER—BUT THEY’RE IMPROVING|improvement claims]], admit when [[axioms/THINGS AREN’T WORKING VERY WELL|things are not working]], and consider that [[axioms/THINGS (THINGS GENERALLY ALL THINGS THE WHOLE WORKS) ARE INDEED NOT WORKING VERY WELL. IN FACT, THEY NEVER DID|malfunction may be ordinary]]. Begin from [[axioms/SYSTEMS IN GENERAL WORK POORLY OR NOT AT ALL|poor systemic function]], [[axioms/COMPLICATED SYSTEMS SELDOM EXCEED FIVE PERCENT EFFICIENCY|low complicated-system efficiency]], and [[axioms/IF ANYTHING CAN GO WRONG, IT WILL|adverse possibilities]].

Preserve the evidence. [[axioms/CHERISH YOUR EXCEPTIONS|Keep exceptions]], [[axioms/CHERISH YOUR SYSTEM-FAILURES|keep failures]], and [[axioms/CHERISH YOUR BUGS. STUDY THEM|study bugs]]. Ask [[axioms/BUG OR BONANZA|whether the defect reveals a capability]] and accept that [[axioms/ERROR CORRECTION IS WHAT WE DO|correction is continuing work]]. Search empirically because [[axioms/THE CRUCIAL VARIABLES ARE DISCOVERED BY ACCIDENT|critical variables arrive by accident]], [[axioms/ONE DOES NOT KNOW ALL THE EXPECTED EFFECTS OF KNOWN BUGS|known bugs have unknown effects]], [[axioms/INTERMITTENT FAILURE IS THE HARDEST CASE|intermittence hides causes]], and [[axioms/A BUG MAY BE PURELY LOCAL, BUT YOU AND I CAN NEVER KNOW THAT FOR SURE|locality is uncertain]].

Widen the failure boundary. Expect [[axioms/ANY LARGE SYSTEM IS GOING TO BE OPERATING MOST OF THE TIME IN FAILURE MODE|large-system failure mode]], [[axioms/A SYSTEM CAN FAIL IN AN INFINITE NUMBER OF WAYS|unbounded modes]], and [[axioms/IN COMPLEX SYSTEMS, MALFUNCTION AND EVEN TOTAL NON-FUNCTION MAY NOT BE DETECTABLE FOR LONG PERIODS, IF EVER|undetected nonfunction]]. If [[axioms/IF IT DOESN’T FAIL HERE, IT WILL FAIL THERE|failure moves]], follow it. Challenge [[axioms/WHEN A FAIL-SAFE SYSTEM FAILS, IT FAILS BY FAILING TO FAIL SAFE|fail-safe claims]], since [[axioms/WHEN BIG SYSTEMS FAIL, THE FAILURE IS OFTEN BIG|big failure scales]], [[axioms/COLOSSAL SYSTEMS FOSTER COLOSSAL ERRORS|colossal systems enlarge errors]], and [[axioms/COLOSSAL ERRORS TEND TO ESCAPE NOTICE|colossal errors hide]]. Do not infer diagnosis merely because [[axioms/IF IT’S TREATED BY DOCTORS IT MUST BE A DISEASE|a treatment exists]].

Interpret failure as information about the frame. [[axioms/PROBLEMS ARE NOT THE PROBLEM; COPING IS THE PROBLEM|Inspect coping]], locate [[axioms/LOOK FOR THE SELF-REFERENTIAL POINT—THAT’S WHERE THE PROBLEM IS LIKELY TO BE|self-reference]], and [[axioms/STAY AWAY FROM SELF-REFERENCE—THIS MEANS YOU|remove your own recursive trap]]. Reject closure when [[axioms/THIS SYSTEM IS THE ONLY CORRECT SYSTEM|the system certifies itself]]. If [[axioms/IF YOUR PROBLEM SEEMS UNSOLVABLE, CONSIDER THAT YOU MAY HAVE A META-PROBLEM|the problem is meta-level]], check whether [[axioms/IF YOU ARE NOT PART OF THE SOLUTION, YOU ARE PART OF THE PROBLEM|your participation sustains it]], [[axioms/THE SOLUTION IS OFTEN PART OF THE PROBLEM|the remedy belongs to it]], or [[axioms/THE AUTOMATIC PILOT IS NOT MUCH HELP WITH HIGHJACKERS|the control fits an obsolete problem]].

Look for feedback signatures. When [[axioms/IF THINGS ARE ACTING VERY STRANGELY, CONSIDER THAT YOU MAY BE IN A FEEDBACK SITUATION|behavior becomes strange]], [[axioms/WHEN PROBLEMS DON’T YIELD TO COMMONSENSE SOLUTIONS, LOOK FOR THE THERMOSTAT|find the thermostat]] and [[axioms/STAY OUT OF THE POSITIVE FEEDBACK TRAP|interrupt reinforcement]]. Treat [[axioms/A SYSTEM THAT IGNORES FEEDBACK HAS ALREADY BEGUN THE PROCESS OF TERMINAL INSTABILITY|ignored feedback as instability]], [[axioms/TOTAL SYSTEMS TEND TO RUN AWAY (GO OUT OF CONTROL)|totality as runaway risk]], and [[axioms/A TOTAL SYSTEM THAT GOES INTO A RUNAWAY SEQUENCE MAY BE FORCED TO GROW OR TO DISINTEGRATE IN CHAOS|forced growth or chaos as warning]]. Account for [[axioms/THE SYSTEM ALWAYS KICKS BACK|kickback]], [[axioms/NO OBSERVATION WITHOUT ITS EFFECTS|observer effects]], [[axioms/THE PROBE IS ALTERED ALSO|probe changes]], and [[axioms/IN A CLOSED SYSTEM, INFORMATION TENDS TO DECREASE AND HALLUCINATION TENDS TO INCREASE|closed-loop hallucination]].

Choose learning over force. [[axioms/GREAT ADVANCES DO NOT COME OUT OF SYSTEMS DESIGNED TO PRODUCE GREAT ADVANCES|Grand systems do not manufacture breakthroughs]]; [[axioms/MAJOR ADVANCES TAKE PLACE BY FITS AND STARTS|advances are uneven]], while [[axioms/COMPLICATED SYSTEMS PRODUCE COMPLICATED RESPONSES TO PROBLEMS|complication breeds complicated response]]. If [[axioms/IF THINGS SEEM TO BE GETTING WORSE EVEN FASTER THAN USUAL, CONSIDER THAT THE REMEDY MAY BE AT FAULT|the remedy worsens the condition]], [[axioms/ESCALATING THE WRONG SOLUTION DOES NOT IMPROVE THE OUTCOME|do not escalate it]]. Apply [[axioms/THE MOST EFFECTIVE APPROACH TO COPING IS TO LEARN THE BASIC LAWS OF SYSTEMS-BEHAVIOR|behavioral laws before control]], expect [[axioms/COMPLEX SYSTEMS EXHIBIT UNEXPECTED BEHAVIOR|surprise]], and [[axioms/IF YOU CAN’T CHANGE THE SYSTEM, CHANGE THE FRAME—IT COMES TO THE SAME THING|change the frame]] before prescribing again.
