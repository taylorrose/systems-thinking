# Attend to the Error That Is Currently Before You

Give constant attention to the error of the moment — the one actually in front of you — rather than to the goal it is interfering with or to the general question of why errors occur. Identify what has just gone wrong, correct that, and let the next error take its turn.

This requires accepting the bitter pill first: that errors are the normal material of the work rather than interruptions of it.
