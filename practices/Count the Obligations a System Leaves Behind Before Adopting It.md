# Count the Obligations a System Leaves Behind Before Adopting It

Before adopting a system, ask what remains once it stops working. Name the physical remnants, the custodial duties, the space it will continue to occupy, and the parties who will hold those duties after the system's useful life ends.

Then compare that residue against the working life you expect to get from it. Where the remaining obligation is long, unbounded, or transferable only to a successor, treat it as part of the price of adoption rather than as a later question.
