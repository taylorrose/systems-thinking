# Choose a Goal Unlikely to Go Out of Date

When setting or replacing a system's goal, ask what happens to the system if the goal is reached. A goal that can be finished sets a date on the organization built to pursue it.

Prefer a goal that is not likely to become out-of-date within the foreseeable future, and that the existing capability can serve without being dismantled. The administrative artistry is revealed in that choice.
