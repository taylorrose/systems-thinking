# Treat Bizarre System Behavior as a Symptom of Immersion Rather Than Incompetence

When people inside an organization behave in ways that look absurd from outside, resist the explanation that they are incompetent or ill-intentioned. Ask instead what range of experience the system permits them: what reaches them, what contrast they have to judge it against, and how long they have had nothing else.

Check whether the behavior would look normal to someone who saw only what they see. Where it would, address the deprivation rather than the person — replacing an office-holder changes little if the office produces the state.
