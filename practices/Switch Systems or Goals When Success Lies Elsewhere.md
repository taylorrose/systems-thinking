# Switch Systems or Goals When Success Lies Elsewhere

Treat both the system you are working through and the goal you are working toward as open to exchange. Ask which system now offers an elegant route to a worthwhile result, and whether a different goal would use the resources at hand better than the one currently held.

When the answer points away from the present arrangement, move rather than persist. Keep the freedom to switch available, so that neither the system nor the goal is held on to merely because it was chosen first.
