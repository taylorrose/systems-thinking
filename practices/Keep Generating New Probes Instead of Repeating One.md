# Keep Generating New Probes Instead of Repeating One

Treat each attempt as a probe whose purpose is to elicit a response and record what it revealed, rather than as an attempt that either succeeded or failed. When a probe is answered, design a different one instead of repeating it harder.

Ask what part of the system's response range remains untested, and vary the probe along that dimension. Judge progress by how much of the system's behavior has become visible, not only by whether the wanted outcome has appeared yet.
