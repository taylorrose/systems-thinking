# Ask Whether a Simple System Works on Its Own or Only in Skilled Hands

Before relying on a simple system, establish that it works at all, since being simple does not settle the question. Then locate where the reliability sits: does the arrangement hold steady by itself, like a measuring rule, or does it stay steady only while a practiced operator is handling it?

If the reliability depends on skill, name the skill, identify who currently supplies it, and decide what happens on the occasions when that person is not there. If the reliability is in the arrangement itself, the system can be passed on without carrying that condition with it.
