# Treat a Failing Familiar Response as a Sign the Rules Have Changed

When the response that has always worked is making the situation worse, read that as evidence about the rules rather than about your effort. Ask what would have to be true for the usual move to have reversed its sign, and what a system operating under those new rules would call for instead.

Before adding force to a response the situation is already punishing, reconsider the rulebook and revise the strategy that once preserved stability. The reading may be wrong, so prefer a limited move whose result reveals whether the rules have in fact changed.
