# Repair a Study’s Design Before Adding More Measurements

Before quantifying anything further, state what the study is arranged to observe and what question that arrangement can actually answer. Check whether the comparison, the sample, and the measured quantity match the question being asked.

Where they do not match, change the arrangement rather than the arithmetic. Add measurements only once the design would support the conclusion they are meant to sharpen.
