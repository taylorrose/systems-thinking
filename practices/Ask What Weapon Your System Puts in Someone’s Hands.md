# Ask What Weapon Your System Puts in Someone’s Hands

Put the question directly to the system you run: is it putting a weapon in someone's hands? Identify who receives it and what kind of violence it makes possible.

Use the answer as a clue when guessing what the system may be aiming at. Do not promote that clue into certainty about a hidden or “true” goal.
