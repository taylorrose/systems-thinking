# Resist Treating Every Problem as One That Needs Reframing

Having just acquired a technique, one is inclined to see every difficulty as an occasion for using it. Resist that inclination. Before reaching for a new frame, ask whether the present difficulty actually turns on how the situation is described, and whether any better description is in fact available to you rather than merely desirable.

Hold expectations low. Treat a working reframe as an unusual piece of luck when it appears, and keep the ordinary means of dealing with the situation in play meanwhile.
