# Change Your Approach When the Behavior Is Not the One You Intended

Having accepted a share in producing the outcome, move on to a different strategy of communication rather than repeating the one that produced it. Treat the unwanted behavior as information about the approach, not about the recipient’s willingness.

Choose the next attempt for its promise of a different outcome, and test it the same way. The reward on offer is success, rather than the doubtful pleasure of complaining about failure.
