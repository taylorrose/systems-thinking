# Do Not Treat Added Manpower as a Remedy for Lateness

When work is running late, resist the reflex of assigning more people to it. Ask first what the measured effect of the last such addition was, and whether anyone has observed the schedule improve after one.

Where the schedule matters, look for changes other than headcount, and expect any staffing change to be judged by what it actually produces rather than by the direction it was intended to move.
