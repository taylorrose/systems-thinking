# Try a System on the Task Its Shape Suggests

When a system's form resembles a familiar tool, take the resemblance as a hypothesis and test it: put the system to the task its shape suggests and observe whether it works.

Where the form suggests nothing, do not pretend the correct handle is obvious. Recognizing it remains largely intuitive, though careful study of the system may help.
