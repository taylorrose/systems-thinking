# Ask Which Conditions a Person’s Winning Traits Were Selected For

When someone has come through a severe contest, name the conditions the contest imposed and the qualities those conditions rewarded. Then ask whether the situation they now occupy still imposes those conditions.

Where it does not, expect the selected qualities to persist anyway and to fit badly. Treat the mismatch as a property of the selection rather than a defect of character, and judge the person against the demands actually in front of them.
