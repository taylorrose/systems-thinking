# Do Not Predict a New Function From the Old One It Grew Out Of

When a system is being expanded, avoid describing what it will then do as an extension of what it does now. Ask instead what could exist at the new level of organization that has no counterpart at the present one.

Where such a function cannot be named in advance, say so, and watch for it after each step of expansion rather than forecasting it before.
