# Trace the Operations That Produced a Product Before Accepting Its Name

When a system offers something under a familiar name, list the operations that actually produced it, from origin through handling to delivery. Compare that list with the operations the name normally implies.

Where the lists diverge, treat the result as a different thing and describe it by its operations. Reserve the original word for the product whose operations still match it.
