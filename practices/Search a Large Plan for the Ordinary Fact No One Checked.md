# Search a Large Plan for the Ordinary Fact No One Checked

On a plan whose scale makes failure expensive, spend deliberate effort on the assumptions too obvious to have been examined. Ask which basic facts of geography, capability, or population everyone believes are already settled, then find who actually verified each one and when.

List the people and places the plan acts upon but has not consulted, and ask what they will do rather than what the plan requires of them. Treat the absence of a recorded check as an open question, not as evidence that the matter is fine.
