# MAKE THE CHANGE TESTABLE AND REVERSIBLE

Before acting, record the baseline, prediction, contrary result, boundary, and observer. Tread carefully because [[axioms/IN SETTING UP A NEW SYSTEM, TREAD SOFTLY. YOU MAY BE DISTURBING ANOTHER SYSTEM THAT IS ACTUALLY WORKING|a new system can disturb working neighbors]]. Include [[axioms/NO SYSTEM WITHOUT ITS OBSERVER|the observer]], since [[axioms/NO OBSERVATION WITHOUT ITS EFFECTS|observation has effects]], [[axioms/THE SYSTEM IS ALTERED BY THE PROBE USED TO TEST IT|the probe changes the system]], and [[axioms/THE PROBE IS ALTERED ALSO|the system changes the probe]]. Detect recursion through [[axioms/PRIMATE PEEPER PEEPS, SEES PRIMATE PEEPING AT PRIMATE PEEPER|mutual observation]].

Bound consequences without claiming isolation. [[axioms/ANY GIVEN ELEMENT OF ONE SYSTEM IS SIMULTANEOUSLY AN ELEMENT IN AN INFINITY OF OTHER SYSTEMS|Each element belongs elsewhere]] and [[axioms/EVERYTHING CORRELATES|relationships cross the boundary]]. Hold together [[axioms/(A) YOU CAN’T CHANGE JUST ONE THING|unavoidable side effects]] and [[axioms/(B) YOU CAN’T CHANGE EVERYTHING|limited reach]]. Treat [[axioms/THERE IS NO SUCH THING AS NONINVOLVEMENT|withdrawal as action]] because [[axioms/NONINVOLVEMENT HERE MEANS INVOLVEMENT THERE|absence shifts involvement]].

Make the test discriminating. Specify what behavior would confirm or contradict the model, the feedback interval, and the stopping threshold. Do not let [[axioms/ADDING NUMBERS TO A BAD STUDY DOESN’T CLARIFY IT|numbers legitimize a bad test]] or [[axioms/LARGE AMOUNTS OF POOR DATA TEND TO PREEMPT ANY AMOUNT OF GOOD DATA|poor volume bury good evidence]]. Require [[axioms/JUST CALLING IT FEEDBACK DOESN’T MEAN THAT IT HAS ACTUALLY FED BACK|feedback that actually returns]], [[axioms/IT HASN’T FED BACK UNTIL THE SYSTEM CHANGES COURSE|an observable course change]], and [[axioms/NATURE IS ONLY WISE WHEN FEEDBACKS ARE RAPID|a short enough delay]], while remembering [[axioms/FEEDBACK ALWAYS GIVES A PICTURE OF THE PAST|the signal describes the past]].

Choose favorable conditions. [[axioms/AVOID UNFAVORABLE SETTINGS|Reject hostile settings]], let [[axioms/SYSTEMS RUN BEST WHEN DESIGNED TO RUN DOWNHILL|operation run downhill]], and [[axioms/GO WITH THE FLOW|use existing forces]]. Preserve [[axioms/LOOSE SYSTEMS LAST LONGER AND FUNCTION BETTER|loose coupling]] and [[axioms/LOOSE SYSTEMS HAVE LARGER INTERSTICES|room between parts]]. Do not expect [[axioms/BAD DESIGN CAN RARELY BE OVERCOME BY MORE DESIGN, WHETHER GOOD OR BAD|added design to repair the premise]].

Create the way back before the way forward. Since [[axioms/ALMOST ANYTHING IS EASIER TO GET INTO THAN OUT OF|entry is easier than exit]] and [[axioms/TAKING IT DOWN IS OFTEN MORE TEDIOUS THAN SETTING IT UP|removal is costly]], decide to [[axioms/PLAN TO SCRAP THE FIRST SYSTEM YOU WILL ANYWAY|scrap the first system]] and [[axioms/ALWAYS ACT SO AS TO INCREASE YOUR OPTIONS|preserve options]]. Name the rollback operator, trigger, mechanism, and latest safe reversal time.

Limit the claim. [[axioms/A LITTLE GRANDIOSITY GOES A LONG WAY|Use minimal ambition]]; reject [[axioms/THE FINAL TRUTH IS JUST AROUND THE CORNER|promised final truth]], [[axioms/WHEN THE CURRENT REVISION IS COMPLETE, THE SYSTEM WILL BE PERFECT|perfect next revisions]], and [[axioms/PERFECTION CAN BE ACHIEVED ON THE DAY AFTER THE FINAL DEADLINE|post-deadline perfection]]. Apply [[axioms/IN DEALING WITH LARGE SYSTEMS, THE STRIVING FOR PERFECTION IS A SERIOUS IMPERFECTION|perfectionism as a defect]] and, when learning requires motion, [[axioms/IF IT’S WORTH DOING AT ALL, IT’S WORTH DOING POORLY|permit an imperfect bounded attempt]].

Pre-mortem the test. [[axioms/IN ORDER TO SUCCEED IT IS NECESSARY TO KNOW HOW TO AVOID THE MOST LIKELY WAYS TO FAIL|Name likely failures]], leave room for [[axioms/A SYSTEM CAN FAIL IN AN INFINITE NUMBER OF WAYS|unknown failures]], and treat [[axioms/IF IT PUTS A WEAPON IN YOUR HAND, IT IS AIMING AT SOME KIND OF VIOLENCE|affordances as behavioral warnings]]. Stop expansion when [[axioms/AS SYSTEMS GROW IN SIZE AND COMPLEXITY, THEY TEND TO LOSE BASIC FUNCTIONS|growth removes basic function]].

Finally, anticipate the successor state. [[axioms/NEW SYSTEMS MEAN NEW PROBLEMS|New systems add problems]], [[axioms/IN ORDER TO REMAIN UNCHANGED, THE SYSTEM MUST CHANGE|continued identity requires change]], [[axioms/THE OLD SYSTEM IS NOW THE NEW PROBLEM|old systems become problems]], and [[axioms/THE GHOST OF THE OLD SYSTEM CONTINUES TO HAUNT THE NEW|old structure persists]]. Watch when [[axioms/SYSTEMS EXPAND, AND AS THEY EXPAND, THEY ENCROACH|expansion crosses boundaries]] or [[axioms/THE SOLUTION IS OFTEN PART OF THE PROBLEM|the solution joins the problem]]. Record how [[axioms/THE FUTURE IS PARTLY DETERMINED BY WHAT WE DO NOW|this action shapes the future]].
