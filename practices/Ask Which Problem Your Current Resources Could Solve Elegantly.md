# Ask Which Problem Your Current Resources Could Solve Elegantly

Begin from what you have rather than from the problem in front of you. Inventory the resources and systems currently at hand, then ask the two questions the proficient student asks: what can I do right now and succeed at it, and for which problem do my current resources promise an elegant solution?

Add timing to the match, since the same resources and the same problem may fit now and not later. Then take on the problem that fits, rather than waiting for an impasse to force the choice.
