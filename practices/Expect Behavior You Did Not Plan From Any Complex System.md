# Expect Behavior You Did Not Plan From Any Complex System

Treat unplanned behavior as a normal property of complexity rather than as evidence that someone was careless. When you meet behavior you did not design, ask what the system is doing rather than whether it is allowed to do it.

Check your own position as well: authorship of a system is not knowledge of it, and the sense that you have covered every contingency is a wish worth testing against what the system actually does.
