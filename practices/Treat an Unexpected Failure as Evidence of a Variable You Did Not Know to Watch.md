# Treat an Unexpected Failure as Evidence of a Variable You Did Not Know to Watch

When a system fails in a mode its design did not predict, record the condition that changed immediately before the failure and the boundary within which success had previously been judged. Ask what limit the failure crossed and where the burden went.

Add the newly exposed variable to the next observation or test. Do not explain the event away merely because the variable was absent from the original model; its absence is what the failure has revealed.
