# GOVERN THE RESULT

Do not confuse installation with completion. At each review, restate the desired effect and compare it with observed function. If [[axioms/IF A SYSTEM IS WORKING, LEAVE IT ALONE. DON’T CHANGE ANYTHING|the system still works, avoid gratuitous change]]. Otherwise expect [[axioms/WHATEVER THE SYSTEM HAS DONE BEFORE, YOU CAN BE SURE IT WILL DO IT AGAIN|prior behavior to recur]], because [[axioms/A SYSTEM THAT PERFORMS A CERTAIN FUNCTION OR THAT OPERATES IN A CERTAIN WAY WILL CONTINUE TO OPERATE IN THAT WAY REGARDLESS OF THE NEED OR OF CHANGED CONDITIONS|operation outlives changed need]] and [[axioms/THE SYSTEM CONTINUES TO DO ITS THING, REGARDLESS OF CIRCUMSTANCES|activity persists]].

Audit goals as well as outputs. Watch [[axioms/SYSTEMS DEVELOP GOALS OF THEIR OWN THE INSTANT THEY COME INTO BEING|emergent goals]], [[axioms/INTRASYSTEM GOALS COME FIRST|internal priority]], [[axioms/SYSTEMS DON’T WORK FOR YOU OR FOR ME. THEY WORK FOR THEIR OWN GOALS|goal autonomy]], and [[axioms/THE SYSTEM BEHAVES AS IF IT HAS A WILL TO LIVE|self-preservation]]. Revalidate purpose whenever maintenance of the system begins substituting for the effect.

Constrain growth. Expect [[axioms/THE SYSTEM ITSELF (DAS SYSTEM AN UND FUER SICH) TENDS TO GROW AT 5-6% PER ANNUM|routine growth]], [[axioms/SYSTEMS TEND TO EXPAND TO FILL THE KNOWN UNIVERSE|expansion into available space]], and [[axioms/SYSTEMS EXPAND, AND AS THEY EXPAND, THEY ENCROACH|encroachment]]. Scale is not repetition: [[axioms/A LARGE SYSTEM, PRODUCED BY EXPANDING THE DIMENSIONS OF A SMALLER SYSTEM, DOES NOT BEHAVE LIKE THE SMALLER SYSTEM|larger systems behave differently]], [[axioms/NEW STRUCTURE IMPLIES NEW FUNCTIONS|new structure adds functions]], [[axioms/AS SYSTEMS GROW IN SIZE AND COMPLEXITY, THEY TEND TO LOSE BASIC FUNCTIONS|complexity removes basics]], and [[axioms/THE BIGGER THE SYSTEM, THE NARROWER AND MORE SPECIALIZED THE INTERFACE WITH INDIVIDUALS|interfaces narrow with size]]. Preserve [[axioms/LOOSE SYSTEMS LAST LONGER AND FUNCTION BETTER|looseness]] where coupling is unnecessary.

Trace displaced cost. [[axioms/THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF ANERGY IN THE UNIVERSE IS CONSTANT|Burden is not abolished]]; [[axioms/SYSTEMS OPERATE BY REDISTRIBUTING ANERGY INTO DIFFERENT FORMS AND INTO ACCUMULATIONS OF DIFFERENT SIZES|systems redistribute it]]. Widen review because [[axioms/ANY GIVEN ELEMENT OF ONE SYSTEM IS SIMULTANEOUSLY AN ELEMENT IN AN INFINITY OF OTHER SYSTEMS|elements belong to neighboring systems]], while holding together [[axioms/(A) YOU CAN’T CHANGE JUST ONE THING|cross-effects]] and [[axioms/(B) YOU CAN’T CHANGE EVERYTHING|bounded authority]].

Treat success as a risk point. [[axioms/SYSTEMS TEND TO MALFUNCTION CONSPICUOUSLY JUST AFTER THEIR GREATEST TRIUMPH|Triumph can precede failure]], [[axioms/THE ARMY IS NOW FULLY PREPARED TO FIGHT THE PREVIOUS WAR|success prepares yesterday’s response]], and [[axioms/PERFECTION OF PLANNING IS A SYMPTOM OF DECAY|planning perfection can signal decay]]. Require live feedback because [[axioms/A SYSTEM THAT IGNORES FEEDBACK HAS ALREADY BEGUN THE PROCESS OF TERMINAL INSTABILITY|ignored feedback starts instability]].

Maintain retirement authority. Assume [[axioms/A TEMPORARY PATCH WILL VERY LIKELY BE PERMANENT|temporary patches persist]], [[axioms/ALMOST ANYTHING IS EASIER TO GET INTO THAN OUT OF|exit is harder than entry]], and [[axioms/TAKING IT DOWN IS OFTEN MORE TEDIOUS THAN SETTING IT UP|decommissioning is real work]]. Decide to [[axioms/PLAN TO SCRAP THE FIRST SYSTEM YOU WILL ANYWAY|plan replacement]] before necessity decides for you.

At every review, identify the successor problem. [[axioms/NEW SYSTEMS MEAN NEW PROBLEMS|New systems create problems]], [[axioms/THE OLD SYSTEM IS NOW THE NEW PROBLEM|old solutions become problems]], and [[axioms/THE GHOST OF THE OLD SYSTEM CONTINUES TO HAUNT THE NEW|old structure persists]]. Continue only while behavior improves the intended effect, feedback remains trustworthy, burdens remain acceptable, and retirement remains possible; otherwise reduce, reverse, replace, or retire.
