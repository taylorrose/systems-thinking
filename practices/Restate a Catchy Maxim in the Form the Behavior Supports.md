# Restate a Catchy Maxim in the Form the Behavior Supports

When a memorable formula is offered as a principle, separate its appeal from its claim. Say plainly what it asserts about systems, and check that assertion against what the systems in question are observed to do.

Where the two diverge, write the version the observed behavior does support and use that one instead. Discarding the slogan is not required; correcting its form is.
