# Judge Instrumentation by Whether It Yields a Recognizable Picture

Ask of any panel, report, or dashboard what picture it produces when everything happens at once, not merely whether each reading is correct. Count how many signals a person must combine before the situation has a name, and note which readings would be indistinguishable from each other during trouble.

Where no recognizable shape emerges, treat that as a defect in the instrumentation rather than in the observer, and reorganize the presentation until the state of the system can be recognized and named.
