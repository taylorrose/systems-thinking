# Do Not Assume a New System Has No Goals of Its Own

When something has just been set up, resist the assumption that its own interests lie safely in the future. Look for them at once: what the new arrangement already needs in order to keep existing, whose position now depends on it, and which of its activities would continue even if the original need disappeared.

Do not wait for age or size to justify the question. Ask it while the system is small, when the answers are still cheap to act on.
