# Check the Message Received Rather Than the Message Sent

Treat a message as unsent until you have evidence of what was received. Ask the recipient to state the point back in their own terms, or watch what they do with it, before concluding that it arrived.

Where the reconstruction diverges, look for what supplied the divergence: an assumption you did not know they held, or a feature of your own illustration you never meant to assert.
