# Judge a System’s View by What Its Intake Can Register

Before trusting what a system reports about its situation, inventory the channels through which it takes in information. Ask what each channel can detect, what it cannot detect at all, and who or what decides which observations are admitted.

Treat conditions outside that range as invisible to the system rather than absent from the world. Expect the system’s picture to be no better than the organs that produced it, and seek those conditions through some other means when they matter.
