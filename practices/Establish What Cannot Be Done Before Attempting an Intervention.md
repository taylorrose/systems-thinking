# Establish What Cannot Be Done Before Attempting an Intervention

Open work on a complex system by stating what cannot be done with it, not by listing what should be achieved. Name the outcomes the present state makes inaccessible, the behaviours that cannot be fully catalogued, and the results that would require overriding a natural law of systems.

Then check each proposed intervention against that list before spending effort on it. The purpose is to avoid the futile struggle, and the waste of energy that goes with it, rather than to dampen ambition; anything not ruled out remains available to try.
