# Prefer the Specific Intervention to Drenching the Whole System

When a system’s output is unsatisfactory, ask whether the proposed intervention acts on the part responsible for that output or merely disturbs the whole apparatus until the measure moves. Look for the equivalent of the escapement: the specific mechanism whose adjustment would produce the change for the reason you intend.

If no such mechanism can be identified, treat any improvement that follows a global intervention as unexplained rather than as evidence that the mechanism was found.
