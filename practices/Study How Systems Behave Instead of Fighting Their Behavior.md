# Study How Systems Behave Instead of Fighting Their Behavior

Before attempting to make a system do something, learn what it does on its own. Identify the regularity that produced the behavior you dislike, and ask which of the outcomes you want that regularity already permits.

Where a course of action requires the system to behave contrary to its own laws, abandon it and look for the pathway that is available instead.
