# Attribute Part of What You Observe to the Act of Observing

Before treating an observation as a fact about the system, ask which part of it is a response to being observed. Identify what the system does only while someone is watching, and what the watching itself consumed, disturbed, or provoked.

Where the two cannot be separated, record the observation together with the means used to obtain it, so later readers know what was measured and what was caused.
