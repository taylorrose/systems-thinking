# Test a Communication Against the Behavior It Produces

Before judging what a message meant, identify the behavior that followed it. State what the recipients actually did, not what they were told or what you meant.

Take that behavior as the meaning your communication carried, however far it sits from the one you intended. Where the two differ, record the difference rather than explaining it away.
