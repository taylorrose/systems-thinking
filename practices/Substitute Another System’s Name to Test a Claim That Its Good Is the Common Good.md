# Substitute Another System’s Name to Test a Claim That Its Good Is the Common Good

When a system’s interest is presented as the general interest, keep the sentence and change the system named in it. Try a system whose advantage you would not accept as everyone’s advantage.

If the substituted version is plainly false, the original was resting on the standing of the system rather than on an argument. Ask what would have to be shown — for this system, at this moment — to support the claim on its own.
