# Test a Claim of Correctness From Outside the System That Makes It

When a system asserts that it is the only correct one, note that the claim is about the system and is made by the system, and do not attempt to settle it with the system's own criteria. Identify which standard of correctness the claim assumes and who supplies it.

Then seek a standard, a record, or an observer outside the framework, and compare the claim against that. Treat a claim that cannot be checked from outside as a standing possibility of runaway rather than as a settled matter.
