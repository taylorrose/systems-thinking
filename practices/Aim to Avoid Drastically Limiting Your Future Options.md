# Aim to Avoid Drastically Limiting Your Future Options

Set the more humble aim in place of the grandiose one. Rather than trying to expand your options or to decide correctly, examine a proposed action for the options it would foreclose, and be grateful when the drastic foreclosures are avoided.

Before committing, ask which future moves the action removes, whether any of them could be needed later, and whether a less irreversible version of the action exists. Prefer the move that keeps the fewest doors permanently shut.
