# Estimate How Much of the Outside Reality Reaches the Decision Point

Treat the reality reaching a decision-maker as a quantity to be estimated rather than assumed. List what was presented to the system from outside, then trace how much of it actually arrived at the officer or control unit that acts on it.

Ask how much of the intake is instead taken up by positive feedback, which competes with reality for the same channels. The higher that feedback, the smaller the fraction of reality left, and the less the system’s picture can be relied on.
