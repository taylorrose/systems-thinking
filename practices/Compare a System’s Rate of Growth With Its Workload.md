# Compare a System’s Rate of Growth With Its Workload

Track the size of a system over several years alongside a measure of the work it actually handles, and compare the two rates.

Ask whether the additional people, budget, or procedures correspond to additional output, and where growth continued while the workload was flat or falling. Treat a persistent gap between the two rates as growth belonging to the system itself, and expect the gap to widen unless something checks it.
