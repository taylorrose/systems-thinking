# Seek a Different Approach Rather Than a Larger System

When a problem resists effort, treat the resistance as evidence that its conceptualization is wrong before treating it as evidence that more apparatus is needed. Ask how the situation has been framed, who framed it that way, and what the framing forces every proposed answer to assume.

Then restate the situation under a different distinction and see whether it becomes tractable. Prefer the approach that dissolves the difficulty to the system that would study and attack it, and count any system you would have to build as a second problem added to the first.
