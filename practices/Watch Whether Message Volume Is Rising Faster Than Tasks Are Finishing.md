# Watch Whether Message Volume Is Rising Faster Than Tasks Are Finishing

Track the volume of formal internal messages alongside the rate at which tasks actually close. Rising traffic against a growing backlog is a sign to read as a symptom rather than as diligence.

Look at what the added messages are about. Questions such as what went wrong, how far along something is, and why the feedback is poor indicate reporting on the work rather than doing it. Also check how much of the traffic ever leaves the system: when nearly all of it refers only to the system's own state, treat what it reports as unverified until something outside confirms it.
