# Count the Cost of Getting Out Before Getting In

Before entering an arrangement, a commitment, or a system, ask what leaving it would require. Identify who would have to agree, what would have to be undone, what would have to be replaced, and what would remain behind after the exit.

Weigh that cost alongside the cost of entry rather than after it, and treat an exit you cannot describe as a reason to reconsider the entry.
