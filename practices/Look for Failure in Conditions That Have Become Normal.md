# Look for Failure in Conditions That Have Become Normal

List the large harms that are routinely described as facts of life, unavoidable costs, or progress. For each one, identify the system that repeatedly produces it and compare the result with that system’s stated function.

Do not use familiarity as evidence that the condition is necessary. Ask what would be called a failure if the same result appeared suddenly or at a smaller scale.
