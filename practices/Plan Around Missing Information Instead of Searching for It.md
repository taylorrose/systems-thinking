# Plan Around Missing Information Instead of Searching for It

When an item is already missing, bound the search before starting it: decide how long you will look and what you will do if it does not appear. Treat the deadline as the real constraint rather than the item.

Then choose the course that works on what you can still reach, and note which part of that choice would have been different had the item been in hand. When the item does surface later, use that note to see what the gap cost rather than reopening a decision already made.
