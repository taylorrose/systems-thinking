# Change the Remedy Rather Than Increase the Dose

When a remedy has failed, treat “not enough of it” as the last explanation to accept rather than the first. Ask whether any amount of this action could produce the intended direction of change.

If it could not, spend the next increment of effort on a different remedy instead of a larger one, and treat renewed calls for redoubled vigor as evidence about the remedy rather than about the resolve of the people applying it.
