# Test a Slogan Against the Behavior of the People Who Post It

When an organization displays a humane principle, treat the display as a claim to be checked rather than as a description of what happens there. Ask what the people it addresses were actually doing at the moment you read it, and whether any of their time is spent on the thing the slogan names.

Where they are occupied with the system’s own business instead, read the slogan as evidence of a gap between the principle displayed and the practice observed, not as proof that the principle is being maintained.
