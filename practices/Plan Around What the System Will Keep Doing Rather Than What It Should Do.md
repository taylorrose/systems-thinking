# Plan Around What the System Will Keep Doing Rather Than What It Should Do

Before relying on a system in a plan, write down separately what it currently does and what the situation now requires of it. Treat the first as the prediction and the second as a wish until something has actually altered the operation.

Where the two diverge, plan for the divergence to persist: arrange the work so it survives the system continuing unchanged. Reserve the assumption of adjustment for cases where the change in operation has already been observed.
