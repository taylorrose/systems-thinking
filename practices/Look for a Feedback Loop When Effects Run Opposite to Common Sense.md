# Look for a Feedback Loop When Effects Run Opposite to Common Sense

When an action keeps producing the opposite of its intended effect, treat that reversal as a lead rather than as an anomaly to be dismissed. Consider that you may be in a feedback situation.

Trace what your action feeds into and what responds to it, and look for the point where your own output returns as your input. Start the search at the strangest behavior in the situation, since that is where the loop is most likely to be closing.
