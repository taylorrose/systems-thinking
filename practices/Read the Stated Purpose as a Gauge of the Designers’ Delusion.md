# Read the Stated Purpose as a Gauge of the Designers’ Delusion

When a system announces what it exists to accomplish, do not read the statement as a description of the system. Read it as a report on the people who wrote it: ask what it assumes the system can do, how far that exceeds what the structure could produce, and which wishes the gap reveals.

Use the size of that gap as the measure of the delusion you are dealing with, and expect the system’s inner goals to be found somewhere other than in the announcement. Learning to identify such statements quickly leaves less delusion in your own dealings with the system.
