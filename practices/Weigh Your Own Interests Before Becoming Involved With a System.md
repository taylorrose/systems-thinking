# Weigh Your Own Interests Before Becoming Involved With a System

Treat entry into a system as a decision that gets made, not one that gets discovered. Before joining, name what the involvement would ask of you, what it would give, and which of your own interests it serves or spends.

Then make a balanced judgment on that basis rather than drifting, sailing, or barging in uncritically. If the judgment cannot be stated, the decision has not yet been made.
