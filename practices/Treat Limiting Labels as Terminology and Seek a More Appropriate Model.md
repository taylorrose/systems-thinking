# Treat Limiting Labels as Terminology and Seek a More Appropriate Model

When a label leaves a problem intractable, examine it as an artifact of terminology rather than a permanent attribute of the universe or human nature. Ask what metaphor the label imposes and seek a model that permits a more appropriate response.
