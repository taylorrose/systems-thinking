# Write Down Counter-Evidence at the Moment You Encounter It

When an observation or argument cuts against the account you currently hold, record it before moving on. Write what happened, what it contradicts, and what would have to be true for both to hold.

Do not wait for the exception to be explained or resolved first; the point of the note is to keep the fact available after the moment of noticing has passed. Review the accumulated exceptions periodically rather than only when a theory is already in trouble.
