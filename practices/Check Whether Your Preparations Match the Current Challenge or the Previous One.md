# Check Whether Your Preparations Match the Current Challenge or the Previous One

Take an inventory of what you are actually prepared for: the equipment held, the drills rehearsed, the procedures written, the roles staffed. For each item, name the challenge it was designed to answer and when that challenge last occurred.

Then compare that list with the challenge in front of you. Where the two diverge, ask what would have to be restructured — not merely reassigned — to answer the present challenge, and treat the size of that answer as the measure of how far readiness has drifted into the past.
