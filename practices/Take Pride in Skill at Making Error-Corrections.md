# Take Pride in Skill at Making Error-Corrections

Treat error-correction as an art with a standard of mastery, and take pride in your command of it rather than only in the outcomes it protects. Judge yourself on how quickly an error is noticed, how accurately it is diagnosed, and how cleanly it is corrected.

Held this way, encountering an error becomes an occasion to exercise the skill instead of evidence of falling short.
