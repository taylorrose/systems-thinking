# Preserve an External Source of Corrective Feedback

Keep some observer, resource, or decision outside the system’s boundary and able to refuse what the system demands. Give that external position a channel for reporting excess and enough independence to impose a limit.

If every source of correction has been absorbed into the system, treat apparent stability as untested. Recreate an outside before relying on the system to regulate itself.
