# Treat Information as Perishable Rather Than Stored Once and Trusted

When relying on a record, first establish its age and what has changed since it was made. Ask when it was true, what could have moved since, and whether anything confirms it still holds.

Build the same assumption into how information is kept: date what is stored, plan for it to be renewed rather than merely retained, and do not treat a file's continued existence as evidence that its contents are still good.
