# Determine a Role’s Real-World Function Instead of Reading Its Title

Take a role whose title you have been treating as a description and work out its real-world function. Ask what the occupant is supposed to do, then watch what actually consumes the hours, the effort, and the budget, and note what the system rewards and punishes.

State the function in terms of those observed activities rather than the title. Treat the difference between the two statements as the measure of how far the name can be trusted.
