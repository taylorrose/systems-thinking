# Proceed One Small Step at a Time Instead of Attempting Grand Solutions

Break an intended change into the smallest step whose result can be observed, and take only that step. Before enlarging the plan, ask what the step actually produced and whether the next one still looks worthwhile.

Treat the ambition to settle the whole matter in one move as a warning sign rather than a strength. When a proposal can only be evaluated after it is fully in place, reduce it until some part of it can be judged earlier.
