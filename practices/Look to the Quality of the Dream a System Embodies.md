# Look to the Quality of the Dream a System Embodies

Ask what image the system is being built to realize, and whose image it is. State it plainly enough to be examined: what it treats as worth having, what it leaves out, and what it would require of the people around it.

Then judge the image itself rather than only the plan derived from it. Where realizing it would be a nightmare for others, that is a defect in the dream and not merely in the execution, and it is the dream that needs revising.
