# Note Trends Without Treating Them as Predictions

Keep track of the directions in which the quantities you care about have been moving, and say so in those terms: what has changed, over what period, by how much.

Stop short of extending the line. Where a decision needs a figure for the future, mark it as an assumption imported by the decision rather than a result delivered by the trend.
