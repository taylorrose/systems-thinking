# Take Responsibility for Routing Your Own Message Through the System

When communicating with a system, take on a far greater degree of responsibility and initiative than conversation with a person or a pet requires. Drop the expectation that the system will do the receiving, routing, and answering for you.

In practice: establish that the message actually arrived and that someone read it; identify by name the part that would have to act, and reach that part rather than the system in general; state what response is wanted instead of assuming it will be inferred; and follow up on your own schedule, since silence is at least as likely to mean the message never reached a responder as that it was refused.
