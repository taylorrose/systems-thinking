# Suspect the Remedy When Trouble Accelerates

When a situation is getting worse faster than it used to, put the current remedy on the list of possible causes. Ask when the acceleration began, what was being applied at that time, and whether each application has been followed by a deeper setback.

Investigate that relationship by a method safe for the system in question before intensifying, withdrawing, or replacing the remedy. Do not interpret failure as proof that too little of the remedy was applied.
