# Identify the Special Feature Before Copying a System That Survives a Hostile Setting

When a system appears to work in conditions that defeat others, do not read its survival as evidence that the setting is workable. Locate the specific feature its survival rests on, and state plainly how paradoxical or specialized that feature is.

Then ask whether you can reproduce that feature, and whether it would still apply to your own setting. If it cannot be reproduced, the surviving system is not a precedent for entering the same conditions.
