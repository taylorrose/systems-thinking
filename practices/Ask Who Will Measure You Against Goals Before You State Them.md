# Ask Who Will Measure You Against Goals Before You State Them

When a system asks you to commit aims to writing, treat the document as a measuring instrument rather than a description. Ask who will read it, what will be scored against it, whether partial or unplanned achievement can be recorded, and what the statement licenses others to prescribe about how you spend your time.

Then keep the statement no more specific than the work can bear. Say what you are actually pursuing, avoid promising counts and schedules you would not want enforced, and leave room for the unplanned turns that carry the work forward — so that what you write does not become the standard used to obstruct it.
