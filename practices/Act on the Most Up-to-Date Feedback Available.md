# Act on the Most Up-to-Date Feedback Available

Before committing to a response, establish how old the information behind it is. Ask when the report was generated, what has been allowed to happen since, and whether a more current reading can be obtained at acceptable cost.

Where the newer reading would change the response, wait for it or act provisionally in a way that can be corrected. Where no fresher reading exists, treat the chosen action as a move made on stale information rather than as a settled answer.
