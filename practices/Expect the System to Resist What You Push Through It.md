# Expect the System to Resist What You Push Through It

Before driving a process through a system, ask what conditions the process itself will create, and whether those conditions will make the next increment of the same process harder.

Budget for that resistance rather than reading it as sabotage, bad luck, or local incompetence. When effort produces counter-pressure, treat the counter-pressure as the expected response of the system and plan the next move around it instead of pushing harder on the same point.
