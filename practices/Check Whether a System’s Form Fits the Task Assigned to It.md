# Check Whether a System’s Form Fits the Task Assigned to It

Before assigning a task, describe the task concretely and then look at the system's actual construction—its parts, its handles, what it is arranged to move. Ask whether anything in that arrangement corresponds to the work being requested.

Where nothing does, treat the mismatch as evidence that the task belongs to some other instrument, and check the fit before investing in the attempt rather than after it fails.
