# Weigh a Helpful Intervention as Carefully as a Harmful One

Do not let a helpful motive shorten the deliberation. Before intervening, ask what the intervention will do to the system if the hoped-for improvement does not occur, and whether that outcome is one you can live with.

Prefer the smallest intervention whose result you can observe, and keep the helpful intent out of the evidence: judge the move by what the system does afterward, not by what was meant.
