# Trace an Element Through the Other Systems It Belongs To

When an element appears unrelated or uninvolved, list the other systems in which it participates. Follow what enters and leaves through each membership: obligations, information, resources, constraints, and effects.

Use those memberships to find the relationship hidden by the boundary of the system first examined. Treat absence from that one system as a change of route, not as evidence of no involvement anywhere.
