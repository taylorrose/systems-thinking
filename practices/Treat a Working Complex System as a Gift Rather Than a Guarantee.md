# Treat a Working Complex System as a Gift Rather Than a Guarantee

When a complex system is found to be working, receive that with thanks rather than with the assumption that it was owed. Ask what would be lost if it stopped, and avoid speaking or planning as though its functioning were entailed by its design.

Reserve the confidence that a complex system will work for cases where it has been seen to work, and keep it out of expectations about systems that have not.
