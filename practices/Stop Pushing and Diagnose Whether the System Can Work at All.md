# Stop Pushing and Diagnose Whether the System Can Work at All

When pressure has already been applied without result, take the absence of result as information rather than as a reason for more pressure. Ask what evidence exists that this system works on its own, and whether anything in reach would change that.

Watch for the signs that the push is serving the pusher: exertion that is visible but unconnected to the outcome, and reasons for continuing that require denying what the last attempt showed. Where a further push might still be warranted, treat it as a single test with an observable result rather than an escalating campaign.
