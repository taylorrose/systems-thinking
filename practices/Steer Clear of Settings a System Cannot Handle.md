# Steer Clear of Settings a System Cannot Handle

Before committing a system to a job, examine the conditions it will have to work against. Ask how fast they change, how regularly, and whether the system could register and respond to that change at all.

Where the conditions fluctuate faster or less predictably than the system can follow, treat the setting as unsuitable rather than the design as unfinished, and look for another way to do the job.
