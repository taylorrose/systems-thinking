# Look for a Systems-Law Behind a Failure Instead of a Culprit

When something goes wrong, notice whether your account of it names an agent—bad luck, a hostile world, one careless person, one missing message. Set that account aside long enough to state what the system did in terms of how systems behave generally.

Then ask which regularity would have made failure expectable, and whether that regularity appears in other systems. Adopt a single-cause explanation only after the general account has failed to fit.
