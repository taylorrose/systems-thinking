# Re-examine a Winning Strategy Before Applying It to the Next Challenge

When a strategy has just succeeded, treat that success as a reason to inspect it rather than to repeat it. Ask which features of the old situation the strategy depended on, which of those features are present in the new one, and what the strategy assumes that is no longer true.

Where the answers diverge, look for what the new challenge asks for on its own terms before committing the proven response to it.
