# Check Whether an Event Has Entered the Record Before Expecting the System to Act on It

Before relying on a system to respond to something that happened, establish whether the event exists in the form the system recognizes. Identify which record counts, confirm that an entry was made in it, and confirm that the entry says what the event was.

If no such entry exists, treat the system as not yet in possession of the event, and make the entry rather than repeating the account to people who cannot act on it.
