# Make the Most of What Can Be Done Within the Limits

Once a limitation has been identified, turn attention to the room that remains inside it rather than to the boundary itself. Ask what the situation still permits, which of those possibilities has not yet been used, and how far the permitted moves can be pressed.

Treat a recognized limit as settled information and resume work on that basis. Determination is spent on the accessible part of the problem, not on the part reality has closed.
