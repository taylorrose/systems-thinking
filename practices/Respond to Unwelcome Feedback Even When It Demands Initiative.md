# Respond to Unwelcome Feedback Even When It Demands Initiative

When information arrives that the system did not want, separate your reaction to the messenger from the content of the message, and record the content before deciding what to do about it. Ask what adjustment the feedback is asking of the system, and what it would cost.

Expect that adjustment to be taxing and to require initiative, and treat reluctance as a signal that the response is needed rather than as grounds for deferring it. Where the system is being left to wait for its environment to adjust instead, name that as the choice being made.
