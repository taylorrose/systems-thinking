# Read a System’s Persistent Behavior as Evidence of Its Inner Goals

When a system continues an activity through changed conditions, absent need, or explicit instruction to stop, treat the continuation as data rather than as malfunction. Ask what the activity keeps intact: which role, budget, routine, or internal state is preserved by carrying on.

Infer the goal from what survives, not from what the system is said to be for. The behavior that persists under pressure is the most reliable statement of what the system is actually pursuing.
