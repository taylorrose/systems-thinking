# Trace an Outcome to Its Causes Before Crediting the System

When a system is credited with a favorable result, list what the system actually did and what else was in play. Ask whether the outcome required the system’s operation, whether comparable results appear where the system was absent, and how much of the claim rests on having merely been present.

Assign the credit only to the part the system can be shown to have produced.
