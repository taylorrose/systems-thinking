# Survey Actual Events Before Accepting a Claim of Improvement

When told that systems are getting better, do not argue the claim. Collect a starter kit of what has actually happened over the intervening period: cases where a measure produced the opposite of its stated aim, where a safeguard became the hazard, where an authority rejected the advance it existed to recognize.

Include the encouraging items as well, so the survey tests the claim rather than confirming a mood. Then judge the claim against the collected record instead of against the impression the claim creates.
