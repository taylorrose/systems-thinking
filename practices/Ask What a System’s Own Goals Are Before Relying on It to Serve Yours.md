# Ask What a System’s Own Goals Are Before Relying on It to Serve Yours

Before you depend on a system for an outcome, work out what it is pursuing for itself: what it must protect, what its people are rewarded for, and what its budget and routines require. Then ask where your request sits relative to those things.

Where the two run together, expect the system to be effective. Where they compete, plan for your request to wait, and decide in advance whether you can afford that wait or need another route to the outcome.
