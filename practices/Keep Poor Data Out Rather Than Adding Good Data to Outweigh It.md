# Keep Poor Data Out Rather Than Adding Good Data to Outweigh It

When a decision is being fed unreliable material, do not try to dilute it. Identify where the poor data enters, what makes it cheap to produce, and who is obliged to read it, then close or narrow that inlet.

Prefer a small body of material you can vouch for to a large one you cannot, and judge an incoming stream by whether it can be trusted rather than by how much of it there is.
