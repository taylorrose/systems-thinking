# Look for a General Explanation Before Blaming Local Circumstances

When something performs badly, hold the local account in suspense long enough to ask whether the same failure has appeared elsewhere, under different people, places, and pressures. List the cases you know of before naming the cause.

If the failure recurs across circumstances that share nothing but being systems, look for the explanation in how systems behave rather than in the particulars of this one. Keep the special-circumstances account only where the failure is genuinely confined to those circumstances.
