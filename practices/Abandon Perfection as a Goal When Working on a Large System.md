# Abandon Perfection as a Goal When Working on a Large System

When a goal is stated as making a large system perfect, treat that framing as a defect in the plan. Replace it with a limited improvement that can be recognized without claiming that the system has reached a final state.

Keep the paradox visible: in a large system, striving for perfection is itself a serious imperfection. Readiness to cope begins with recognizing that limit rather than pushing it aside.
