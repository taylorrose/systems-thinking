# Pace an Advance Through Catalytic Management Instead of Forcing a Schedule

Before setting a deadline for a discovery, ask whether the target date is derived from the work or imposed on it. If it is imposed, expect the pressure to reshape the reports rather than the results.

Manage catalytically instead: create the conditions in which an advance can occur, keep attention on the underlying question rather than on the schedule, and let the work proceed at the irregular rate it actually moves.
