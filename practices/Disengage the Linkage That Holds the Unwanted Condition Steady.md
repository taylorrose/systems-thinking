# Disengage the Linkage That Holds the Unwanted Condition Steady

When repeated doses of an obvious remedy leave the condition where it was, stop increasing the dose and look instead for what is being held steady. Ask which quantity stays constant no matter how much effort is applied, what reads that quantity, and what it adjusts in response.

Where an identifiable linkage recreates the condition, consider disengaging that linkage rather than continuing to act only on the visible symptom. Confirm the linkage before intervening, because control may be distributed across the system rather than located in one obvious unit.
