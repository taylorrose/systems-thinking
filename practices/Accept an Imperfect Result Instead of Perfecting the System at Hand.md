# Accept an Imperfect Result Instead of Perfecting the System at Hand

Decide first whether the thing is worth doing at all. If it is, do it at whatever quality you can reach now and let the result stand as imperfect, rather than holding it open for further refinement.

Then spend the attention you have recovered on the question refinement crowds out: whether the whole undertaking should be arranged some other way. Treat a persistent urge to polish as a signal to check what has dropped out of view.
