# Ask What Justifies Expecting the Future to Repeat the Past

When a plan depends on continuity, name the specific regularity being relied on and ask what supports it besides its own past record. If the only support is that the pattern has held so far, record the expectation as an assumption rather than a finding.

Then ask what would happen to the plan if that regularity broke, and treat the answer as part of the plan's cost.
