# Ask Which Aspect of a Person a System Actually Registers

When a system acts on someone, identify the record it is actually acting on: the number, licence, file, or account that represents them at the interface. Name which aspect of the person that record captures and which aspects it has no field for.

Then judge the system’s treatment of the person against that narrow record rather than against the person, and expect the gap to widen as the system grows larger.
