# Distinguish What You Hold, What You Want, and What You Need

Write three lists rather than one: the information already in hand, the information you are asking for, and the information the decision cannot be made without. Keep them separate on the page so the differences stay visible.

Compare them in pairs. Mark the gaps between what you hold and what you want, then check whether what you want would actually answer what the decision needs. Correct a misdirected request before spending further effort collecting against it.
