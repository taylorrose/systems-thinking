# Change a Few Things at a Time and Work Out the Unexpected Effects

Choose one thing, or a few things, to change rather than the whole design. Make the change, then look for the shifts it produced elsewhere and work those out before adding the next change.

This is generally easier than attempting to correct everything in one grand design, even though the smaller change will still have unexpected effects.
