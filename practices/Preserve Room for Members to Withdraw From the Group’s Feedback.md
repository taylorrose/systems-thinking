# Preserve Room for Members to Withdraw From the Group’s Feedback

When designing a group, a family arrangement, or a committee, provide for time and space in which a member is not receiving or answering the others’ signals. Identify which channels are always open, and ask whether any of them could be closed periodically without losing information the group actually needs.

Cap the number of participants who must react to each other in real time, and treat a request for distance as maintenance of the group’s ability to settle rather than as withdrawal from it.
