# Test Whether a Condition Is Actually Unalterable Before Accepting It

When something uncomfortable is presented as simply the way things are, treat that as a claim rather than a fact. Ask who established that it cannot be otherwise, what would have to be true for it to be unalterable, and whether anyone has recently tried.

State the condition plainly and out loud, in the terms someone outside the system would use. A condition that survives being named and examined may indeed be fixed; one that has merely never been questioned often does not.
