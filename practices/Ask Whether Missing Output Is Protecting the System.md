# Ask Whether Missing Output Is Protecting the System

When a system has been active for a long time without delivering a result, ask what would happen to it if a result were delivered. Identify who would be able to evaluate that result, against what promise, and what the evaluation could cost the system.

Treat the answer as one possible reading rather than a verdict: shortage of output may reflect difficulty, and it may reflect protection. Look for the absence of any claim of achievement alongside the absence of the achievement itself, since a system shielding itself has reason to avoid both.
