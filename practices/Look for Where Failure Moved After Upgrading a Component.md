# Look for Where Failure Moved After Upgrading a Component

After making one part of a system more reliable, do not record the improvement and stop. Look next at the joints between that part and its neighbours, and at whichever remaining components are hardest to upgrade, since these are where difficulty tends to reappear.

Judge the change by the behaviour of the whole assembly rather than by the failure rate of the part, and ask which functions the assembled system still lacks no matter how good its components become.
