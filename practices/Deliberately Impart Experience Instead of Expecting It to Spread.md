# Deliberately Impart Experience Instead of Expecting It to Spread

Do not expect proximity, succession, or shared membership to carry hard-won experience to the next person or the next system. Name what was learned, decide who needs it, and choose a method that actually imparts it.

Then confirm the transfer rather than assuming it. Ask what the recipient can now do that they could not do before; if nothing has changed, the experience has not been transmitted, whatever was said.
