# Reverse the Machine Metaphor When Reasoning About Complex Things

When you catch yourself explaining something complex as a machine, turn the comparison around and ask what it does as a system instead. In particular, test whether the machine picture has made you assume that knowing the mechanism would make the behavior understandable and predictable.

Apply the reversal to machinery too. Once a mechanism is large and complex enough, reason about it with the expectations you would bring to a system rather than the ones you would bring to a device.
