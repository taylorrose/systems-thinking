# Read a System’s Selection Pressures Before Judging the People It Attracts

Before treating the people a system attracts as merely peculiar, describe what the system actually demands of them. Ask which behaviors it rewards, which it punishes, how narrow the demand is, and how long anyone must sustain it to remain inside.

Then ask what kind of person that demand would leave standing. Traits that look strange from outside are often precise adaptations to a pressure the outsider has not named, so identify the pressure before attributing the trait to the person.
