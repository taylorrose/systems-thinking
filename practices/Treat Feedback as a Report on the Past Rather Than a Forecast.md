# Treat Feedback as a Report on the Past Rather Than a Forecast

When feedback is used to justify a decision, separate what it records from what is being inferred. State the observed history in its own terms, then state the additional assumption needed to carry that history forward.

With the assumption written out, it can be examined, doubted, or hedged against instead of travelling unnoticed inside the data.
