# Ask Which Traits a System Rewards Before Trusting the People It Selects

Look at who a system attracts, retains, and promotes, and name the attributes it is actually rewarding. Then name the attributes its work requires, and compare the two lists.

Where they diverge, expect selected people to be strong in traits the task does not need, and expect some of them to be adapted to living off the system rather than running it. Treat additional screening as another set of positions inside the system, and consider changing what the office itself rewards.
