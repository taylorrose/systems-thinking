# Do Not Dismiss a Failure You Cannot Reproduce

When a reported failure will not appear on demand, do not read the failed attempt as evidence that nothing is wrong. Keep the fault on the list of open faults, record the conditions each report describes, and look for what those occasions have in common, since the reports are the only trace such a failure leaves.

Reserve the conclusion that the phenomenon does not exist for cases supported by something more than the absence of a demonstration.
