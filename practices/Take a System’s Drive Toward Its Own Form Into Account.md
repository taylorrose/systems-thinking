# Take a System’s Drive Toward Its Own Form Into Account

Treat this drive as a force in the situation rather than as an oddity to be argued with. Before intervening, ask what mature form the system appears to be moving toward, and judge the intervention by whether it changes that form or only the path.

Where the form is unaffected, expect trimming and reform to be absorbed, and weigh any plan against the direction the system is already taking.
