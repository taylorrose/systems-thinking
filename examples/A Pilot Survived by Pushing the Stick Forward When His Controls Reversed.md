# A Pilot Survived by Pushing the Stick Forward When His Controls Reversed

During the Battle of Britain, fighter pilots who accelerated past a certain speed in a dive met severe buffeting and then, with dismaying regularity, crashed. One pilot pulled out alive. Hauling back on the stick with all his might was only making things worse, he said, so he shoved the stick forward and the plane responded: his aircraft had gone supersonic, where the reaction to the stick is reversed. He survived by throwing away the rule book and assuming a new set of rules was in force.
