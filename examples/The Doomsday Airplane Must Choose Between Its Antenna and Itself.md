# The Doomsday Airplane Must Choose Between Its Antenna and Itself

The Doomsday Airplane exists to fly above the radioactive debris of a stricken United States and transmit the final order to fire nuclear missiles, and for that it carries a radio antenna over five miles long, weighing more than a ton, which tends to snap like a whip. A crew member can cut the antenna loose with a special pair of shears, saving the airplane but leaving no way to send the order, since there is no spare. The arrangement's purpose is obscure in any case, as the submarine commanders already have orders to fire anyway.
