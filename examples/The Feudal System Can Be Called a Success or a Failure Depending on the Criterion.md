# The Feudal System Can Be Called a Success or a Failure Depending on the Criterion

Was the Feudal System a success or a failure? It can be called a success because it achieved the physical survival of Western Civilization, or a failure because in place of the internationalism of Rome it bequeathed the doubtful legacy of nationalism and a divided Europe. The same history yields either verdict once the criterion is chosen.
