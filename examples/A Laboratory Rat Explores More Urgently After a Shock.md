# A Laboratory Rat Explores More Urgently After a Shock

The common laboratory rat does not stop poking its nose into boxes because some of them are wired with electric shocks; a shock makes it explore more urgently than before. Learning theory, resting on the dogma that creatures quickly learn to avoid painful stimuli, treats this as a scandal. From the rat's side it is straightforward: it matters which boxes are safe and which are not, and the way to find out is to keep probing.
