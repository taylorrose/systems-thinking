# The UN Answered African Famine by Planning a Conference Center

The UN responded to famine in sub-Saharan Africa by planning a magnificent new Conference Center, to accommodate UN personnel meeting to discuss the problem. The response is real and substantial, and it is directed at the discussion rather than at the famine.
