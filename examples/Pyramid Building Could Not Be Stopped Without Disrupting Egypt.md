# Pyramid Building Could Not Be Stopped Without Disrupting Egypt

Egyptian tombs grew slowly for centuries as modest brick mastabas, then suddenly became the pyramids of the pharaohs. Professor Mendelssohn has suggested that by then the tomb-building system had drawn in so much of the social order that halting it would have brought unemployment, hunger, and dislocation to the state. It had become a total system in a runaway sequence, and each turn of the cycle had to be larger than the last.
