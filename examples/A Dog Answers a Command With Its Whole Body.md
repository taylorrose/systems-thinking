# A Dog Answers a Command With Its Whole Body

The command "Here, Spot!" reliably produces not merely an approach by the four legs but accommodative reactions of head, ears, tail, and body. The whole dog hears and responds. Even a disobedient Spot will usually show, on a repetition, unmistakable body language that the command was received.

One may be annoyed at the dog, but the communication process itself raises no question: a unified entity heard, and something whole answered.
