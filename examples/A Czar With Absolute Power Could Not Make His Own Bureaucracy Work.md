# A Czar With Absolute Power Could Not Make His Own Bureaucracy Work

“I never ruled Russia. Ten thousand clerks ruled Russia,” said Czar Alexander on his deathbed. The imperial bureaucracy was designed, if any system ever was, to be a tool in one man’s hands and to carry his slightest whim into effect. It would not work, and Alexander’s absolute authority did not let him make it work.
