# Napoleon Wrote Opera Regulations While His Army and Empire Hung in the Balance

In Moscow, with the fate of his army and empire hanging in the balance, Napoleon spent his days and nights writing detailed regulations for the administration of the Paris Opera. His absorption in administrative detail exemplifies the tunnel vision attributed to striving for perfection.
