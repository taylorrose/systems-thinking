# Air Travelers Land at Orly and Believe They Are in Paris

Jet-age airports sit twenty, forty, or sixty miles from the cities they are named for. Travelers arrive at Chantilly, Romulus, Viracopos, Grapevine, Rosemont, or Orly while believing themselves to be in Washington, Paris, Dallas, Sao Paulo, Detroit, or Chicago — places they have not yet reached, and which may lie an hour further on.
