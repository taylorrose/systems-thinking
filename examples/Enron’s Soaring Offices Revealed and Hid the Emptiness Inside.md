# Enron’s Soaring Offices Revealed and Hid the Emptiness Inside

The single term “Enron” stands for a whole nexus of interlocking corporations. Their enigmatic logos and blandly soaring home office buildings reveal as well as hide the utter emptiness within: the presentation was itself the most visible product.
