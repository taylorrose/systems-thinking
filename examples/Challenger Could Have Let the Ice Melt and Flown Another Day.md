# Challenger Could Have Let the Ice Melt and Flown Another Day

On the morning of January 28, 1986, the space shuttle Challenger sat on the launching pad completely encased in ice, and the authorities, aware that a presidential speech was to mention the successful lift-off, gave the go-ahead. A scholar steeped in the Tao at the top of that command chain might have said instead: today is not a good day to fly; let the schedule go, let the ice melt, and live to fly some other day.
