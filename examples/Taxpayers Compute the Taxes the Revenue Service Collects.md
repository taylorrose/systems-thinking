# Taxpayers Compute the Taxes the Revenue Service Collects

The Internal Revenue Service collects taxes but also requires taxpayers to compute the tax for it. The calculation needed by the collecting system is thus performed by the person being taxed, at a cost in time and temper that the system does not absorb.
