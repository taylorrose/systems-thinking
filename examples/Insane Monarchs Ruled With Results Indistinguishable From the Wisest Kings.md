# Insane Monarchs Ruled With Results Indistinguishable From the Wisest Kings

The list of absolute monarchs who were hopelessly incompetent, even insane, is surprisingly long. They ruled with utter caprice for decades at a stretch, and the net result to their countries was undetectably different from the rule of the wisest kings.
