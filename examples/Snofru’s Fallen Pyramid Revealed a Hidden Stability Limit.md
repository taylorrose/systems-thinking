# Snofru’s Fallen Pyramid Revealed a Hidden Stability Limit

Snofru enlarged the pyramid form that had worked for Zoser. The new pyramid fell, leaving enough rubble to show that it had nearly been completed. Only after the collapse did an outsider infer that Zoser’s structure had already been near its stability limit and that the increase in scale had crossed it.
