# A Strategic Air Command Chief Did Not Know Submarines Travel Under Water

In testimony before Congress in April 1983, an Air Force General serving as Commander-in-Chief of the Strategic Air Command was surprised to learn that submarines actually travel under water. The officer at the top of a nuclear command system lacked a basic fact about one of its arms.
