# Protesting by Refusing to Break the Rules Shows Systemantics Taking Hold

Police in a state in southern Brazil, protesting low pay and poor conditions, stopped making illegal arrests, and arrests fell by ninety percent. Workers of the Solidarity Union in Poland struck for the right to strike, and Newfoundland civil servants struck against restrictions on their own right to strike. Each protest works by giving the system precisely what it claims to want.
