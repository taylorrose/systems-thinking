# Jelly-Bread Falling Jelly Side Down Is the Everyday Form of the Law

Schoolboys the world over know the law as *jelly-bread always falls jelly side down*. The source presents this commonplace wording as the everyday form of Murphy’s more general systems theorem, while rejecting the suggestion that the universe deliberately chooses the worst outcome.
