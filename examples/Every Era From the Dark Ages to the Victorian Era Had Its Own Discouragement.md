# Every Era From the Dark Ages to the Victorian Era Had Its Own Discouragement

A good deal of discouragement prevailed during the Dark Ages, morale was rather low in the Middle Ages as well, the Industrial Revolution brought depressing times, and many felt the Victorian era to be particularly gloomy. Each period had its own occasions for gloom; what carried across all of them was the complaint itself.
