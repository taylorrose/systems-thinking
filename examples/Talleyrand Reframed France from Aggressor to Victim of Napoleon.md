# Talleyrand Reframed France from Aggressor to Victim of Napoleon

At the Congress of Vienna, Talleyrand argued that France was another victim of Napoleonic oppression—and the one that had suffered most—rather than the aggressor to be punished. The Great Powers accepted the new understanding and turned their attention to restoring the legitimate rights of every European power, including France.
