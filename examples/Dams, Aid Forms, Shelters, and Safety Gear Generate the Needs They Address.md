# Dams, Aid Forms, Shelters, and Safety Gear Generate the Needs They Address

Four paradoxical cases share one shape. The Aswan Dam generates increased need for electricity. The government of Haiti requires emergency assistance in order to fill out its requests for emergency assistance. Large space-rocket shelters produce weather hazards of their own. Safety equipment has become a major source of sports injuries.

In each case the remedy is at work on a need or hazard that the remedy itself is helping to create.
