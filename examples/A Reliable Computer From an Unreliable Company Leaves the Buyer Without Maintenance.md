# A Reliable Computer From an Unreliable Company Leaves the Buyer Without Maintenance

A failure rate of one in a million is regularly achieved by computers whose parent companies have a failure rate of one in three. The buyer ends up with an extremely reliable machine and a complete system that lacks a maintenance function.
