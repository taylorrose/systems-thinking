# Employees Watched Identical Bills Go Out and Said Nothing

A large private medical clinic’s new computerized billing system printed a bill for exactly $111.11 for every one of more than 50,000 people who had ever attended. The operator, the office clerk, the programmer, and twelve employees stuffing, sealing, and stamping the envelopes all noticed the striking identity of the numbers, and none of them stopped it. The switchboard jammed, emergency calls could not get through, ten thousand former clients left, and the clinic lost almost a million dollars. The system had hypnotized them.
