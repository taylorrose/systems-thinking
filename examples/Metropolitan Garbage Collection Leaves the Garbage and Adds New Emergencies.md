# Metropolitan Garbage Collection Leaves the Garbage and Adds New Emergencies

Garbage collection in large metropolitan areas is the relativists' standing case. The garbage does not get collected, and on top of that the city must feed and clothe armies of striking workers, immunize its population against diseases of filth, and cope with transport systems that fail because vehicles cannot get around the mountains of refuse.
