# Correcting Pisa and Zilwaukee Threatens to Exceed Their Original Cost Estimates

The same tendency appears where the flaws in a new system are not thought to justify taking it down at all. From the Leaning Tower of Pisa to the Sagging Bridge of Zilwaukee, painful experience has shown that the time and effort spent trying to correct the new problem eventually threaten to exceed the original cost estimate for the whole thing.
