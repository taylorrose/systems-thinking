# Two Brainless Toys Collide Because They Need the Same Resource

Electric turtles are metal shells on three wheels, driven by flashlight batteries, with a switch that sends them back to a small recharging shed when the batteries run low, and nothing resembling a brain. Placed together on a tabletop, two of them whose batteries fade at the same time both head for the shed, then bump and knock each other about at the entrance until one or both is disabled.
