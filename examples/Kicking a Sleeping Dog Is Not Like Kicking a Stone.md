# Kicking a Sleeping Dog Is Not Like Kicking a Stone

A kicked stone moves as the physics of the kick dictates, and science explains the result completely. A kicked sleeping dog reacts to what the kick means to it, and the same physical description says almost nothing about which reaction follows. Most people sense the difference intuitively, even though the second kind of situation is the kind daily life keeps producing.
