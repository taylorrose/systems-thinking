# An Airplane’s Parts All Tend to Fall to Earth

An airplane has been defined as a collection of parts with an inherent tendency to fall to earth, held up only by constant effort and supervision. The system called "airplane" was designed to fly; not one of its parts has that tendency, and it flies, if at all, only as a system.
