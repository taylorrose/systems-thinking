# Egypt, China, and Britain Changed the Structure, Eligibility, or Appeal of High Office

Ancient Egypt used a dual bureaucracy, pairing an honorary office-holder with an actual executive. China selected high officials from those who passed an examination in the ancient Classics. Britain limited high office to aristocrats’ sons, then made the jobs boring, poorly paid, and tedious enough to repel flashy applicants.
