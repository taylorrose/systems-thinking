# An Eighteen-Inch Model Fly Was Received as the Fly’s Actual Size

A lecturer discussed the tsetse fly and illustrated his remarks with an eighteen-inch model of it. Afterwards one of those attending agreed that such flies could pose a problem, but reported that the local variety were much smaller. The model had been offered as a visual aid; it was received as a statement of size.
