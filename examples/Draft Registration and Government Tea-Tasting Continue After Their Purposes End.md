# Draft Registration and Government Tea-Tasting Continue After Their Purposes End

The Selective Service System goes on registering young men for a draft that no longer exists. Government tea-tasters go on tasting tea long after the tasting has ceased to serve any useful purpose.

In each case the need ended and the procedure did not.
