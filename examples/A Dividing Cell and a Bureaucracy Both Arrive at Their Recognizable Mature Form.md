# A Dividing Cell and a Bureaucracy Both Arrive at Their Recognizable Mature Form

A dividing cell seems hell-bent on producing a salamander, a fruit fly, or an oak tree, whatever hardships its environment imposes; short of being stopped altogether, it produces something recognizably salamander-like, fruitfly-like, or oak-like. Bureaucratic systems grind on toward their mature form through frantic hackings and trimmings, revisions and reforms.

Neither the environment in the first case nor the reformers in the second alter the form that finally appears.
