# The Aswan Dam Runs at Capacity to Supply the Need It Created

The Aswan Dam was built at enormous expense to improve the lot of the Egyptian peasant. It caused the Nile to deposit its fertilizing sediment in Lake Nasser, where it is unavailable, so Egyptian fields must be fertilized artificially. Gigantic fertilizer plants were built to meet the new need, and those plants require so much electricity that the dam must operate at capacity merely to supply the demand its own construction created.
