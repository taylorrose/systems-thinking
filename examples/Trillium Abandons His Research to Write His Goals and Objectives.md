# Trillium Abandons His Research to Write His Goals and Objectives

Lionel Trillium, an assistant professor of botany, receives a memo from his department head requiring a statement of Goals and Objectives in triplicate by Monday. His study of Angiosperms is at a critical point, and his real motive for the work — a boyhood curiosity about reproduction — is neither presentable nor consciously formulated.

He cannot answer in one line that he likes botany, and he cannot state his true goals without seeming a putterer, so he fills the document with things he is not interested in. Unable to concentrate while the task hangs over him, he finally gives up his research, stays home three days, and writes it.
