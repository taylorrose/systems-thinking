# An Admiral’s Ship Turns Out to Be a Legal Apprenticeship

Gilbert and Sullivan’s Admiral explains that he sailed a ship so well that he now rules the Queen’s Navee. The “ship” was his legal *apprenticeship*. The title he holds names seamanship; the work that earned it was clerical.
