# Government Efforts and a Large Anticancer Screen Failed to Deliver Their Intended Results

Successive governmental administrations failed to eradicate poverty, reduce crime, or even get the mail delivered on time. Government assistance further accelerated a railroad system's already advanced bankruptcy. In science, a famed research center screened more than twenty thousand substances for anticancer activity at great expense and obtained negative results.

The cases supply the source's unfavorable record of large organized efforts; they do not establish that every system mentioned was originally created for the problem on which it later performed poorly.
