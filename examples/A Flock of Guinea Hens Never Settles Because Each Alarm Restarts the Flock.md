# A Flock of Guinea Hens Never Settles Because Each Alarm Restarts the Flock

A flock of guinea hens trying to sleep on a moonlit night is set off by every slight sound and every turning leaf. One fowl raises the alarm, the alarm rouses the whole flock, and the flock keeps setting itself off until every member is too sleepy to continue; a single bird still alert enough to cluck once starts the sequence again, and nobody, farmer included, gets any sleep.

A large flock of committee-members behaves the same way.
