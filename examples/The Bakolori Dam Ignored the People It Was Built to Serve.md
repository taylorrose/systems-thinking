# The Bakolori Dam Ignored the People It Was Built to Serve

The Bakolori Dam on the Sokoto River, three miles long and 165 feet high, was built to irrigate the dry valley below. The 13,000 villagers living above it were not taken into account; ordered to resettle in the barren uplands they refused, and hundreds died in the resulting riots. The farmers downstream, also unconsidered, insisted on growing millet rather than the wheat the government intended. They use and pay for far less water than planned, revenues fall short of operating costs, the government makes up the annual loss, and no wheat gets grown.
