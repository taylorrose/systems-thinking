# The Telephone Company Charges for What It Once Provided

The Telephone Company was once happy to tell a caller the number of the person he wanted to reach; it now charges the price of a call to speak to the Information Operator. A listing in the Telephone Book has become a privilege paid for, and a subscriber exasperated by dinner-time calls from promoters who bought his number from the company pays again to be removed from the book.
