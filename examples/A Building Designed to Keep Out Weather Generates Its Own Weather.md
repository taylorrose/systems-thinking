# A Building Designed to Keep Out Weather Generates Its Own Weather

The Space Vehicle Preparation Shed at Cape Canaveral, the largest building in the world, generates its own weather, including clouds and rain. Built to shelter space rockets from the elements, it pelts them with storms of its own making.
