# An Automobile Built for Eighty Miles an Hour Protects Its Occupants Only at Twelve

An automobile built to travel at eighty miles per hour carries bumpers designed to withstand impact at two miles per hour. Its brakes can stop the car abruptly while unrestrained occupants continue forward, and a twelve-mile-an-hour impact with the windshield is fatal. Its rear-view mirror, unchanged over three generations of design, leaves a blind spot on each flank large enough to hide a twenty-ton truck.
