# Six Hundred Simultaneous Alarms Did Not Reveal What Had Gone Wrong

The main control panel at Three Mile Island carried more than six hundred indicator dials, alarm signals, klaxons, sirens, and bells. When they all sounded at once, no one in the room could tell what had gone wrong; six hundred alarms clanging in unison did not add up to a comprehensible picture of the problem.
