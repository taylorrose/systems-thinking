# Toxic Plants Were Sited Upwind of a City and in a Poorly Ventilated Valley

A toxic chemical plant was placed just upwind of a city of 900,000 people at Bhopal, India, and another was nestled in a populous valley with limited air circulation at Institute, West Virginia. Both placements left foreseeable routes of failure that imagination in disaster might have led planners to avoid.
