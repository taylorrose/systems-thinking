# Long Selection Can Give an Organism a Third Eye or Sonar

Under long enough exposure to a particular pressure, a plain organism can grow a third eye, learn to fly at night by sonar, or survive drought by burrowing into river mud and holding its breath. Each capacity is extraordinary in general and unremarkable against the one demand that produced it.
