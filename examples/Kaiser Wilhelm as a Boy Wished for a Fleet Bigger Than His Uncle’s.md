# Kaiser Wilhelm as a Boy Wished for a Fleet Bigger Than His Uncle’s

Kaiser Wilhelm of Germany, visiting his uncle the King of England as a boy, saw the English fleet and promptly wished for one of his own—like Uncle Bertie's, only bigger. The example shows the kind of dream from which a system can grow; it does not by itself establish what later system the wish produced.
