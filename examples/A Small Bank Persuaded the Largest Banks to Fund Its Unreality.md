# A Small Bank Persuaded the Largest Banks to Fund Its Unreality

The president of a neighborhood bank in an Oklahoma City shopping center, exposed to the speculative ambience of the oil fields, was led step by step to underwrite ever more ambitious ventures, until in a climax of trust he issued an unsecured line of credit of one hundred million dollars to a single customer. The demonstration of Hireling’s Hypnosis came next: he persuaded several of the largest banks in the country to actually lend the money. The little bank repeated the process with growing confidence and grandiosity before sinking into bankruptcy and taking the banking giants with it.
