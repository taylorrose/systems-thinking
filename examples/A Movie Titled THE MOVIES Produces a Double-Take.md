# A Movie Titled THE MOVIES Produces a Double-Take

Being asked to watch a movie entitled THE MOVIES produces the characteristic hesitation: the thing and its subject matter are the same thing. The feeling returns in force when a television schedule announces a special program on the first twenty-five years of the television guide that lists it.

In both cases nothing is wrong with the individual words. What creates the double-take is that the work names itself as its own topic.
