# A Government That Must Be Taught to Request Aid Shows Failure Reaching Even the Request for Help

The government of Haiti is described as consisting, with one exception, entirely of departments that do not function. Dozens of national and international aid agencies, unable to get assistance accepted, sent emergency representatives to teach government officials how to fill out requests for aid. Failure had extended even to the function of asking for help with failure.
