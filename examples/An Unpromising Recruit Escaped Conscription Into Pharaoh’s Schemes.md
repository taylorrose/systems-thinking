# An Unpromising Recruit Escaped Conscription Into Pharaoh’s Schemes

The Egyptian peasant of five thousand years ago, to whom the author credits the invention of the technique, discovered that the surest way to avoid conscription into Pharaoh’s grandiose schemes was to be a hopelessly unpromising recruit. Nothing had to be refused; the recruiter simply looked elsewhere.
