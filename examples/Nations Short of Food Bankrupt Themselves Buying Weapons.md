# Nations Short of Food Bankrupt Themselves Buying Weapons

Many nations whose greatest need is food to feed their people sell their crops and bankrupt themselves to buy advanced military hardware instead, in order to defend themselves against equally poor neighbors who are doing exactly the same thing.
