# The Maginot Line’s Cannons Could Not Be Turned Around

The French Maginot Line was a chain of underground fortresses with long-range cannon aimed at Germany, the ultimate development of World-War-I-style trench warfare. In 1940 the Germans simply went around it, leaving the cannons pointed the wrong way. Only then did the defenders realize that the cannons could not be turned around: the response had been built into the concrete.
