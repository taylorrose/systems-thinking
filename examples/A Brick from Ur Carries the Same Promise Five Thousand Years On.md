# A Brick from Ur Carries the Same Promise Five Thousand Years On

Legend has it that a lesser-known museum of Middle Eastern archeology holds a baked brick from Ur of the Chaldees on which a scribe, five thousand years ago, incised in cuneiform the message that the final truth is just around the corner. Whatever the legend is worth, the same promise is still being made.
