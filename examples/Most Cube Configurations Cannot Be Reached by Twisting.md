# Most Cube Configurations Cannot Be Reached by Twisting

Rubik's Cube asks no more than that certain coloured faces be aligned on a cube of twenty-six smaller cubes mounted on a central core. Yet given any specified starting configuration, most other configurations are completely impossible, no matter how ingeniously or how long the Cube is twisted.

Millions of devotees twisting cubes do not change which configurations a given start makes available; to reach one of the others, the solver has to start over from a different initial state.
