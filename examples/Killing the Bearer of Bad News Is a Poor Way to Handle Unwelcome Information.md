# Killing the Bearer of Bad News Is a Poor Way to Handle Unwelcome Information

In the infancy of mankind, kings could perhaps afford the luxury of killing the bearer of bad news. The practice removed the messenger while leaving the news it carried in force, and it is now regarded as a poor way of dealing with information that is needed but unwelcome.
