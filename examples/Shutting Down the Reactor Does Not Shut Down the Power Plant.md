# Shutting Down the Reactor Does Not Shut Down the Power Plant

A large reactor declared “shut down” keeps generating heat from secondary radiation — some two hundred million watts in a good-sized plant. Its coolant must keep circulating for several weeks. The reactor system is in its shutdown state while the plant as a whole cannot be stopped without risking a meltdown.
