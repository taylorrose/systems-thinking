# Unarmed Merchant Ships Sank Because Planners Forgot the U-Boats

America’s military planners simply forgot that German submarines could cross the Atlantic, and so failed to arm American merchant ships carrying the supplies of war up and down the East Coast. Four hundred such ships, unable even to fire back at their attackers, were sent to the bottom by U-boats in the first six months of the war.
