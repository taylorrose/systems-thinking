# Choosing Occupation and Location Aptly Keeps the Coast Guard Out of Your Life

No one is absolutely required to join the Coast Guard, and someone who chooses occupation and geographical location aptly need not be involved with the Coast Guard in any immediate way. Not joining the Coast Guard can serve as the paradigm for the whole meta-strategy.
