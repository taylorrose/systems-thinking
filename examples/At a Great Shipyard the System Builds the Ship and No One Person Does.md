# At a Great Shipyard the System Builds the Ship and No One Person Does

A neighbor building a boat alone in his back yard, from his own plans, can fairly be called a boat-builder. Look for a ship-builder at a great shipyard and none can be found: there are welders, carpenters, foremen, and engineers, while the executives who use the title spend their days writing contracts and planning budgets. The ship is built, but the builder is the system.
