# The WASH-740 Risk Estimate Was Restudied Until a Paid Source Obliged

The Atomic Energy Commission authorized the “WASH-740” report to establish in advance that nuclear power plants would not suffer serious accidents. The submitted estimate of risk was too high to be comfortable, so a second report was commissioned — keeping the question in the study phase. The second report returned worse figures than the first, and the decision was then made to suppress it. A third estimate was sought from another source, which, for a fee, obliged.

Each round of study postponed accepting an unwelcome estimate until a source returned a result the commission could use.
