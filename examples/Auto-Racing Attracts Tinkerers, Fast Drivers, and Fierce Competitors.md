# Auto-Racing Attracts Tinkerers, Fast Drivers, and Fierce Competitors

People drawn to auto-racing are likely to be those who enjoy tinkering with high-powered cars, driving fast, and beating other people in fierce competition. The racing system then calls forth exactly those attributes and rewards their most extreme forms.
