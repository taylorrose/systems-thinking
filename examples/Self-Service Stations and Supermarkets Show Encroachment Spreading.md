# Self-Service Stations and Supermarkets Show Encroachment Spreading

Russell Baker drew attention to the self-service gas station, the self-service supermarket, and the self-service salad bar, recalling an era when “you didn’t have to work for the grocery industry; the grocery industry worked for you.” Sketching a future that might include self-service funerals, he observed that industry’s discovery that the buyer could be made to do its work promises to blight the lives of generations to come.
