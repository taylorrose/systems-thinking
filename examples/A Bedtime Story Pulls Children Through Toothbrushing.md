# A Bedtime Story Pulls Children Through Toothbrushing

Children reluctant to brush their teeth before bed are not pressed on toothbrushing at all. The bedtime story simply begins while they are still brushing, and before long they hurry through brushing after supper because that is when the story starts. The story is a second system used, like Jupiter, as a slingshot through the first.
