# Congress Exempts Itself from the Laws It Passes

The Congress of the United States specifically exempts itself from the Civil Rights Act, the Equal Pay Act, the Fair Labor Standards Act, the National Labor Relations Act, the Occupational Safety and Health Act, the Freedom of Information Act, and the Privacy Act. The body that writes the obligations writes itself outside them.
