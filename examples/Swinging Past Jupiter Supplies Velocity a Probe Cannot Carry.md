# Swinging Past Jupiter Supplies Velocity a Probe Cannot Carry

A probe aimed at Pluto would need enormous quantities of fuel to reach the required velocity on its own. A quick swing around Jupiter, using that planet's gravitational field as a slingshot, neatly supplies the missing speed. The probe, as system A, ducks into system B to borrow what its own system could not provide.
