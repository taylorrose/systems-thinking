# Amtrak Trains Stood Still Waiting for the Clock to Catch Up

At 2 AM on Sunday, October 27, 1985, Amtrak trains all over the United States stopped and stood motionless for a solid hour. Bewildered passengers were told that the nation was switching back from Daylight Saving Time to Standard Time, and the trains were waiting for the clock to catch up.
