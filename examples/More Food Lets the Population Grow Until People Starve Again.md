# More Food Lets the Population Grow Until People Starve Again

The Green Revolution used high technology to raise the amount of food grown per acre in Third World countries. The result was that large fractions of the human race could starve at far higher population densities than before: in such settings, providing more food merely allows the population to grow until people are starving again. The regulating linkage in the case is the one between nutritional status and reproductive rate.
