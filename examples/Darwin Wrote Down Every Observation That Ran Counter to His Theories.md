# Darwin Wrote Down Every Observation That Ran Counter to His Theories

Charles Darwin made it a rule to write down immediately any observation or argument that seemed to run counter to his theories. He adopted the rule because he had noticed the tendency in himself to forget inconvenient facts, and the written note was what kept such a fact available for later thought.
