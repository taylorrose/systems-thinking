# Forty Years of Better Software Did Not Catch a Dropped Decimal Point

Forty years after a clinic mailed 50,000 identical bills, in an age of sophisticated computers and equally sophisticated software, the decimal point was dropped from the hotel bills of some 26,000 patrons of three nationwide chains, inflating their charges one hundred fold. By way of compensation the overcharged patrons were offered two free nights at any hotel the three chains operated, at an unspecified cost to the owners.
