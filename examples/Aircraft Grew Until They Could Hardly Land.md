# Aircraft Grew Until They Could Hardly Land

The simple biplane of the nineteen-twenties could land in a plowed field. The 747 needs a mile of reinforced concrete. The Concorde and the Space Shuttle can hardly land at all. Each step toward greater complexity narrowed the conditions under which the aircraft could complete an elementary part of flight.
