# A Nation Called Its Own Deprivation Normalcy Until Roosevelt Named It

Until Franklin Roosevelt remarked that one-third of a nation were ill-housed, ill-fed, and ill-clothed, the nation as a whole — especially the other two-thirds — assumed it was in a state of normalcy. The deprivation was present the whole time; what changed was that it was said out loud.
