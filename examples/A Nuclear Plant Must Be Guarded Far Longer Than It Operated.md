# A Nuclear Plant Must Be Guarded Far Longer Than It Operated

A nuclear power plant may have a useful life of perhaps thirty to fifty years. When that life ends, the plant must be sealed off and guarded for something on the order of five hundred thousand years while its radioactive poisons decay.

The custodial task that begins on the day the plant stops generating power is thus vastly larger than the task the plant was built to perform.
