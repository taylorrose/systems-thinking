# Messier’s List of Objects to Avoid Became the Catalogue of Galaxies

The eighteenth-century comet hunter Charles Messier kept running into fuzzy objects that were not comets and that distracted him from his search. He listed them so that he would not waste time looking at them again. Much later astronomers recognized those same objects as the star clusters and galaxies on which the modern conception of the universe rests, and the famed catalogue of things to be shunned became a catalogue of things to be studied.
