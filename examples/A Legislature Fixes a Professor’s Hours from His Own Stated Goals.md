# A Legislature Fixes a Professor’s Hours from His Own Stated Goals

On the advice of university administrators, state legislators establish by law how many hours a professor of botany must spend on each phase of his professional activities. Trillium can hardly protest: the lawmakers are only formalizing what he himself told them he wanted to do.

The management scheme built to measure his performance and improve his efficiency has, at that point, kicked back and opposed its own proper function.
