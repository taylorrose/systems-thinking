# Talleyrand Could Reframe the Congress of Vienna but Not Napoleon

Napoleon could not grasp that “enemy” was a provisional label. He could not see that there was no need to liquidate the government and armies of Austria, that under other conditions they would make splendid allies, and that they were in any case essential to the stability of a postwar Europe. Talleyrand reframed the entire Congress of Vienna; on this point at least, he could not reframe Napoleon.
