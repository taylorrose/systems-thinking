# The Largest Telescope Cannot Focus Before the Night Ends

The largest telescope in the world, a 230-inch reflector, takes so long to reach thermal equilibrium with the surrounding air that the night is over before it can focus a star image. The dimension that was enlarged to gather more light also governs how long the instrument takes to settle.
