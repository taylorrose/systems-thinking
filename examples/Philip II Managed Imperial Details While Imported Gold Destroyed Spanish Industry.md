# Philip II Managed Imperial Details While Imported Gold Destroyed Spanish Industry

Philip II of Spain gave himself to the details of administering his empire. Engrossed in that work, he failed to notice that the rivers of gold arriving from the New World were suffocating Spanish industry, and so destroying the basis of the power he was administering.
