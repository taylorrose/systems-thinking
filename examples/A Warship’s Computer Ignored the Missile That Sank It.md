# A Warship’s Computer Ignored the Missile That Sank It

The British fighting ship H.M.S. Sheffield was defended by a computer. It was struck and sunk by a missile that the computer had been instructed to disregard as “friendly.” The defending system worked exactly as instructed while the ship was lost.
