# The Sorcerer’s Apprentice Cannot Stop the Powers He Sets in Motion

The Sorcerer’s Apprentice looks through his master’s book of incantations for something that will get his chores done, and commands bucket and mop to work. Only too late does he realize he cannot stop them. The resulting flood is monumental and catastrophic.
