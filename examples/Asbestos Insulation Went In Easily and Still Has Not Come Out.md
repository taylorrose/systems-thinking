# Asbestos Insulation Went In Easily and Still Has Not Come Out

Asbestos products were installed as insulation in homes, schools, and factories as a simple matter. Years later, when their power to cause lung cancer was generally realized, the material had already spread as sheathing along the billions of pipes and ducts of industrial civilization.

Removal requires trained personnel with masks, special vacuum cleaners, and leak-proof bags, and even that proved simple compared with finding out where the material had been put. Insurance rates for removal workers were then set so high that few if any companies were willing to undertake the work, and the asbestos remains unremoved.
