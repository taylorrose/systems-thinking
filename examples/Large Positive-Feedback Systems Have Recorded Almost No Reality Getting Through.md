# Large Positive-Feedback Systems Have Recorded Almost No Reality Getting Through

Coefficients of Fiction above 0.99 have been recorded in large systems employing positive feedback. The examples named are evangelistic religious movements, certain authoritarian governmental systems, and the executive suites of some large corporations.
