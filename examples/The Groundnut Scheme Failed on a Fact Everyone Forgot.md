# The Groundnut Scheme Failed on a Fact Everyone Forgot

To relieve a shortage of cooking oil after World War II, the British embarked on a gigantic plan to grow groundnuts in East Africa. After eight years of hostile soil and adverse climate the plan came to grief, with eighty million dollars lost. It was abandoned when someone finally remembered that peanuts thrive in West Africa, not East Africa.
