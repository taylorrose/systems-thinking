# The UN Spent a Day on Its Own Airfare Instead of Detente and Famine

The United Nations suspended its work on detente, the Middle East, and the drought in North Africa in order to spend an entire day debating whether its employees should go on flying first class.

The business it set aside was the business the organization exists for; the business that displaced it concerned the conditions of the people inside.
