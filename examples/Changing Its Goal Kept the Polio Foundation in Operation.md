# Changing Its Goal Kept the Polio Foundation in Operation

The National Foundation existed to conquer polio, and came perilously close to instability when polio was actually conquered. It adopted a new goal, the conquest of genetic defects, which let the organization go on doing *everything it had previously been doing* with undiminished zeal and efficiency, now in pursuit of an up-to-date aim.
