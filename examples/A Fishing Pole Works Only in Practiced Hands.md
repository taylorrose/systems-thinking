# A Fishing Pole Works Only in Practiced Hands

A fishing pole with line and hook does what it is meant to do, but only for someone who has learned to cast and to set the hook. The butterfly net, skis, and the safety razor belong to the same group, and a boomerang in Western hands shows the other side of it: the design is minimal, so the skill that keeps it working sits with the person operating it.
