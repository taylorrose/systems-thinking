# Temporary Navy Buildings Outlasted Two Wars and the Pentagon

Temporary buildings erected to house Navy personnel in World War I continued to see yeoman service in World War II and in subsequent ventures, and became a permanent, if fading, feature of Constitution Avenue in Washington, D.C. The construction of the Pentagon, a few miles away across the Potomac, never even threatened their tenure. Their temporariness described the intention behind them, not their lifespan.
