# An Anti-Meltdown Device Started the Meltdown It Was Installed to Prevent

At the Fermi Number One fast breeder reactor in Monroe, Michigan, a special anti-meltdown device was installed to cover the remote possibility of a meltdown. The device failed, blocking the flow of molten sodium through the reactor core and initiating a meltdown sequence.

It did not fail while carrying out its protective function. It failed during normal operations, and in doing so brought about the very accident it had been installed to deal with.
