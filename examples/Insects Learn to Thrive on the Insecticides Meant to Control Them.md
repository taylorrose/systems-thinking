# Insects Learn to Thrive on the Insecticides Meant to Control Them

Insecticides were introduced to control disease and improve crop yields. They turn up in the fat pads of Auks in the Antipodes and in the eggs of Ospreys in the Orkneys, causing incalculable ecologic damage, while the insects develop immunity and even learn to thrive on them.
