# A Gold Quotation Can Be Useless Within Thirty Seconds

At certain large banking headquarters in London, Zurich, and New York, the useful half-life of a quotation of the dollar value of gold may be as short as thirty seconds. The figure is the one the trade turns on, and it can be spent before the minute is out.
