# Throwing Out the Rascals Produces a Fresh Set of Rascals

The universal experience of Mankind has been that only a short time elapses after the Rascals get Thrown Out before the new office-holders begin to look like a fresh set of Rascals. The reform replaced the actors and left whatever produced their conduct in place.
