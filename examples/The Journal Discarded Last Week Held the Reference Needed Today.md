# The Journal Discarded Last Week Held the Reference Needed Today

The reference being sought today was in the throwaway journal that went into the wastebasket last Friday, and the cleaners emptied the wastebaskets over the weekend.

Nothing malfunctioned. The need for the reference arrived after each ordinary step that put it beyond reach, and that sequence is the typical scenario rather than an unlucky one.
