# Changing the Actors Cannot Change the Dialogue of a Play

Replacing the actors does not improve a play's dialogue or alter its outcome, and punishing them is equally ineffective. Those matters are controlled at the level of the script, so an intervention aimed at the cast cannot reach them however forcefully it is applied.
