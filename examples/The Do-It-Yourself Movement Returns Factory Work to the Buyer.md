# The Do-It-Yourself Movement Returns Factory Work to the Buyer

The “do it yourself” movement was promoted by the managers of a highly developed system of mass production, and it asks the consumer to assemble the product he has bought on the grounds that it saves work and expense. Yet mass production was established in the first place because such assembly can be done more cheaply and rapidly under factory conditions. The work did not become easier; it moved to the buyer.
