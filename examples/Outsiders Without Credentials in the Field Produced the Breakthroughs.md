# Outsiders Without Credentials in the Field Produced the Breakthroughs

Pasteur took up the cause of silkworm disease having never seen a silkworm, and was not a biologist. The Wright brothers, who built the first successful heavier-than-air machine, were bicycle makers. The gene's molecular structure was revealed by X-ray crystallography, and James Watson, who solved the puzzle, was neither a crystallographer nor a chemist, and had been refused renewal of his research grant for not sticking to the point. Philo Farnsworth conceived television as a fourteen-year-old farm boy plowing furrows on the Great Plains.

In each case the person who solved the problem held no standing in the field the problem belonged to.
