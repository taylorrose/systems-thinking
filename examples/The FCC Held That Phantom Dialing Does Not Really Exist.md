# The FCC Held That Phantom Dialing Does Not Really Exist

In the epidemic of Phantom Dialing, cordless telephones dialled the 911 emergency number spontaneously, on their own initiative. The Federal Communications Commission took the position that the phenomenon, being merely anecdotal, does not really exist.
