# Complexity Consultants Cannot Predict the Future Any Better Than Their Clients

Helen Harte observes that the complexity consultants can no more predict the future than the clients can. Expertise in the accumulated record of a field does not confer forecasting power, which is what one would expect if extrapolation from experience rests on faith rather than on proof.
