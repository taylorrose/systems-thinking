# Bomber Control Systems Overpowered One Another in Flight

W. Evert Welch, who worked on control systems for high-altitude bombers during World War II, reported that the engine temperature control had a habit of overpowering the automatic pilot, that the cabin airflow control seldom agreed with the engine control, and that the cabin temperature control produced side effects of its own. The unexpected interactions were discovered after the fact while the bombers were flying.
