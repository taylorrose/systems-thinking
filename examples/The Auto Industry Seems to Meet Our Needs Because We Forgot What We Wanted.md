# The Auto Industry Seems to Meet Our Needs Because We Forgot What We Wanted

The auto industry supplies millions of new cars a year and tailors them to changing tastes in style and performance, which looks like an exception. What was originally wanted was a means of going from one place to another that would be cheap, easy, convenient, safe, and fast — a requirement we have almost completely forgotten rather than met.
