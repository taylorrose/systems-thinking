# Cheops Preserved His Pyramid While the Burden Shifted to Egypt

Cheops kept his larger pyramid from collapsing by improving its construction and distributing its stresses. The structure survived, but the effort required to build pyramids on that scale imposed its stress on the Egyptian state instead.
