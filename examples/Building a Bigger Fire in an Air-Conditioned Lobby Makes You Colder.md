# Building a Bigger Fire in an Air-Conditioned Lobby Makes You Colder

A Fuegian, used to carrying fire for warmth, feels chilly in the lobby of an air-conditioned hotel and lights his fire there. The fire trips the lobby thermostat, the air-conditioning goes to high, and he grows colder. He adds wood, the cooling rises further, icicles form on the chandelier, and he breaks up the furniture for a real bonfire. From where he stands, fires make you cold in big cities, and bigger fires make you colder.
