# A Report Issued Before a Meeting Described How the Meeting Went

On March 6, 1985, Canadian national television news reported that Paul Nitze had met with Prime Minister Mulroney and had afterwards expressed satisfaction with their conversation. The American State Department had released that report before the meeting actually took place, so the meeting and its tone were already on the record while both were still ahead.
