# Bismarck Accepted Social Benefits After Civil Servants Were Reframed as a Standing Army

Bismarck opposed anything resembling Socialism, but accepted sickness benefits and pensions after a million loyal civil servants were described as a standing army in disguise. The changed label made the same proposal intelligible inside a frame he already accepted.
