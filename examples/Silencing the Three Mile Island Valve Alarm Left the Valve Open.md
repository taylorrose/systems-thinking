# Silencing the Three Mile Island Valve Alarm Left the Valve Open

At Three Mile Island, an alarm signalled that a valve was stuck in the “open” position. The alarm was wired to the control panel so that pressing the “close” button silenced it — whether or not the valve closed. The valve remained open; the panel went quiet.

The signal answered the button, not the valve.
