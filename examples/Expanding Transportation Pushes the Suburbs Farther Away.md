# Expanding Transportation Pushes the Suburbs Farther Away

As cities grow larger, commuters must travel farther and faster to reach the pleasant suburbs where they prefer to live, so more cars and trains are needed to carry them. The transportation system must expand, and as it expands the city grows with it, which increases the need for still more and faster cars and trains traveling even farther to reach suburbs that keep receding.
