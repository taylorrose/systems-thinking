# Calling Someone a Crook Creates Opposing Categories

Calling someone a “crook” creates two mutually dependent categories: crook and non-crook. Once that distinction is accepted, argument shifts toward deciding who belongs on each side instead of questioning whether the distinction is the most useful way to understand the situation.
