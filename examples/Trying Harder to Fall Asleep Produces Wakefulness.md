# Trying Harder to Fall Asleep Produces Wakefulness

A parent who urges a sleepless child to try harder to get to sleep may not realize that trying harder makes getting to sleep more difficult rather than easier. The instruction creates a small system called trying to get to sleep, and the operation of that system — the trying — produces wakefulness.

The child can comply fully with the instruction and be further from sleep for having done so.
