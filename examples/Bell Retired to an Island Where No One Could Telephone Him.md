# Bell Retired to an Island Where No One Could Telephone Him

At a less exalted level, even so altruistic a person as Alexander Graham Bell, having invented the telephone, retired rather promptly to a lonely Atlantic island where no one could ring him up. The inventor arranged his own exemption from the system he had given everyone else.
