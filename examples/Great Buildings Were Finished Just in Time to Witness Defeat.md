# Great Buildings Were Finished Just in Time to Witness Defeat

Parkinson’s succession of cases runs the same way each time. The Palace of Versailles was completed in time to receive the news of defeat at Blenheim; the League of Nations Building was readied in time for Mussolini’s invasion of Abyssinia; the Pentagon was followed by Korea and Vietnam. The massive new home of the FBI was completed just in time to witness the disgrace of both the Agency and its first Chief. The New Senate Office Building, the most costly office building in history, cannot be called complete, yet has already witnessed the Abscam scandals, Koreagate, and the Pageboy furore.
