# Schools and Funding Agencies Claim the Achievements They Only Enabled

The public school system is held responsible for the literary work of Faulkner, Hemingway, and Arthur Miller because it taught them to write. In the same manner the NIH is treated as responsible for a generation of biomedical advances — and has claimed the credit — because it funded the research.
