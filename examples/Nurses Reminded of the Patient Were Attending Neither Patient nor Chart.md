# Nurses Reminded of the Patient Were Attending Neither Patient nor Chart

A hand-lettered sign in a medium-sized hospital, taped above the console that let the nurses record breathing and pulse without walking down the hall, read “THE CHART IS NOT THE PATIENT.” When the author looked for the nurses, they were neither at the bedsides nor at the charts. They were in the hospital auditorium, taking a course in Interdisciplinary Function.
