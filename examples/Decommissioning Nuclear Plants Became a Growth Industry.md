# Decommissioning Nuclear Plants Became a Growth Industry

Taking down old and broken-down nuclear power plants, including ones that never quite managed to be activated at all, is itself a growth industry, guaranteed to thrive into the twenty-first century and probably beyond. The plants that were never switched on show the point most sharply: the dismantling work exists whether or not the system ever performed.
