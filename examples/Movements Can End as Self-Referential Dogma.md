# Movements Can End as Self-Referential Dogma

Religious and social movements often begin as dynamic new systems for better living. This appears to be a particular pitfall for them: over time such a movement may end up an elaborately self-referential thicket of other-worldly dogma, its internal doctrine having replaced the living conditions it set out to improve.
