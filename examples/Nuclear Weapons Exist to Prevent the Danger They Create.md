# Nuclear Weapons Exist to Prevent the Danger They Create

The most poignant instance lies in nuclear weaponry. The danger of nuclear destruction exists because — and only because — the nations of the world have armed themselves with nuclear weapons in order to prevent nuclear destruction.

The threat and the protection against it are the same stockpile.
