# Cutting Hospital Beds Shifted Greater Costs onto Other Agencies

When the British National Health Service cut hospital beds to save money, support services and welfare agencies were swamped by sick people whose need for them had risen precisely because their medical problems went untreated. The added budgetary demands on those agencies far outran the original saving on beds.
