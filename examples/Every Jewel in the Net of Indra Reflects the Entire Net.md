# Every Jewel in the Net of Indra Reflects the Entire Net

In Hindu legend the Net of Indra is infinitely large, with a precious jewel at every intersection of its meshes. The net is an infinity of jewels, and each jewel reflects the whole net, including the infinity of other jewels. The Hindu sages are taken here to have been expressing a principle of systems-thinking rather than describing an object.
