# A Healthy Man Won Two Hundred Hospital Admissions by Appearing Ill

A case history in the British Medical Journal records Stewart McIlroy, who over thirty-four years achieved at least 207 admissions to 68 or more British hospitals, living in them for roughly ten years in total. Despite flamboyant symptoms he appears to have been essentially healthy, and quick enough on his feet to leave when discovery threatened. His one rare and specialized ability was to look ill enough to be admitted.
