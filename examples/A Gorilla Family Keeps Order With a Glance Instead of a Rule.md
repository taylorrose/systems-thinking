# A Gorilla Family Keeps Order With a Glance Instead of a Rule

Jane Goodall observed gorilla families napping after meals. The youngsters wake early, grow bored, and start clambering about the nest. The father eventually raises himself on one elbow and fixes the offender with a long stare, and the juvenile settles down again, at least for a few minutes.

Nothing in the arrangement is scheduled or enforced. One unhurried gesture from one member restores order, and the family keeps working.
