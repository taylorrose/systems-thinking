# Three Independent Sets of Boilers Fail Simultaneously

The Queen Elizabeth II, the greatest ocean liner ever built, carries three separate sets of boilers for safety, reliability, and speed. On one cruise, in fine weather and a calm sea, all three sets failed at the same time. Separating the boilers did not separate their fates.
