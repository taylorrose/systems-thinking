# Charlemagne’s Fair Division of His Empire Produced a Thousand Years of Strife

Wishing to be fair to his three sons, Charlemagne divided his empire among them. The division gave rise to France, Germany, and Alsace-Lorraine, and to more than a thousand years of strife. The decision of one of the greatest and wisest kings proved disastrous, running opposite to the case of the incompetent ruler whose reign changed nothing.
