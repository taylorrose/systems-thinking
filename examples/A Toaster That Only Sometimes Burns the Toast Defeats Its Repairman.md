# A Toaster That Only Sometimes Burns the Toast Defeats Its Repairman

A toaster that sometimes burns the toast, or an air-conditioner that fails to cool on the second day of a ten-day heat wave, typically leaves the repair person unable to find anything wrong. After the second or third call for help, the repair person can no longer be found either.
