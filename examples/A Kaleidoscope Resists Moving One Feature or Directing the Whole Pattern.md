# A Kaleidoscope Resists Moving One Feature or Directing the Whole Pattern

Trying to manipulate a kaleidoscope so that its green triangle moves from left to right or from top to bottom gives an intuitive understanding of both limits: one feature cannot be changed alone, yet the whole display cannot be directed at will.
