# Readiness for the Previous Challenge Leaves the System Exposed to the New One

A system organized thoroughly around a familiar response may be unable to redirect that preparation when a new challenge arrives. Its apparent readiness then leaves it exposed because its strength is aimed at the situation it previously faced.
