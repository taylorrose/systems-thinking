# Instability Diverts a System’s Effort From Output to Survival

Once an unstable system is launched, its pitching and bucking threatens to carry its riders into catastrophe. Attention that was meant for useful output is drawn instead into the struggle for mere survival, so the instability costs the system its work as well as its safety.
