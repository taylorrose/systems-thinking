# A Complaint Repeated in Every Era Counts as a Permanent Condition

When the same observation returns in period after period, it stops being evidence about any single period. It comes gradually to be recognized as an ongoing fact of life, an inseparable component of the human condition, rather than a local fault still awaiting its repair.
