# The Observer Effect Grows as Probes Become More Sophisticated

The observer effect did not recede as investigation improved. Poorly publicized and largely ignored at first, it persisted and encroached, and as probes became more sophisticated it expanded until it filled the observable universe.

Greater sophistication in the probe does not make the observer effect go away. In the source's formulation, the effect instead expands to fill the observable universe.
