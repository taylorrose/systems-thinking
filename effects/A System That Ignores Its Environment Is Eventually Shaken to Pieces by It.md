# A System That Ignores Its Environment Is Eventually Shaken to Pieces by It

The environment a system refuses to register does not stop acting on it. Contact continues, unanticipated and therefore violent, and repeated violent contact eventually shakes the system apart. The instability is already under way from the moment the feedback begins to be ignored, not from the moment the damage becomes visible.
