# Responses That Once Saved the System Make Things Worse After the Rules Change

The repertoire that formerly preserved the system does not merely become useless when the ground rules shift; the responses that once were life-saving now aggravate the situation they were meant to correct. The system is left worse off for continuing to do the thing that formerly saved it.
