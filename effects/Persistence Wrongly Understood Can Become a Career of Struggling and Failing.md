# Persistence Wrongly Understood Can Become a Career of Struggling and Failing

The precept is two-edged. Read as a warrant for repeating an approach that has already failed, it can supply the justification for a lifetime of Struggling-and-Failing, in which renewed effort is spent on the same unyielding problem and counted as virtue.
