# The Unexpected Behavior of an Ultimate Model Is Often a New Way of Failing

When the largest and most complex model behaves in a way no one anticipated, that behavior often takes the form of an unsuspected way of failing. The unexpected result is therefore not merely weaker performance along a known dimension; the way the system fails may itself be new.
