# Commonsense Remedies Can Generate Escalating Counterproblems

More food can support population growth until starvation returns; more transportation can enlarge the city and lengthen commutes; new antibiotics are followed by resistant microbes; and elaborate shelters sustain species that no longer survive in the wild. In these feedback situations, supplying the commonsense remedy can reproduce or enlarge the problem that calls for another round.
