# The Promise of Perfection Remains a Delusion However Elegantly Reformulated

Final truth may be placed around the corner, perfection after the current revision, or perfection on the day after the final deadline. Changing the formulation does not rescue the idea: however elegantly it is stated, the source calls it a delusion.
