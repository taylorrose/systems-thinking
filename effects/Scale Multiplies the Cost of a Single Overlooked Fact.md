# Scale Multiplies the Cost of a Single Overlooked Fact

In a colossal undertaking, one ordinary fact left unchecked can carry a cost proportional to the whole enterprise. The oversight itself is unremarkable — a geography, a capability, a population — but the scale of the system converts it into hundreds of lost ships, eighty million lost dollars, or a permanent public liability.
