# A Condition Held to Be Inevitable Attracts No Attempt to Change It

While a condition counts as simply the way things are, no one looks for its causes, proposes remedies, or measures how bad it is. Effort goes elsewhere, and the condition can persist for as long as the belief in its necessity does.
