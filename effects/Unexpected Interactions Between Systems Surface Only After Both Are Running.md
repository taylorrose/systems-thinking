# Unexpected Interactions Between Systems Surface Only After Both Are Running

Behavior of this kind belongs to the systems in combination rather than to either one alone, so it is not present in either separate design and cannot be found by inspecting them. It becomes visible only once both systems are actually operating, which is to say after the fact.
