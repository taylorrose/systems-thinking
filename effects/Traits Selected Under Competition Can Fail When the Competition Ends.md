# Traits Selected Under Competition Can Fail When the Competition Ends

Paradoxically, when the trial is over and the competitive conditions are relaxed, the successful contenders may show a sudden loss of control or of adaptability. The cessation of the struggle may leave the survivor disoriented, without the customary frame of reference, in a way that resembles an hallucinatory response.
