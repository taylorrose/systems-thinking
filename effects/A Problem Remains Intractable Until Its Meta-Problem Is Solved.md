# A Problem Remains Intractable Until Its Meta-Problem Is Solved

While a problem is attributed to the wrong level, work directed at it accumulates without producing a solution. The appearance of unsolvability persists, and it persists for as long as the question of where the problem actually sits remains unanswered.
