# The Measuring Role Can Outgrow the Work It Was Created to Serve

Judging the work can become a career in its own right. Someone whose own productive line has dried up may find new standing in assessing the strengths and especially the weaknesses of colleagues, and the wider contacts that role brings can confirm rather than correct that judgement.

The measuring apparatus is thereby reinforced by the same episode that damaged the work, and those who administer it gain standing over those they were originally meant to serve.
