# A System Built or Wound Too Tightly Seizes Up, Peters Out, or Flies Apart

A system built too tight, or wound up too tightly, has three ways out and all of them end its functioning: it seizes up, it peters out, or it flies apart. Looser systems, by contrast, last longer and function better.
