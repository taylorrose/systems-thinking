# A Frame Shift Can Change the Same Finding From Nuisance to Discovery

When the frame of reference applied to an anomaly changes, what the anomaly counts as changes with it, while the anomaly itself stays as it was. Being able to make that switch therefore confers major advantages in dealing with systems and their bugs: findings that one frame files as interference become, in another, the most informative thing available.
