# Perfecting the System at Hand Leaves No Attention for Better Alternatives

Energy spent refining the present arrangement is energy unavailable for considering other, possibly better, ways of doing the whole thing. Whatever is being perfected therefore stays in place by default, not because it was found to be the best available approach.
