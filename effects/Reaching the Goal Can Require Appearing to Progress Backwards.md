# Reaching the Goal Can Require Appearing to Progress Backwards

Even from a starting configuration for which ultimate success is possible, the route to the goal can require repeatedly undoing almost completely what has been achieved so far. The Cubologist who has aligned part of the puzzle must take that alignment apart again to go further.

Over large stretches of such a course, therefore, visible progress runs the wrong way, and measures of accumulated gain report loss while the work is going correctly.
