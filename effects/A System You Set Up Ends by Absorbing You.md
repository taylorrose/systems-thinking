# A System You Set Up Ends by Absorbing You

A system that has been set up grows and encroaches, behaves in strange ways, breaks down unexpectedly, gets in the way, and opposes its own proper function. Being inside it distorts your perspective, so you grow anxious and push on it to make it work.

Encroachment is complete when you come to believe that the grudging product it delivers is what you wanted all along. At that point you have been absorbed, and you have become a systems-person.
