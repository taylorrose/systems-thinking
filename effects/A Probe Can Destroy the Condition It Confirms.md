# A Probe Can Destroy the Condition It Confirms

A probe can succeed as a measurement and still leave the system without the property it just established. The finding then describes a state that existed up to the moment of testing rather than the state the system is in once the test is complete.

Success of the probe is therefore no guarantee that the confirmed condition survives it.
