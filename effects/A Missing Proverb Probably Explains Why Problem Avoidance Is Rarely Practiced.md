# A Missing Proverb Probably Explains Why Problem Avoidance Is Rarely Practiced

There is no sufficiently short, pithy aphorism in current circulation to convey this idea compellingly, which makes the strategy hard to teach. As usual, the lack of a well-honed proverb reveals a blind spot in the popular culture, and that blind spot is probably why so few of us actively practice problem avoidance.
