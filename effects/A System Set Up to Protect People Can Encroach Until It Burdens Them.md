# A System Set Up to Protect People Can Encroach Until It Burdens Them

Encroachment can carry a system past the purpose it was created for. Under the ancien regime in France, peasants bore grinding taxation to support an untaxed aristocracy; a system of government originating to protect the people had encroached until it became their worst oppressor.
