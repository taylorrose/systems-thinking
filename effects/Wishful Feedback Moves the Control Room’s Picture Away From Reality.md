# Wishful Feedback Moves the Control Room’s Picture Away From Reality

A system whose controls register the operator’s wishes rather than actual conditions is likely to spend most of its time inside the Potemkin Village Effect. Because its responses are addressed to a state of affairs that does not obtain, events run further and further ahead of them, and the model of the world assembled in the control room resembles the outside world less and less as the gap widens. Such a system can hallucinate its way toward terminal instability.
