# Anyone Who Operates a Large System Meets Resistance From It

Le Chatelier’s Principle may look like a curiosity when confined to physical and chemical processes. Applied to large systems, however, its relevance is recognizable to people who have experienced their operation: the system sets up opposition to what is being done through it.
