# Internal Communication Can Crowd Out the Work It Was Meant to Coordinate

As a system sinks further into a backlog of unfinished tasks, the business of exchanging messages can expand exponentially. At the limit, a non-functioning system is completely occupied with its own internal communication processes, and the work those messages were supposed to coordinate no longer receives any of its capacity.
