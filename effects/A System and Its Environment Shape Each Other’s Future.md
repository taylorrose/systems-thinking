# A System and Its Environment Shape Each Other’s Future

Because the output of each becomes the input of the other, a system and its environment move together rather than one adapting to a fixed setting. Each response alters what the other has to respond to, and over successive rounds both are changed. Neither party's later condition can be attributed to itself alone; what each becomes is partly the work of the other.
