# Information Useless to Everyone Is Treated as Raw Material for History

Information with no conceivable present usefulness to anyone is not thereby destroyed. It is commonly treated as legitimate raw material for History, where it acquires an indefinite second life.

In that transmuted form it feeds readers who savor crises safely past and who store away how those battles were won or lost, in case a similar adventure reaches them. The chain of systems that can find a use for information does not obviously end.
