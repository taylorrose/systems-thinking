# Troubleshooting Complex Systems Cannot Be Reduced to Checking Lists

Without the problem contributed by the solution, troubleshooting a complex system might eventually have become a matter of working through the entries in a technical manual. Because that problem is present, the fault need not correspond to any item a manual can enumerate in advance.

Diagnosis therefore stays open-ended: the list can be completed while the malfunction remains.
