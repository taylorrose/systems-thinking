# A System’s Impact Returns Even When the Other System Does Not Retaliate

Declining to respond in kind postpones the reckoning rather than cancelling it. The impact of one system on another is eventually transmitted onward, perhaps through further systems, and arrives back at its origin; the system kicks back regardless.
