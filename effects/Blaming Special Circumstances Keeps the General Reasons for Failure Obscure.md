# Blaming Special Circumstances Keeps the General Reasons for Failure Obscure

While every instance is charged to its own circumstances, the reasons why things so often go wrong, do not work, or work in ways never anticipated remain obscure. The evidence stays scattered across cases, and nothing in the accumulation of local explanations brings the shared reasons into view.
