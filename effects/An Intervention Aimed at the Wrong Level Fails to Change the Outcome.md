# An Intervention Aimed at the Wrong Level Fails to Change the Outcome

Effort applied at one level does not reach what is governed at another, so an intervention placed on the wrong rung leaves the outcome as it was however forcefully it is pressed. Stepping up and down the ladder of logical levels appears to be something the human mind does only with reluctance and difficulty, which is why failures to distinguish one level from another recur rather than being corrected by experience.
