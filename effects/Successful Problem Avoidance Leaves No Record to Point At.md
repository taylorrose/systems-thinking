# Successful Problem Avoidance Leaves No Record to Point At

Asking for a list of successful applications of the technique is nugatory: it amounts to asking for a list of difficult situations that never actually materialized. The successes leave no wreckage, no rescue, and no story, so the method accumulates no visible track record while less effective strategies accumulate plenty.
