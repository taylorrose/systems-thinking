# A Dreamed Nightmare Becomes a Nightmare for Others

The image a system is built to realize can as easily be a nightmare as a hope. A system founded on such an image tends strongly to be, or to become, a nightmare for the people it touches. The quality of the original wish thus reappears in what those outside the system have to live with.
