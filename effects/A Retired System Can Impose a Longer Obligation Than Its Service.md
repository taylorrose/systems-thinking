# A Retired System Can Impose a Longer Obligation Than Its Service

The commitment a system leaves behind is not bounded by the period in which it worked. Containment, custody, and disposal can outlast the working life by orders of magnitude, and they fall due precisely when the system has ceased to return any benefit.

An accounting that stops at the end of useful service can therefore understate the total demand a system places on those who adopt it.
