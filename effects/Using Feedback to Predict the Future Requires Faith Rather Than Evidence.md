# Using Feedback to Predict the Future Requires Faith Rather Than Evidence

Because the data available for prediction necessarily come from the past, anyone forecasting from feedback is relying on an unproven assumption that the past continues to hold. The confidence attached to such a forecast rests on that assumption rather than on the data themselves, and its validity remains doubtful.
