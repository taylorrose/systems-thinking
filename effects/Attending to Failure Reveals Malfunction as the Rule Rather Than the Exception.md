# Attending to Failure Reveals Malfunction as the Rule Rather Than the Exception

Once failures are deliberately noticed instead of forgotten, the scale of the discrepancy between the world as advertised and the world as it actually functions becomes apparent. What then appears is the reverse of the assumed picture: malfunction turns out to be the ordinary case and flawless operation the exceptional one.
