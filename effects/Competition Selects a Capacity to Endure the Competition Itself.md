# Competition Selects a Capacity to Endure the Competition Itself

A further consequence of extreme competitive conditions is a peculiar quality of endurance, a special ability to survive under precisely those conditions. The capacity selected is fitness for the contest, which is not the same as fitness for whatever the contest was supposed to award.
