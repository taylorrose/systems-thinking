# Reading Failure as Insufficient Effort Leads to Escalation

Once a system's own functioning is producing the failure, the stage is set for the systems-delusion that past failures came from not trying hard enough, or, worse, that they are somebody's fault. Neither reading questions the loop.

Such a delusion leads directly to escalation, which in theory can progress as far as meltdown and the explosive release of poisonous vapors.
