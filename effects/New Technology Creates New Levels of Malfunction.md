# New Technology Creates New Levels of Malfunction

A new technology does not remove the old opportunities for error; it adds opportunities at levels of complexity and grandiosity that were not previously reachable. The microprocessor, like the multinational corporation, keeps supplying fresh instances of unexpected misbehavior, and intelligent machines can be expected to produce forms of malfunction the human imagination is unable to anticipate in advance.
