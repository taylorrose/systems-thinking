# Hireling’s Hypnosis Spreads to People Outside the System That Produced It

The trance is quite capable of jumping large distances to infect innocent bystanders. Outsiders with no stake in the system’s internal life can be brought to act on its unreality — and can be ruined by it when the system that generated it fails.
