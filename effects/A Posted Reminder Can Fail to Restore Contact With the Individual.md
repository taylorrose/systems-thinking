# A Posted Reminder Can Fail to Restore Contact With the Individual

A slogan with humanistic implications can turn out to be misleading about the place it hangs. The attention it asks for may have already moved past both the person and the record into the system’s own internal activity, leaving the reminder as evidence of an intention rather than of a practice.
