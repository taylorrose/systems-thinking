# Naming a Problem Invites Experts to Elaborate Its Complexity

Once a problem is recognized as a “Problem,” it undergoes a subtle metamorphosis. Experts in the problem area proceed to elaborate its complexity, and they design complex systems to attack what they have elaborated.
