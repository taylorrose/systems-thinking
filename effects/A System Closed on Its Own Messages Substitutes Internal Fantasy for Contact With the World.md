# A System Closed on Its Own Messages Substitutes Internal Fantasy for Contact With the World

Once a system's traffic is entirely internal, what circulates inside it stops being tested against anything outside. Like a catatonic patient, the system becomes preoccupied with a wholly internal life: the stock of information about the world tends to decline while invented content about it tends to grow.
