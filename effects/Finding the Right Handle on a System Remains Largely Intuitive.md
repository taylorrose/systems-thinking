# Finding the Right Handle on a System Remains Largely Intuitive

When a system's form does not disclose its uses, it becomes hard to know which end to grasp for which task. Skill in recognizing and grasping the appropriate handles therefore stays largely intuitive rather than following from any stated procedure.
