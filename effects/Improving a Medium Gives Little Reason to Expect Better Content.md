# Improving a Medium Gives Little Reason to Expect Better Content

Refining the apparatus through which something passes does not by itself change what passes through it. There is accordingly little reason to suppose that tinkering with brain metabolism will improve the quality of people’s thoughts, feelings, and behavior.
