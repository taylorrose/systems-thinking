# A Quick Fix Can Surface Distant Effects Under Field Conditions

Treating a bug as purely local and patching it on the spot most likely leads to the discovery of major distant effects, whether of the bug itself or of the procedure used to correct it. Those effects tend to appear later, under operating conditions in the field rather than at the bench where the repair was made.
