# A Built-In Capability Stays Hidden Until an Environment Elicits It

A capability present in the structure can remain invisible for as long as nothing calls it out. It comes to light through the accident of surroundings that happen to be capable of eliciting that response, so its discovery depends on experience rather than on inspection of the design.
