# Setting Up a System Redirects Your Effort Into Its Care and Feeding

Once a system is set up, your time and effort are likely to be consumed by the care and feeding of the system itself rather than by the job it was meant to do. The effort that the system promised to save is spent instead on keeping the system running, so the harder the system is to maintain, the less of you is left for the work.
