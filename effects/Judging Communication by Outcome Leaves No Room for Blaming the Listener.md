# Judging Communication by Outcome Leaves No Room for Blaming the Listener

Once meaning is read off the outcome, the sender appears as a participant in what occurred rather than as a party wronged by it. The passive luxury of complaining about other people or external circumstances is no longer available, and what takes its place is stark clarity about one’s own relations with other people and other systems.
