# A System’s Size Becomes a Poor Indicator of the Work It Does

When enlargement proceeds at its own rate rather than in response to demand, size stops carrying information about output. A larger department need not be handling more work than a smaller one, and steady growth need not be evidence that anything more is being accomplished.
