# A Problem No One Has Recognized Cannot Be Referred for Help

Help of any kind — advice, repair, escalation, rescue — has to be asked for, and asking requires a problem to point at. While the problem remains unrecognized, the ordinary channels for getting assistance stay closed, however available that assistance may be.
