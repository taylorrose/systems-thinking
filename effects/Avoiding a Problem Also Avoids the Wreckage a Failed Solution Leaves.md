# Avoiding a Problem Also Avoids the Wreckage a Failed Solution Leaves

Many of the world’s biggest problems consist of dealing with the wreckage of old failed solutions cluttering the landscape. A problem that is never engaged produces no such solution and therefore adds nothing further to that accumulation.
