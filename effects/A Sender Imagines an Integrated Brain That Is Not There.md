# A Sender Imagines an Integrated Brain That Is Not There

Having sent a message, the sender pictures an integrated organizational brain that will consider it and respond in an integrated way, and in all likelihood pictures wrongly. The expectation is a delusion produced by mental habit: childhood experience of communicating with people and pets trains everyone to address unified entities, and that habit is carried unexamined into dealings with systems.
