# Larger Interstices Make a System Less Hostile to the People Inside It

Where a system is loose, the gaps between its parts are wide enough for people to move, improvise, and live at their own pace. A tighter system leaves narrower gaps, so more of ordinary life must be conducted on the system's terms, and it tends to be somewhat harsher on the human beings who inhabit it.
