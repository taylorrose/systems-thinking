# A Job Title Becomes Weaker Evidence of What Its Holder Does

As the resemblance between a named function and the work performed under it declines, the title becomes weaker evidence of how its holder actually spends time, effort, or resources. In larger and more complex systems, observing the work is therefore more informative than relying on the position’s name.
