# Experience with a Smaller System Does Not Establish How the Larger One Will Behave

Once the dimensions of a smaller system are expanded, familiarity with the original does not establish the behavior of the enlarged one. Expectations learned from the smaller version may therefore fail when carried across the change in scale.
