# Unwelcome Findings Can Be Suppressed Rather Than Acted On

When study exists to postpone commitment, a finding that forces commitment becomes an obstacle rather than an answer. Such a finding can be set aside and the question reopened elsewhere, so the system's stated knowledge drifts toward what it can live with rather than toward what its inquiries actually found.
