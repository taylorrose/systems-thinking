# Trying Harder Can Produce More of the Unwanted Behavior on Both Sides

When a system is locked in a self-referential loop, greater effort feeds the loop rather than clearing it. The primatologist who escalates the effort to observe may merely escalate the primate's level of observing the observer, so the one piece of behavior nobody wanted becomes the behavior that grows.

At that point the system is in a positive feedback trap, and the increase appears on both sides at once: each party's added effort supplies the other party's reason to do more of the same.
