# Intermittent Bugs Wake Up During Critical Sequences

Bugs of this type take up residence in the interstices of sophisticated and complex projects, where they lie dormant. They rouse themselves only at rare intervals, and those intervals tend to be critical sequences such as the maiden voyage of the Space Shuttle or the launching of a multimillion-dollar communications satellite.
