# Systems Also Attract People Who Thrive at the System’s Expense

Alongside those whose qualities make for success within it, a system draws individuals with specialized traits fitted to parasitizing it. As a barnacle attaches to a whale, they attach themselves to the system and take a free ride for as long as it survives.
